# cooldown15-01: decision cooldown 30 s -> 15 s (setting canary), 2026-11-01

Control session's decision (owner away), made under serving D (owner 11-01) and the owner's setting fast path
(10-16). This is a PROGRESS entry, queued after fleetbuild-01 and **ahead of fewerdec-01**. Both change the
decision cadence, so fewerdec builds on this result: its `after` is cooldown15-01, and v39 autorebases its patch.

## The change

`bots/src/config.mjs`: `decisionCooldownMs = cappedCooldownMs(LLM_DECISION_COOLDOWN_MS, LLM_DECISION_COOLDOWN_CAP_MS || 15000)`.
Every fleet env file sets `LLM_DECISION_COOLDOWN_MS=30000`, so the canary runs at 15 s. The cap is a ceiling,
not a floor, and a cap that is not a positive number is ignored.

- Variant `45ed1239` on fleet base `dfb3ff13`, branch `cooldown15-on-dfb3ff13`. The patch source is set.
- Rollback: deploy the base sha, or set `LLM_DECISION_COOLDOWN_CAP_MS=30000` in the env. That is one number
  and needs no code change.
- Test: `bots/test/cooldown-cap.test.mjs`. It imports the config in a fresh process for each env case, and
  includes a wiring mutant.
- Review: Codex, blockers only, one round: no blockers. Tier 1 by `protected.py`. It runs as a canary by
  choice, because it changes the cadence fleet-wide and adds load on the 5080.

**Why the change is in code and not in CANARY_ENV.** The model-env path is capped at `draw_k` 1..2
(`canary-sched.py validate_entry` and `drawrec.sh`). An env-only canary therefore cannot take 4 pools (20 bots).
A model-env KEEP also never changes the fleet tip, so fewerdec could not build on it. The 09-11 20 s canary
used the same in-code clamp (`bb1af53`).

## Baseline and capacity (11-01 07:30–12:30Z, 80 bots all on the 5080)

Data: `llm-<bot>.jsonl` and `skill-<bot>.jsonl` on .31, 1,129,017 rows walked, 80 bots. Script:
`cooldown15-01/cdcap.py.txt`.

- **Load:** 29,166 model calls, which is 97.2/min (1.11–1.36/min/bot by pool). Latency p50 1.92 s, p95 3.46 s,
  p99 4.27 s. Mean in flight 3.2. The 10-min bins ranged 86–102/min with p95 3.24–3.92 s.
- **Latency by the number of calls already in flight at start:**

  | in flight | p50 | p95 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | 1.05 s | 2.98 s |
  | 2 | 1.65 s | 3.20 s |
  | 4 | 2.05 s | 3.38 s |
  | 6 | 2.32 s | 3.62 s |
  | 8 or more | 2.77 s | 4.13 s |

  The mean grows by about 0.19 s for each extra call in flight.
- **Vetoes:** repeat_loop is 19.3% of model calls.
- **Idle cooldown wait:** 42% of inter-decision time when runner skills count as busy, 28% when every skill
  row counts. This matches the 09-19 correction. The fewerdec note's "67%" overstates it.
- **Projected calls per bot** (same decision sequence, timer model of `#scheduleNext`, 10 s busy re-check):
  x1.28 at 15 s, x1.17 at 20 s, x1.41 at 10 s. About 37% of gaps are skill-bound or non-idle, and those do not
  shrink. The 09-11 20 s run measured 1.41x LLM rows against x1.17 projected, so the plan assumes
  **1.3–1.6x per canary bot**.
- **Canary (20 bots):** +7 to +15% fleet calls (97 -> ~104–112/min). Projected 5080 p95 +2 to +5%. The task's
  "+15–25%" assumed calls double per bot.
- **Fleet-wide after promotion:** ~126–155/min, mean in flight ~5–7, projected p95 ~3.8–4.3 s, inside 5 s.
  At 10 s it would be ~140–200/min, which is past that.

## Read lines (+90/+180)

The generic read covers the tripper, the death gate (two-death floor) and crash loops. The own lines below are
read from the two log files.

- **Arms:** canary pools against control, the non-canary 5080 bots. The qwen3.5 A/B pools (isolated-a,
  isolated-c, placebo-c) are excluded while they are on the Blackwell, until 11-02 13:13Z.
- **Windows:** pre is the 180 min before `declared_at`; post starts at +15 min.

| # | line | rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | model calls/min/bot, ratio-DiD (mechanism) | at least +15% (expected +30–60%), else the cap is not live -> REVERT |
| 2 | control-pool 5080 p95 (capacity) | post at most 1.20x its own pre and at most 5 s, else REVERT |
| 3 | repeat_loop share of canary model calls | up by at most 5 pp, control-adjusted, else REVERT |
| 4 | items/bot-h and value/bot-h, ratio-DiD | reported and named in the close |
| 5 | in-skill share of bot-time | reported; expected up |

Under fun mode, INCONCLUSIVE with no harm counts as a KEEP.

**After promotion** (automatic, about 10 min after a KEEP), one 60-min tripwire runs on the fleet:

- **Pass:** 5080 p95 stays at or below 5 s, and aborted or timed-out decisions do not rise.
- **Fail:** put `LLM_DECISION_COOLDOWN_CAP_MS=30000` back in the env files and restart staggered, or redeploy
  the previous sha.

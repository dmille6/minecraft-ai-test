## 3. Why none of them is ever cleared — two defects

1. **The recount never tries.** `reconcileCobble` (skills.mjs) fails a lid-blocked chest with `lid blocked` before it walks.
   This is every `lid_blocked` in the table above.
2. **The deposit's and the withdraw's lid clear cannot succeed without a pickaxe in hand.** Both call `bot.dig(lid)`
   without equipping, raced against a 10,000 ms timeout. A bare hand breaks cobblestone in exactly 10,000 ms
   (prismarine-block digTime 1.21.8: hardness 2 x 5; wooden pickaxe 1,500, stone 750; not on ground 50,000). Fleet,
   8 days: 5 deposit lid digs, 4 `aborted`, 1 `confirmed` (placebo-c's, which ended its lid_blocked rows). The withdraw's
   `container_blocked ... has cobblestone on its lid and it would not break` rows (hive-c-Delta x4 today, isolated-a-Alpha
   x4 on 10-15) are bots that went to withdraw a pickaxe because they lacked one.

## 4. Design (chosen: (a), the lid clear, in the recount + the tool/budget fix for all three sites)

**r2 (after both design reviews r1, APPROVE WITH CHANGES from Claude and Codex, no safety disagreement):** the breakable
set is cobblestone and cobbled deepslate ONLY (both: material is not permission; stone_bricks is the town platform); the
server rule must also say the block blocks; the well's cells are refused (well.mjs wellBreakCost); a waterlogged neighbour
is water; both halves of a double chest are judged; the hand is `toolFor`'s pick EQUIPPED AND AWAITED, else `emptyHand`
(never a fire-and-forget unequip, never a spent pickaxe swung); digTime read on the ground; a look, then a FINAL check on a
fresh read with no await before `dig(block, 'ignore')`; outcome `dug` (the bot's own world model -- the window after it
is the proof; a dug lid whose open times out is `lid_dug_open_timed_out`); ONLY evidence about the lid (`refused`, `failed`,
`unconfirmed`) marks the deposit's container unavailable -- our clock is `outOfTime`, and `slow`/`reach`/`hand` are a new
`lid_not_now` that marks nothing; a pre-walk refusal in the recount writes its own `_lid_clear outcome=refused` row;
exposure is frozen before the deploy. The text below is r1 where it differs.

**One pure rule, `bots/src/lidrule.mjs`:**
- `chestLidBlocked(above)` moves here unchanged (re-exported by skills.mjs).
- `lidClearRefusal({ lid, above, sides })` -> null or a reason. Refuses: lid unknown; a lid block that is not bot scaffold
  (`LID_BREAKABLE`: stone-family, dirt-family, planks, logs, leaves, glass -- never a container, station, door, bed or any
  block entity); an unknown cell above or beside; liquid above or beside (the existing lidSafeToBreak rule); a falling block
  above.
- `lidDigBudgetMs(digMs)` -> digMs + 2,500, or null past 15,000 (a bare hand in water / off the ground).
- `serverLidBlocks` documents the server's rule (census and tests only; the bot keeps chestLidBlocked).

**One actuator, `clearChestLid(bot, chestBlock, { signal, msLeft, by })` in skills.mjs**, used by all three sites:
lid not blocked -> nothing; `lidClearRefusal` -> refuse; `bestTool` (the cheapest tool that harvests, the existing
iron-retention policy) equipped, none -> bare hand; `bot.digTime(lid)` after the equip -> budget, null -> refuse
`lid_slow`; not enough of the caller's time -> refuse (our clock); `bot.canDigBlock` false -> refuse (reach); dig inside
`withDigScope({ purpose: 'lid', by })` (the `_dig_town` instrument then tags it purpose=lid -- a purpose only this build
writes); read back 300 ms later: still blocked -> `lid_unconfirmed`. One `_lid_clear` row per attempt (status, chest, lid
block, tool, digMs, budget, outcome, by). The open that follows is the server's proof: a window opens only if the lid is
really clear, and the count is written only from a real window (cobblecap's fail-closed rule is untouched).

**Sites:**
- **recount** (`reconcileCobble`, used by count_town and the town deposit's count step): before the walk, a blocked lid that
  `lidClearRefusal` refuses fails `lid blocked` exactly as today (no walk). A clearable one is walked to; after the arrival
  check the lid is re-read and cleared; a failed clear fails `lid not cleared` (this bot's backoff only, never a recount
  miss, never the container memory).
- **deposit** and **withdraw**: their `bot.dig(lid)` is replaced by `clearChestLid`; outcomes and fail classes unchanged
  (`container_blocked`, the container marked `unavailable` for 30 min on a refusal or a failed clear).

**Compositions checked:**
- Town-ground zone (townzone-01): the lid clear is not a discretionary target dig (gather/mine/tunnel); the deposit has
  always dug lids without the zone. Cobblestone on a chest lid is classed `ground` by `townDigClass` (TERRAIN), so it gets a
  `_dig_town` row -- now with `purpose=lid`, not `other`.
- cobblecap / stock targets: unchanged; the only new thing is that a container that used to stay unknown gets counted.
- The well, the composter, the plinth (plinth-01 not deployed): none sits on a lid (well sites keep
  WELL_CONTAINER_DISTANCE from containers; a composter is not a full cube). `LID_BREAKABLE` would refuse anything else.
- A bot standing on the lid block: breaking it drops the bot 1 block onto the chest; harmless.
- A dig the server silently refuses (ghost air, 09-29): the open then times out -> `open timed out` exactly as today.
- Re-blocking: if a bot pillars on the chest again, the next recount clears it again (bounded by the recount's own
  cooldown/backoff and RECONCILE_MAX = 3 containers per order).

**Not chosen:**
- (b) retiring or bounding an unopenable container: no census case needs it (18 of 18 clearable). It would also be a change
  to cobblecap's fail-closed rule. If a refused lid ever appears, the read reports it (`_lid_clear status=no_effect
  outcome=refused`) and (b) becomes a separate change.
- (c) stopping the placement: one dated cause (a model `build pillar` on the home cell) and 16 undated; the placers
  (pathfinder scaffolding, entombment escapes, builds) are many and each is its own change. (a) works whatever placed it.
  Named as a follow-up: `build` should refuse a structure whose cells include a container's lid cell.

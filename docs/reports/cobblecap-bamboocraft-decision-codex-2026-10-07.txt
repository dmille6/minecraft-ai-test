The built rule implements the second half (banking only frees slots, keeps 64/bot) but NO per-town ceiling. A hard cap (stop banking cobble when the town's chests hold >= 256) needs a per-town stock count (chest contents are known only when a bot opens them; a shadow "mayor" records stock but is not authoritative). Without a cap, towns accumulate cobble (the bank was 124,894 items in/48 out per day before a one-time admin clear on 10-04 freed 233 full containers; chests filling is what triggered that clear). Options: (a) no cap now, (b) build the cap now as part of stonecap, (c) defer the cap until chests are measured as filling again.

2. BAMBOO vs BLOCKED CRAFT: today when a craft order is blocked by a full bag, the craft decision wins before the bamboo fold that would free a slot (so the craft just fails). Proposal: make "fold bamboo to free a slot" one of the craft's own room-making remedies (CLAUDE.md rule: a refusal must name a remedy the bot can perform from where it is; deterministic beats advisory). Risk: coupling two canaries (bamboo-01 not yet kept; a grid-fix canary precedes it); scope creep; stick caps. Options: (a) add now inside bamboo-01 before it is canaried, (b) separate follow-up canary after bamboo-01 is kept, (c) don't.

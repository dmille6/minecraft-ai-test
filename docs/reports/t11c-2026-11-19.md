# t11c-01show: a grade cut and its cap are one unit; an unreached grade cell goes last (2026-11-19)

The branch is a714d15e9, plus vegclear (6974fc702..e00b9df1d), plus the commits listed below.

## Changes

- **(a) A cut and its cap are one unit.**
  - `gradetidy.mjs pairCuts` marks the lowest stone-exposing grade cut of a column with its cap: the open grade topsoil cell under it.
  - `buildrun.mjs`'s clear pass digs a marked cut only when `pairHeldWhy` passes. The checks, in order:
    - the cap's block is one the foreign rule digs (`cap_kept`);
    - the bag spares the cap's block (`no_material`);
    - the time left covers `PAIR_MS` (25 s; `no_time`);
    - the placer's own stand test finds a stand (`stepZ`, run on the world with the cut and the cap's block gone; `no_stand`);
    - the cap segment's lease is free (`lease`).
  - The lease is taken right before the cut, after every skip, and released after the pair. `layPair` then digs the cap's block and lays the earth, re-checking the lease and the block first.
- **(b) An unreached grade cell goes last.** `buildrun.mjs` reports the cells whose foreign dig ended `unreached`. The tidy visit keeps them for an hour (`noteUnreached`). `gradeFirst` puts their grade segments, and every grade segment that depends on them, behind the other grade segments.

   **Evidence:** D:245–249, D:318; `bots/src/skills.mjs:480`, `:650`, `:761–772`, `:2602`, `:14672–14684`; `bots/src/runner.mjs:256–263`.

   There is another concrete path: **home → surface → surfaceClimb**, whose catch at 14683 rethrows the raw error when the signal is aborted. That can reach the runner as `skill_error`. Replace the diagnosis and planned change with this path, and reproduce it in the composed test. The historical rows support investigating it, but do not prove every row came from it. Also, M:571 and M:576 show **225 GoalChanged messages and one Timeout**, not 226 GoalChanged errors.

2. **P1 — Lease renewal, expiry, and stranded recovery contradict one another.**  
   **Evidence:** D:350–351, D:386–403, D:420–421; `CLAUDE.md:150–167`.  
   The lease renews per leg, yet TTL expiry is the promised terminal escape from stranded mode. Repeated return attempts could keep renewing it indefinitely. Conversely, expiry removes deterministic return ownership and permits another departure while the first bot remains stranded. “One expedition per town” then limits lease holders, not bots lost outside town. Revocation similarly removes the lease that supposedly gives return legs priority.  
   Specify separate **renewable ownership**, **an absolute trip deadline**, and **persistent return/recovery state**. Define whether unresolved stranding blocks another departure. Test revocation, expiry, restart, repeated failed legs, and two competing bots together. “Wait, then let normal orders take over” does not establish a selected recovery chain.

3. **P2 — H2 is real, but its proposed guard needs an explicit liveness contract.**  
   **Evidence:** D:237–239, D:316–333; `bots/src/watchdog.mjs:79–81`, `:291–320`, `:383–411`; `bots/src/runner.mjs:225–242`.  
   Re-entry is real: the interval does not await `check()`, and the old samples remain while escalation awaits `runner.run()`. However:
   - Stranding sampling currently happens inside `judgeStranding()` through `#strandingVerdict()`, **not** through `#sample()`. Merely leaving the latter running does not preserve stranding history.
   - A hung skill body has an existing runner hard-stop: normally **180 seconds plus 30 seconds grace**, so “judged ≤180 seconds later” is not guaranteed.
   - The pending flag needs unconditional release, including errors, and the document must define arbitration between stranding rescue and stagnation rescue.

   Add controlled-clock tests for a nonsettling skill reaching hard-stop, a rejected escalation, and continued stranding-history accumulation. This is a fixable specification gap, not evidence that an in-flight guard inherently creates an endless hang.

4. **P2 — The “surface-only” return guarantee is stronger than the design supports.**  
   **Evidence:** D:374–387, D:397–405, D:415; `bots/src/skills.mjs:497–499`, `:552–555`, `:676–698`; `docs/reports/ziggurat-mineshaft-2026-10-15.md:783`.  
   `GoalNearXZ` does not constrain elevation, and existing goto can retry with digging or larger drops. Standing on a breadcrumb proves a cell existed; it does not prove the reverse traversal is possible. The proposed six-block-drop scene directly exercises that asymmetry.  
   Define enforceable outbound elevation/escape constraints and reserve accounting, or explicitly describe v1 as bounded-risk exploration rather than guaranteed surface travel. Test an irreversible drop with exhausted scaffolding and tools. The shaft design already distinguishes a verified return route from inventory possession.

5. **P2 — Outpost deposit composition is incomplete; changing the height predicate is insufficient.**  
   **Evidence:** D:392–393, D:452–480; `bots/src/chestfull.mjs:69–78`; `bots/src/skills.mjs:5450–5454`, `:7004–7010`; `bots/src/towndeposit.mjs:39–43`, `:84–86`; `bots/src/admission.mjs:397–412`.  
   `depositTargetOk` really is a home-relative vertical filter. But `farFromBank`, admission’s storage probe, deposit target selection, and `town_deposit` do not all use interchangeable rules. `town_deposit` remains confined to **16 horizontal / 12 vertical blocks of configured home**. The measured “at town” population instead uses 48 horizontal blocks (M:414).  
   Specify the deterministic outpost deposit entry point, anchor ownership, item policy, and matching admission/remedy predicates. Test a different-elevation outpost, full containers, and a blocked return. The shed fallback starts at 34 slots, while expeditions turn back at 30; the intervening refusal chain needs an explicit action. Also correct the blanket claim that `deposit` always refuses far away: admission is conditional, and the skill itself can call home (`skills.mjs:2723`).

6. **P2 — E0 cannot immediately certify its proposed 14-day negative.**  
   **Evidence:** D:290–308; `scripts/lib/estelemetry.py:79–93`.  
   On October 19, a 14-day lookback includes October 7, which the telemetry library explicitly refuses because of duplicate documents. Excluding that day creates unknown coverage, not evidence of no visit. Five-minute position samples also miss intervening visits and do not establish surface reachability.  
   Define coverage validity, backfill/reconciliation or a warm-up period, and distinguish **“not observed visited”** from **“unvisited/reachable.”** A healthy heartbeat alone cannot validate these negatives.

7. **P2 — Measurement labels and projected outcomes need tightening.**  
   **Evidence:** D:16, D:49–62, D:177–179, D:257, D:335–336; M:23–45, M:143–146, M:213–218, M:462, M:487–494; `bots/src/logger.mjs:59`.  
   - The 526/762 interruption-duration statistic is **all pools**, whereas 538 aborted homes is **shared towns**. Label that scope explicitly.
   - The home extraction ends at **15:24:20**, not 15:00; the fixed-window count is 2,931 versus the extraction’s 2,939.
   - A previous row’s end snapshot is a **start-position proxy**, not an exact home-start snapshot. Skill timestamps record starts; a preceding skill’s snapshot can describe a later time.
   - The 806-success counterfactual adds nearby failures selected with `distance <= 5`, while the proposed predicate is horizontal `<=3` plus a vertical check. Replay the actual predicate across **both failures and existing successes**, since adding vertical validation can remove current successes.
   - Expected home exposure is approximately 14 rows at +180 and 28 at +360, below the stated minimum of 30. Specify the unexposed close/extension rule at +360.

8. **P3 — Correct the arithmetic wording and preserve inherited queue details.**

   - Six eligible bot-snapshots / 1,439 = 0.42%; six eligible town-hours / 288 = **2.08%**. Keep those denominators separate.
   - A 400-block rail line costs 150 iron for rails, **160 with two carts**: 81.5% / 87.0% of the stated 184 median stock, rather than the whole stock.
   - Explicitly retain **smeltfuel before pavilion**, shaftuse’s first-free-slot treatment, and the conditional status of storehouse/projectdemand.

## Rules the plan runs under (so "promote if it keeps" is measurable)
- KEEP means the registered rule v12: readability floors met (>= 15 canary bot-hours), no rung-linked canary death,
  every guard within its bound (one-sided gather/explore at 30%; climb and livelock rows <= +100%; ladders p90
  <= 32 blocks; still-stuck exhausted bots <= trapped-at-deploy + 1), deposit skill_error share <= control's, and the
  change's own registered line not worse. Anything else tears down; a linked death reverts at once.
- Missed-slot rule: if the draw finds fewer than two eligible pools at +-40% by 22:00 UTC, the canary waits for the
  morning draw; the band is never widened past 40% and exclusions are never shortened for a slot.
- Rollback ownership: the fleet reverts to the previous main (kept as main-pre-<date>) by the same deploy path; the
  ledger row names the reason; the owner is woken.
- Every read reports the exposure (bot-hours) and the death count beside the rate; a rate without its count is not
  a result.

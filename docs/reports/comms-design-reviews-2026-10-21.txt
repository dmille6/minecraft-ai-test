Findings refer to `docs/reports/comms-design-2026-10-21.md` unless otherwise named.

- **P1 — §§1, 4.3, 5.3: chatgate does not close the stated trust boundary.** `comms.mjs` accepts arbitrary hazard `what` text, a sender-asserted pool and a fleet-shaped username; `worldfacts.promptLines()` interpolates that text into the cognitive prompt. It also ingests `unreachable`, contradicting the proposed central rule. `OWNER_CHAT_NAMES` protects only `commands.mjs`. **Fix:** specify authenticated/scoped peer identities, enumerated fact fields and safe prompt rendering; explicitly retain or retire legacy reachability sharing. Test both handlers and the resulting prompt, including forged peers. The current parser also has no `death` or `confirmed_hazard` message support, so the proposed cross-host mirror requires a new protocol.

- **P1 — §§4.4, 4.6–4.7: dropping updates can preserve false actionable state.** An empty-furnace observation followed within 60 seconds by a load can lose its invalidation to the per-subject cap. The consumer still sees *live empty*, so “falls back to its own reads” does not apply. Suppressing identical observations also prevents durable freshness refreshes, unlike existing stores. **Fix:** make invalidations, releases and safety events reliable; coalesce ordinary observations with bounded durable refreshes. Test empty→load, claim→release, repeated observation and restart. Define parity only after specifying bootstrap, refresh and reconciliation.

- **P1 — §4.2: disproof and confirmation do not consistently mean server-observed world state.** Two deaths need not share a cause; personal entombment/drowning hits are not observations of a persistent obstacle. No lava/water within two blocks cannot disprove a fall, suffocation or unknown-cause hazard. A negative survey near a sighting also does not establish that its recorded cell—or its 24-block merged resource region—is gone. **Fix:** separate immutable death events from inferred hazards; require cause-specific evidence and complete loaded-cell coverage for disproof. Preserve unknown when coverage is incomplete. Generic water must not become forbidden terrain.

- **P1 — §4.6: the keep-out remedies are not composed.** “Walk N blocks … then mine” assumes a surface route and relies on the next model choice. Mining can refuse underground. A random explore bearing can itself fail the existing lava/death-site checks; `lineHitsDeathSite()` only permits particular outward paths, not arbitrary escape through overlapping discs. **Fix:** require deterministic, executable fallback selection and Paper chains covering underground all-bearing refusal, overlapping discs, no safe random bearing, and climb-cell filtering versus entombment/escape. Printing a remedy is insufficient.

- **P1 — §§0, 2.4; `tools/opps*.py.txt`, `sight.py.txt`: the central empirical conclusion is overstated.** `logger.mjs` timestamps skills at start, while `runner.mjs` captures positions at completion. Gather grouping therefore uses outcome-dependent locations and can count a peer failure that had not completed before the current attempt. Distance is horizontal only; the 400-row cutoff is unvalidated. Sightings use 48-block horizontal outcome matching, permanently prioritize any earlier harvest over later fruitless visits, and include episodes without a full remaining outcome window. **Fix:** use observation-availability times, pre-action/target coordinates, vertical matching, complete follow-up and sensitivity analyses stratified by block, location and bot. Until then, label the rule a conservative design choice—not proof that peer failure predicts nothing or that a deployable veto would block exactly 1,721 successes.

- **P1 — §§1, 2.3, 4.7; `tools/es_deaths.py.txt`: reconstructed sites do not match production sites.** `reportDeath()` adds weight to merged sites; repeated deaths can keep them alive beyond 36 hours. Production also retains merged centers and caps/prunes sites. The script instead creates a 36-hour disc around every death. Thus “on file,” the claimed parity and the opportunity estimate are not established. The controls additionally disagree: files report **33 deaths**, ES reports **31**, and the ES comparison lacks an extract-end cutoff. **Fix:** replay production merge/decay semantics with sufficient initial state, reconcile identical windows, and distinguish merely known sites from sites already satisfying the proposed confirmation quorum. Replace “100 times as deadly” and “1.7 deaths/day opportunity” with qualified descriptive estimates until corrected.

- **P1 — §§4.5, 8–9: owner-direct deployment is misclassified.** CLAUDE.md reserves tier 1 for a constant. A command authorization change and a new journal/fold are not constants; observe-only does not make them tier 1. **Fix:** use reviewed tier-2/3 registrations with their required exposure and gates, or explicitly request an exception to the tier policy. Combining ledger-01 with townindex-01 requires a newly reviewed combined variant and registration.

- **P1 — §§6.1, 6.4: the contract protocol does not yet enforce “peers inform.”** An award naming `deliver_to` effectively creates a courier job unless an existing eligible planner independently selects it. A `gen` field alone does not fence concurrent awards, stale deliveries or offers spending the same surplus across requests. “The guard’s own remedy applies” does not prove cancellation returns to baseline. **Fix:** define legal transitions and authorized writers, atomic award fencing, quantity commitments, fresh courier eligibility and authoritative destination validation. Add composed cancellation/starvation tests and attributable delivery proofs. Specify bilateral exchange separately if “trades” remain in scope.

- **P2 — §§2.1–2.2; `tools/opps.py.txt`, `opps2.py.txt`: repeat and duplication headlines exceed the measurements.** Container resets are town-wide and include `_furnace_session`, omitted from the prose definition; a session is not necessarily a refill. Furnace resets similarly treat every non-drain session as a load. Repeat classification stops at the latest same-bot negative, missing earlier peer observations. Overlap tests inspect only 50 prior runs and measure concurrency, not duplicate output. **Fix:** use witnessed mutations keyed to container/item, report nearest-predecessor versus any-peer repeats separately, and use a complete interval sweep. Relabel 2.55 bot-hours as a proxy, and replace “duplicate jobs: none” with measured overlap rates. Smelt overlap is a useful positive control, but does not prove leases caused the zeros.

- **P2 — §§0, 2.4–2.6; `tools/extras.py.txt`, `opps.py.txt`: controls need matched exposure and timestamps.** Needs and proximity tests backdate end-of-skill bags/positions; uniform controls weight towns equally while shortage events do not. Inventory possession is not eligible surplus or deliverability. Also, 15,042 heading rows versus 15,028 explore runs does not prove every run—or all 100.4 hours—used a sighting. **Fix:** timestamp snapshots when captured, match controls by town/bot/time and observation coverage, evaluate actual offer eligibility, and join heading events to individual runs. Report missing coverage and unresolved episodes explicitly.

- **P2 — §§2.7, 5.6, 8: chat gates can pass for the wrong reason.** `stop`, `resume` and `help` do not create chat-triggered skill rows, so zero such rows does not mean zero commands. The proposed unauthorized `!goto` test omits coordinates; existing argument validation already prevents movement. **Fix:** test valid unauthorized commands and assert runner cancellation/resumption as well as skill dispatch; use authorized equivalents as positive controls. Require minimum `_say` exposure and failing fixtures for T1–T5. Decide whether spectator `mike` should have fleet-wide stop/resume authority.

- **P2 — §§6.3, 8: cooperation gates diverge from the referenced plans.** The overseer’s corrected primary includes **all randomized town-hours over a fixed horizon**, including completion and stalls—not only project-open hours. Its S1 is Paper testing, S2 shadow, and S3 the canary. The model bench uses within-world 3/2 assignment and a gathered-output primary; joint project output cannot simply inherit that estimator. **Fix:** preserve S0–S3 prerequisites and the fixed denominator; preregister a cooperation-specific estimator, interference treatment, exposure and power calculation.

- **P2 — §5.1: the rewording validator cannot guarantee “no new facts.”** Allowed names, items and numbers can be rearranged into false ownership, negated hazards or invented completion claims. **Fix:** keep templates authoritative; permit only enumerated paraphrases preserving actor, relation, polarity and quantities, or explicitly label unrestricted rewording as unverified. Apply command/peer rejection to final LLM output too.

- **P2 — §§4.3, 4.7: board reuse needs an explicit adaptation.** `board.quorumState()` recognizes `worked` versus failure reports; it does not implement this table’s per-type rules. Its shelf-life clock starts at posting, unlike the proposed observation-based freshness. **Fix:** specify a typed adoption/expiry adapter, ordering of disproof versus renewed evidence, and behavioral fixtures for every type. Keep the cobble journal’s generation/retry protocol intact.

- **P3 — §5.2: make the BlueMap integration concrete.** Official CLI source supports config-derived markers and a `--markers` update path; an arbitrary web-root `markers.json` is not a demonstrated fallback. **Fix:** verify the pinned showcase jar, document the supported update command and served endpoint, and test escaped labels plus visible marker removal. [BlueMap CLI source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueMap-Minecraft/BlueMap/master/implementations/cli/src/main/java/de/bluecolored/bluemap/cli/BlueMapCLI.java)

1. Project Sid / PIANO (Altera). https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00114 , https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114
   ~10 concurrent modules; a Cognitive Controller broadcasts one decision to talk and action (fixes say-one-thing,
   do-another). Measures: role specialisation from LLM-read social goals, memes and religion from speech keywords; tax
   compliance (deposits into community chests) is the one action measure. 25-49 agents per test, 50-agent societies,
   500 agents in 6 towns. [summary] ~17 items/agent in 30 min.
2. Mindcraft. https://github.com/kolbytn/mindcraft ; src/agent/conversation.js ; settings.js
   Pairwise conversations (!startConversation / !endConversation) through a central server; busy/idle gating
   (200 ms / 5 s delays, LLM decides whether to answer while busy); timeouts start 30 s and double; max_messages 15.
   MineCollab: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17950 -- performance "drops dramatically from upto 90% down to less than 30%"
   going from 2 to 5 agents (cooking, crafting); withholding the recipe/plan so agents must explain it in words cost
   "over 15%" for every model; "The primary bottleneck ... is efficient natural language communication."
3. VillagerAgent. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05720 -- task DAG + agent controller assigning ready subtasks + a state
   manager; no peer chat. Farm-to-table vs AgentVerse: failure 18.2% vs 44.4%; tokens 1.79 vs 10.3; completion 73.75%
   (2 agents) vs 29.75%. [summary] peaks at 2-4 agents.
4. MineLand. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19267 -- distance-limited chat; a message can interrupt running code; 32 agents
   with visuals, ~128 without. [summary] chat overhead cut the per-agent work saving to ~20%.
5. Generative Agents (Park et al. 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442 -- the party went from 1 (4%) to 13 (52%) of
   25 agents knowing; "Five out of the twelve invited agents showed up"; 1.3% (6 of 453) hallucinated answers;
   embellishment observed.
6. Blackboard systems. Nii 1986, AI Magazine 7(2): https://ojs.aaai.org/aimagazine/index.php/aimagazine/article/view/537 ;
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackboard_system ; LbMAS https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01701 (public/private spaces,
   planner/decider/critic/conflict-resolver/cleaner roles; 81.68% vs 80.56% best baseline; below CoT on MATH).
7. MAST, "Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?" https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657 -- 14 failure modes; [summary]
   inter-agent misalignment 32.15%, reasoning-action mismatch 13.2%. Flooding of manipulated knowledge:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07791 -- fabricated facts spread and persist through agent memory.
8. Contract Net Protocol: Smith 1980, IEEE Trans. Computers C-29(12):1104-1113, doi 10.1109/TC.1980.1675516;
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_Net_Protocol -- announce / bid / award / inform; cancel carried no penalty in
   the original (levelled commitment: Sandholm & Lesser 1995). Original PDF did not render: details NOT verified.
9. BlueMap markers. https://bluemap.bluecolored.de/wiki/customization/Markers.html (config marker-sets or the API);
   https://github.com/BlueMap-Minecraft/BlueMapAPI/wiki (API markers are NOT persistent; re-add in onEnable).
   External-file route (unofficial): https://git.macaw.me/blue/bluemap-telemetry -- NOT verified against BlueMap docs.

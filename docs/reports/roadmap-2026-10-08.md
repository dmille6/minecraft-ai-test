# Roadmap (owner-approved 2026-10-08)

**The goal, in the owner's words:** "I have no interest in beating the game, I want my bots to be able to build cool
structures, survive, and work together for long periods of time." Worlds stay **peaceful** for a while.

Work is ranked by four things: **build**, **survive**, **cooperate**, and **last** (it still works after weeks, unattended).
Game progression (diamonds, enchanting, the Nether) is in scope only where it serves one of those.

## 2. Last: long periods (next priority after the foundation)

- **Depletion is the long-run killer.** Reseeded worlds took log gathering from 5% to 58%, then it fell 67% in six days
  on identical code. Anything that only works on a fresh world does not count.
- **Renewables:** wood (tree farm, built and queued); stone (a cobblestone generator, lava plus water, gives stone
  without hollowing out the land and keeps bots off dangerous mining); then sand, clay and wool sources, planned as
  builds need them.
- **Unattended operation:**
  - no permanently stuck bots (the trapped-bot work);
  - automatic recovery after a reboot: a persistent mayor unit, sandbox 1 enabled at boot, read scripts out of /tmp,
    chains that survive a reboot;
  - logs and stores that do not grow without bound (the cobble claim log, the mayor's snapshots).

## 3. Build

- The blueprint builder exists (design: blueprint-builder-design-2026-10-05.md). The tree farm proves it; then the town
  workshop, the safe mineshaft, then larger structures the owner chooses.
- **Materials come from deliberate production, not hoarded pickups:** sand to glass, clay to bricks, stone to stone
  bricks, wool from sheep with shears. Random decorative pickups go to the junk well. Once building starts, revisit
  keeping clay (bricks) and a little flint in town stock.
- Iron infrastructure that serves building and logistics: hoppers (sorted storage), rails and minecarts (mine to town).

## 4. Cooperate

- **Roles:** gatherers, builder, logistics (feeding the builder), and a rescuer.
- **Town stock:** a town-stock record the overseer reads, and orders assigned from it.
- **Evidence first:** the C2 overseer comparison (results 10-08) is the first test of whether a big-model overseer
  improves cooperation. The strategist layer comes after that, shadow first.

## Measure: the weekly long-run check (to build)

Per town, weekly, alongside the daily scoreboard:

1. Did it build something from its own materials?
2. Did deaths and stuck bots stay low?
3. Did output hold steady rather than decay?

This tracks the goal directly, not iron counts alone.

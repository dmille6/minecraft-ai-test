1. Recommendation

Yes—as a modest landmark attached to verified shared transport. No—as a monumental open excavation all the way to y16.

The existing town stair already supplies the iron descent. Replacing that upper journey has limited immediate transport value. The stronger case is replacing repeated personal cuts below y16: 82% of fleet deepen rows report “could not stand,” and 13–18% of trips end stranded.

Those are failure-row and trip rates, not proof that a new staircase eliminates those failures. Branch mining and return navigation still need attention. Only 2 of 55 fleet deaths occurred during these trips; do not sell the stepwell as a demonstrated fleet mortality fix.

I recommend a 12-block-deep terraced court, then enclosed shared switchbacks to y16 and eventually feet y−53. The lava station works on cells near y−55 from a dry platform. A water pool is scenery, not the descent mechanism.

Disagreement with the proposed sequence: the dry shared descent below iron should not depend on bucketfix. Only pool filling and lava operations need that dependency. Their releases can remain separate.

2. Shape and cell rules

Use full-block treads, cobblestone retaining work, cobblestone-wall railings, restrained brick accents, and repeated symmetrical flights. The stepped court supplies the Indian stepwell character without a 54-block-deep open pit.

All following heights describe feet unless explicitly called a block coordinate.
Let surface entrance feet be F, measured on site; F=70 is an illustration.
Local coordinates are x,z around the court center.

Court:
- Footprint: 33×33, x,z=−16…16, including its outer railing.
- Three terraces, each four blocks lower: F−4, F−8, F−12.
- Four cardinal flights, each three clear blocks wide.
- Terrace bands are four blocks wide before railings; retain three clear blocks.
- Bottom court: 7×7, x,z=−3…3, at F−12.
- Pool: 3×3 centered in that court, one block of water deep, open sky.

Generate the main floor:
- r=max(abs(x),abs(z)).
- d=min(12,4×ceil((16−r)/4)).
- Floor block coordinate is y=F−d−1.
- Clear overburden above that floor to the surveyed surface, preserving planned fixtures.
- The outer ring r=16 is at F.

Generate the flights and their shoulders:
- For 4≤r≤16 and min(abs(x),abs(z))≤2, override d=16−r.
- The three columns with perpendicular coordinate −1…1 are walking treads.
- Perpendicular coordinates −2 and +2 are solid shoulder columns, carrying railings.
- Flights therefore descend twelve one-block steps, crossing terrace levels at r=12,8,4.
- Fill beneath raised flight treads where necessary; do not leave unsupported overhangs.

Railings:
- On each flight shoulder, place a wall at its local feet height.
- On high terrace edges adjoining a drop greater than one, place walls on the high-side floor.
- Omit terrace rail cells only across the three-wide flight opening.
- Join flight and terrace rails without diagonal corner escape gaps.
- Outer ring has walls except at the four three-wide entrances.
- Validate the resulting collision geometry; reject any plan that narrows a designated route below its clear width.

Pool:
- Water cells x,z=−1…1 at y=F−13; basin floor y=F−14.
- Solid perimeter and bottom; no drain or connection to the mine.
- The surrounding two-block-wide bottom court remains dry.
- Keep the basin dry until bucketfix has fleet KEEP and is released on showcase.
- The pool is shallow enough to step out; do not fence it into a trap.

Lights:
- Torch posts outside clear walking cells at every flight/terrace intersection.
- Additional perimeter lights at intervals no greater than eight walking blocks.
- Lights on each underground landing and at flight midpoints.
- Record every post and torch cell; no opportunistic decoration outside the record.

Underground descent:
- Enter through a bottom-court side portal into a separate switchback stack.
- Choose its rotation and exact portal connector from the survey; do not intersect the pool.
- Two-wide flights, three air cells above every tread, solid partition between flights.
- Landing depth three blocks; landings span both lanes and the partition.

A repeatable 16-block descent module, starting at feet H:
- Flight A: x=i, z=0,1, feet H−1−i, i=0…7.
- Far landing: x=8…10, z=0…4, feet H−8.
- Flight B: x=7−i, z=3,4, feet H−9−i, i=0…7.
- Near landing: x=−3…−1, z=0…4, feet H−16.
- Next module starts at H−16.
- Floor at feet−1; air at feet…feet+2.
- Add solid face-neighbor lining wherever it is not another planned air cell.
- z=2 between flights remains a solid partition.
- Approximate lined envelope: 16×7 horizontally.

For an endpoint not divisible by sixteen, truncate the last flight at the target feet height and add a three-deep terminal landing beyond its end. Generate and validate the connector explicitly.

At F=70, the court ends at 58; enclosed descent continues 42 blocks to 16.
Later, a separate verified continuation descends 69 blocks from 16 to −53.
Do not change mining depth to −55 merely because lava and obsidian occur there.

4. Construction safety

“Never opens a pocket under a walking bot” must be an operational invariant, not an optimistic digging order.

Use one exclusive construction frontier. Visitors may enter only completed sections separated from work by recorded barriers.

For each small work unit:
- Revalidate the completed return route and reserve enough time to leave.
- Acquire the project/segment lease and check its generation before each mutation.
- Read the target, its six neighbors, floor, roof, and relevant falling-block column.
- Unknown reads stop work.
- Confirm no entity occupies the target, its supported standing cell, or affected falling column.
- Establish lining and floor from the previous verified stand.
- Clear headroom top-down, then feet space.
- Read back server-confirmed results before publishing the new stand.
- Never dig the builder’s floor, another bot’s floor, or a support carrying an occupied route.

For the court, excavate in shallow horizontal lifts with a maintained access ramp. Complete retaining faces and guards before exposing the next public edge. Do not dig the whole central volume first.

Cave-breach protocol:
- Detect empty neighboring cells before removing the separating block.
- Seal the exposed boundary using reserved cells and reachable support faces.
- If sealing requires removing unsafe material, an unreachable placement, or unreserved expansion: stop above it.
- Water/lava in a proposed walking cell stops that segment; it is not a request to keep digging.
- Unexpected flowing liquid: retreat along verified dry stands, close a preplanned bulkhead if reachable, quarantine the segment.
- No improvised rescue excavations or arbitrary plugs under the building project’s authority.

Reuse shaftgeom.nextOp’s line-before-dig approach, but not blindly: its temporary seals/supports must also be represented in the new project record.

Avoid stair-trap failures through ordered waypoint traversal and explicit egress, not merely wider stairs.
Maintain a renewed stair body claim through transit and bounded interruptions; release it on every exit.
Do not suppress drowning, lava, or other emergency handling globally.
Extending reflex/trip recognition to the new route is protected work.

Paper sandbox must prove:
- Ascending and descending full-block flights, turns, rail joins, and head clearance.
- Every real placement from its planned stand, including lining behind rock.
- Dry-cave, wet-roof, lava-neighbor, gravel-roof, and unsupported-floor fixtures.
- Unknown chunks, refused clicks, stale leases, restart, timeout, inventory exhaustion.
- Occupancy races and orderly egress after every interrupted construction prefix.
- No dirt-pillaring reflex inside completed transport sections.
- Pool escape after bucketfix, and station access with existing lava guards enabled.

5. Miner integration

Initially miners keep using the existing town stair and current routing. A constructed landmark must not become a route merely because its blocks exist.

Publish separate states: construction complete, traversal verified, and transport enabled.
Persist route ID, generation, endpoint, and ordered stands with each trip.

Protected changes include:
- deepmine.mjs: route selection, route persistence, eligibility and return budgeting.
- skills.mjs deep*: descend by verified route; replace personal “deepen” cuts only when the shared lower route is enabled.
- Route-specific repair/egress and keep-out integration.
- Any required admission, escape, or reflex changes.

Iron trips leave at feet16. Diamond trips continue to −53, branch from recorded portals, and return through the same shared route.
Branch trails remain necessary beyond those portals.
Exclude all structural and reserved air cells from mining and sealing candidates.

Initially allow one underground user, with return priority. Wider stairs do not establish multi-bot traffic safety.

If the shared route fails verification, stop new admissions. Existing occupants retain its record and egress handling.
Do not silently fall back to personal digging midway through a failed shared descent.

Tier 3 integration requires both review engines, fleet canary, and fleet KEEP before showcase.
Measure completed round trips, stranded returns, depth reached, reflex events, and travel time against matched controls.
A reasonable acceptance target is zero stranded returns in 100 completed test trips, with failure-injection tests; that is evidence, not a guarantee.

- t=5…7, u=−1…1: three-by-three landing floor y=F−5.
- Shoulders u=±2, t=0…7: floor at corresponding tread/landing level, wall above.
- Far barrier t=8,u=−2…2: supported wall closing the unfinished continuation.

Tests:

- Emit ordinary project-attributed _build place/clear evidence.

Kill switch:

7. Cost and schedule

Planning ranges, not surveyed bills. Assumptions: F≈70, mostly solid terrain, natural sound rock retained, full-block treads, no major aquifer, one builder frontier.

First piece:
- Approximately 80–160 cobble, 21 wall blocks, 2–4 torches, zero required bricks.
- Approximately 80–160 digs, depending on entrance grading.
- Roughly 1–2 active builder-hours including walking, checking, and replenishment.

Full dry court plus enclosed route to16:
- Approximately 6,000–9,000 digs including court, stair, portal, and finishing.
- Approximately 1,500–3,000 cobble placed; 400–550 wall blocks; 50–80 torches.
- Cap decorative bricks at 48 initially.
- Wall crafting consumes additional cobble roughly one-for-one; do not count walls as free.
- This is substantial excavation even though only twelve blocks are open to sky.

Dry continuation 16→−53:
- Approximately 600–900 digs including landings and small terminal chamber.
- Approximately 300–800 lining/tread blocks, 20–40 torches.
- Enclosed flights need little decorative railing.
- Cave crossings can dominate this allowance; stop and re-estimate instead of absorbing unlimited work.

Station:
- Reserve approximately 150–300 enclosure blocks and 8–16 lights for budgeting only.
- Exact digs, casting cells, bucket access, and operating cycle depend on the missing obsidian design and surveyed sources.
- Keep walking feet at−53; isolate service cells near−55 behind a solid partition.
- Casting and bucket access must never make lava a walkable floor or expose it beside a public walkway.
- Prove actual service reach and lava-guard compatibility; do not bypass guards to make the station operate.

Time model:
- Dig time = digs/3.6 per minute.
- Placement time ≈ placements/5 per minute as a conservative serial allowance.
- The tower’s simultaneous “5 placed + 5 cleared” rate is not evidence of 10 interchangeable excavation actions/minute.
- Add 30–60% for hauling, verification, egress, and interruptions.

That gives approximately 45–90 active builder-hours for court plus y16 route, and 5–13 for the dry lower continuation.
At 15 bots spending 2–3% of time building, aggregate capacity is only 7.2–10.8 builder-hours/day, before competing projects.
Expect roughly one to two weeks or more for the upper work, not an overnight landmark.

Cobble supply is adequate; labor and placement are the constraint.
Only about 71 existing bricks are uncommitted. A brick-faced megapit would compete with other bills and the 130/day production rate for little descent benefit.

NAMED DESIGN DEVIATIONS (final)
  1. A qualifying furnace's fuel slot must be empty.
  2. No kept stone. A 1-3 remainder is ordinary stone; unspent inputs are booked, never owed.
  3. The 80% coverage floor, with a recount at ANY coverage for a reachable container the index does not know.
  4. The wood family is accounted over every log and plank name.
  6. The output slot must be empty (own = null).
  (5, claims not subtracted, is gone: outstanding draw claims are subtracted at the take.)

NAMED RESIDUALS (in the registration)
  - copper_ingot has no stock-journal group, so another bot's ordinary copper draw is not subtracted.
  - Parts in a container no recount can open are not seen by the buffer.
  - Input gained on the way back can hide a recovery shortfall up to that gain.
  - A kill inside the take's clicks can draw up to a half stack.
  - A -1 on the held slot within 2 s of a failed placement that is not the placement can read as one.

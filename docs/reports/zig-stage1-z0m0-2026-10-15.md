# Ziggurat + shaft, STAGE 1: offline siting gates Z0 and M0 (2026-10-15, r3)

**Owner decision (10-15 ~05:00Z):** "lets work on a shared mineshaft, and build a ziggurat that is no hollow as an
interesting project per world".
- **Z1 = SOLID, by layers.**
- **Z2-Z8 are operator defaults** from the design doc's recommendations. The owner can change any of them:

  | # | Default |
  |---|---|
  | Z2 | a monument lane outside the projects' WIP=1, sharing their body-time budget, with project jobs first |
  | Z3 | the ziggurat is the generic builder's first job |
  | Z4 | the town floor takes surplus cobble first |
  | Z5 | the cuts are accepted, in the order S1 → S2 → S3 |
  | Z6 | setback S=3 |
  | Z7 | no stone_bricks for now |
  | Z8 | foreign skin blocks are counted and accepted |

**Stage 1 is READ-ONLY.**
- It used copies of the 04:14Z snapshots (`/var/tmp/bm-worlds/<pool>/world` on 10.0.0.30, scp'd to scratch) and the
  pools' composter and junk-well records (`/var/lib/mcai`, read-only). No world command ran, and no live file was opened.
- Code is on branch **`zig-offline`**, based on treefarm-01's tip b7cc60b5:
  - `bots/src/monument.mjs`: geometry, frozen plan, column scan, site score;
  - `bots/src/shaftplan.mjs`: landings, envelope, survey, the collar rule, `walkReach`;
  - `bots/scripts/zigsite.mjs`: the gate driver;
  - `bots/scripts/regionread.mjs`: the read-only region decoder;
  - `scripts/host/zigexposure.py`: exposure from the skill files;
  - `bots/test/monument-plan.test.mjs`: **34 behaviour tests**. Each refusal sits beside the control that differs from it
    in one thing.
- **The same pure functions will run inside the jobs** (stage 4/5), so the site the bots build on is the site this gate
  scored.
- Output: `docs/reports/zig-z0m0-2026-10-15.json`, holding every world's result, the rules used, and exposure with its
  denominators.

## Positive controls

- **The region decoder** read each town's platform under home in 12 of 12 worlds (11 `stone_bricks`, placebo-a's
  `oak_planks`). It was validated 10-13 against read-only RCON on 10 of 10 cells.
- **Every refusal in the scorer and the survey** is tested beside its accepted twin:
  - lava, a partial read, an unread column, foundation share, an avoid overlap;
  - a chest, a pond over the limit, a hill needing levelling, a dirt pillar;
  - water inside the volume vs above it;
  - a cliff (guarded) vs flat ground, a stair approach 2 low vs flat;
  - the stub end face, a waterlogged lining cell, an unread shaft cell, liquid before the first landing, falling blocks;
  - the open entry pad;
  - a walled-off pad vs flat ground.
- **In the real worlds:**
  - every world has shaft candidates stopped above 16 by liquid (`shaftBottoms`), so the stop is live;
  - every world refuses candidates for water and for levelling; lava refuses some in 10 of 12, bee nests in 8 of 12,
    and partial reads in 5 of 12. The counts per reason are in the JSON `zigRejects`.
- **Tree-farm avoidance** runs treefarm-01's own `canonicalFarm` on the snapshot:
  - it found 6-9 plots in 11 worlds;
  - for hive-d it answered "no farm site" (2 usable plots, needs 6), which is a confirmed absence;
  - any other null or an error refuses both gates for that world. None occurred.

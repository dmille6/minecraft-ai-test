# KEEP at +180 for four-pool bag fixes: rule A' (gate v35, 2026-10-08)

**Status: BUILT AND STAGED (`~/gate-v35`), NOT INSTALLED.** The coordinator installs it together with `~/bagfix-regs`
in the empty slot after withdraw2-01.

## The decision

The owner delegated the decision to the operator and Codex, who agreed: "Use A-prime exactly as calibrated… Report the
bag metric at KEEP without gating on it."

A canary registered `"class": "bag-fix"`, drawn at **four** pools, KEEPs at its +180 read only when **all** of these
hold (`bagfixrule.keep_early`):
- every gate and guard passed: no REVERT, no WATCH, no blocked line. **One exception:** the all-cause gate's "death gate
  held (v21)" note (>= 2 canary deaths without a trip) is not a refusal here; the death-rate screen below is its
  calibrated replacement at +180;
- exposure is ready;
- no bag-fix extension is in force;
- every registered read is readable. Evidence is bound and present, immobiledid is readable, and bagread's primary is
  not None;
- **the cumulative canary death rate is <= the control's**, as a point estimate (`death_rate_ok`). Unmeasured exposure
  on either arm fails.

If any of these fails, the read answers NOT_YET and the canary continues to +360 under the existing rules. That
includes the v33 extension path, which is untouched.

**What +360 does NOT add (round 1, Codex):** at +360 the existing rules apply unchanged. There, WATCH is advisory and
A''s readability check does not run. So a canary whose bagread is blind can be refused early KEEP at +180 and still KEEP
at +360. That is today's behaviour, not something A' introduces.

**The bag metric** (every bagread field) is printed at any bag-fix KEEP read and never gated on there. The KEEP
artifact carries `extra.keep_rule = "A-prime +180"`. After promotion, the daily scoreboard and the bag census are the
fleet tripwire.

## Calibration (read-only, on the host)

**Designs compared:**
- A: 4 pools, KEEP at +180;
- B: 2 pools, KEEP at +360 (today);
- C: 4 pools, KEEP at +360;
- A': the rule above.

**Death gate** (`scripts/host/keep180_deaths.py`):
- It uses the all-cause gate (at least 2 deaths and LB > 1.25, polled every 5 min) on the live-estimator death bins
  (`usafe_bins.py`).
- Harm is injected per pool as Poisson at (k−1) × the pool's own rate.
- About 1,220 draws per design.
- **The A' screen is `bagfixrule.death_rate_ok` itself**: the replay calls the built function.

| false KEEP (lower is better) | A | B (today) | C | **A'** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| false trip, no change | 2.6% | 5.3% | 5.6% | **5.5%** |
| deaths ×2 | 82.6% | 75.7% | 70.9% | **72.7%** |

| deaths ×5 | 31.6% | 24.8% | 11.7% | **12.5%** |
| no-change canaries KEPT at +180 | — | — | — | 51% |

**The replay on the built code does NOT exactly match the decision table, and the replay is the right one.**
- The decision table had A' at 5.5 / 70.7 / 42.0 / 12.5, with 56% kept.
- Its screen compared the canary with every other pool, including isolated-a..d.
- The built rule reads the death counts from immobiledid's harm, whose control EXCLUDES isolated-* and self-* bots.
  The replay now uses that same population.
- Deaths ×2 moves from 70.7 to 72.7, deaths ×3 from 42.0 to 41.6, and kept-at-+180 from 56% to 51%.
- A' stays better than today at every k (75.7 / 55.5 / 24.8).

**Harm guards** (`scripts/host/keep180_bins.py`, `keep180_power.py`):
- v15c and v11 are rebuilt per bot, at 420 draws per design over 22 cuts and 6 days.
- **Positive control:** `keep180_check.py` reproduces 4 of 4 real immobiledid evidence objects exactly
  (towndeposit-01/-02 at +180 and +360, airpocket-01 at +180).
- A' is scored as: early KEEP when the +180 guards are silent with no breach and `death_rate_ok`; else the +360 read.

Reading the guard table:
- A' is no worse than today on deaths, and is better on strong harms.
- On harms just past a guard's threshold, the four-pool read lets about 3–5 points more through. That comes from the
  pool count, not the timing: C, at +360, shows the same.
- Today's B is "better" there partly through its own noise: it reverts 2.4% of no-change canaries, against 0.7% for A'.

**The built rule IS the replayed rule** (round 1, both engines). Both calibrations call `bagfixrule.keep_early`
itself, and the A' numbers above are what it produced.
- **"death gate held (v21)" is not a refusal.** That note is the all-cause gate holding with >= 2 canary deaths. The
  death-rate screen replaces it at +180, as calibrated: two canary deaths at half the control's rate KEEP; three above
  it go to +360 (tested).
- **Exactly four distinct pools** (tested: five pools, and four names that are two pools).
- **Other WATCHes the live rule refuses on:**
  - a v15c single breach: modelled as `breach` in keep180_power;
  - an own-line or friction WATCH, a v23 licensed change row held below the floor, a rung-linked death (ladder changes
    only), and the fix's own exposure not ready at +180: none of these is modelled, because the null has no fix.
  - Each of these can only send a canary to +360 (design C, the safe direction), so the live early-KEEP share can be
    below 51%. It cannot raise the false-KEEP rate above A'.

**Disclosed limits:**
- **Two estimators.** Both feed the same `death_rate_ok`, but they are not the same measurement:
  - the live screen uses immobiledid's bot-hours (60-min position-grid minutes) and death counts;
  - the calibration used the death gate's capped-row-gap exposure.
  - One measured comparison: airpocket-01 at +180, canary arm, 30.0 bot-h (immobiledid) against 30.8 bot-h
    (usafegate's capped gaps).
- **Effective n is 6 days** (the windows overlap). The draws are random four-pool sets, not drawrec's filtered ones.

  expected text ('drawn at 2 pools'), and the dry run refused on it, as designed.

  lock with a unique token. It refuses if any hold exists, and releases only its own. `scripts/test_holdtoken.py` tests

  predicted digest, a verified manifest and rollback.

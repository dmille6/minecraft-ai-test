# T11 bundle: tidytop, grade-tidy for T11, the grade_pct reader (2026-11-18)

Branch `t11bundle-01show` is built on f7cc2a19 (drawshort-01show-rel, on top of buildfirst and zoneplant), in `.wt-t11`. It is not pushed. The design is `docs/reports/gradetidy-design-2026-11-18.md` on main.

## Pieces

**(a) tidytop.** The tidytop commits are replayed from `tidytop-01show-rel3` without dirtbank. Two rel3 commits mixed tidytop with dirtbank (ae6d8c0e5 and 52c0dbde7); only their tidytop parts were taken. This base has no tidyflow, so the strike line was rebuilt: a cell waiting for dirt is not a strike.

**(b) grade-tidy**, in `bots/src/gradetidy.mjs` and the inside-the-wall branch of `tidy.mjs` `siteStage`.
- **The target file.** The pool's `grade-target.json` is written by `scripts/host/gradetarget.py`. It holds draft 3's box, h and class, plus `v`, the source's sha256, and the gate. Gate T11 is the 4-connected run of class `t` at y69 that holds (10,-26): 218 columns.
- **How the survey reads it.** `siteStage` reads the file through `setGradeSource`. Its call lines did not change, so the existing structural pins still hold.
- **What each gated column gets.**
  - Above the target: cuts, top first.
  - Below the target: earth fills (which become `core`) with a `topsoil` cell at the target.
  - At the target with a non-earth top: a `topsoil` re-top, but only on a block the visit's foreign pass can dig (`RETOP_DIGGABLE` = `FOREIGN_CLEAR`).
  - A crust over a hollow: the crust is cut and the hollow is filled from its floor up, at most 4 deep. The cuts carry `hollow` and the fills carry `opener`, so the fills are never waived.
- **Classes.** `t`, `b`, `a` and `e` get a target. `o`, `f` and `w` are no opinion. An unknown class is also no opinion; it is counted as `g.unknown_class:n` and never throws.
- **Columns left alone.** A column the grade cannot work as a whole is left and counted by reason: `tree` (a trunk; a canopy overhead does not count), `built`, `water_beside`, `not_cuttable`, `retop_not_diggable`, `hollow_deep`, `avoided:<kind>`, or `cell_column`. The survey's own `keepOff` (no-litter plots, reservations, then `avoid`) is honoured at run time.
- **The survey's other rules stay out of grade columns.** Pits, pillars, rough ground, regrade and banks are dropped from them (`dropGradeCols`). Staircases and the rough survey treat them as busy.
- **Columns the record already names.** In the refresh, a gated column whose named work is all done, carried or waived is released and planned from the target (`releaseGradeCols`). A column with any open work keeps that work.
- **A changed or withdrawn plan.** The record carries the plan's stamp (`grade_v`). A refresh under another stamp, or with the file gone, drops the open grade work (`dropStaleGrade`). Holes the grade already opened keep their fills: an opener that is down, a grade cut that is down, or a re-top already dug out.
- **Order of work: cut first, then fill, inside T11.** Grade segments go first in a visit (`gradeFirst`), and grade cuts are walked first (`chainCuts`). Dirt dug during a visit is laid in the same visit. The test shows a bag with no dirt cutting a pillar and filling the hole next to it.
- **Rows.**
  - The refresh row adds `grade_v=v1:<sha8>:T11`.
  - Its `kind` gains `/gcut<n>/gfill<n>`.
  - Its `why` gains `g.<reason>:n`, `grade_released:n` and `grade_dropped:n`.
  - `_grade_mode` reports the file's state.
  - `GRADE=off` gives the survey as before.

**(c) Core fills consume bag spoil: verified on f7cc2a19, no change.**
- `TIDY_PALETTE.core` is `['cobblestone', 'cobbled_deepslate', 'dirt', ...]` (tidy.mjs:50), and the placer takes the first block the bag can spare (`blockFor`).
- The visit's keep is read once at the start (townKeeps: stone `min(held, 64)`). Spoil dug during the visit therefore counts as spare and is spendable at once.
- The fill bill counts the bag's spare cobblestone and dirt before drawing anything (`fillWant`).
- `gradetidy.test.mjs` "(c)" checks this behaviour.

**(d) grade_pct reader.** `scripts/host/gradepct.py` reads a copy of the region files, never on .40.
- **The figure.** grade_pct = columns at their target height with an earth top (dirt_path counts) ÷ every ground column. Unread columns stay in the denominator and are named.
- **Where it reports.** Town-wide, per terrace (T11 is named), for the gate, and for the town outside the gate. It also gives the height-only share and 2+ steps (ground-ground and ground-fixed). With `--before`, it lists the columns outside the gate whose height changed.
- **A failed read.** A read of nothing raises an error rather than reporting 0%.
- **Tests.** `scripts/host/test_gradepct.py` covers behaviour and has 5 anchored mutants.
- **Check against the 06:30Z copy.**
  - Town: 36.4% (height 49.4%, 1,842 pairs with a 2+ step), matching the plan's summary.
  - T11: 54.1% (118/218), matching the design's baseline.

## 3. The design (Claude, built)

- **The building** (`blueprints/enchantshop@1.json`, generated by `enchantshop-2026-11-04/make_enchantshop.py`): the storage hall's
  shape on a **17 x 11 pad whose stand ring is the 19 x 13 plot exactly** (offline: a 19 x 13 pad's ring met the hall's reservation at
  z -52 and an expedition marker at x -3). Walls set back one cell, a stone-brick course, a plank course with glass bays, stone-brick
  eaves, a plank roof, a 3-wide doorway south toward the hall and home.
- **The enchanting nook:** the table at (u 5, v 5); **15 bookshelves on the 5 x 5 ring round it, less its front middle (the way in)**,
  at the table's level; the 3 x 3 between kept air (`gap`: no torch, sign or cell there -- a torch blocks a shelf; minecraft.wiki
  Enchanting_Table: "one block of air, or a replaceable block between them"; 15 shelves = level 30). A test asserts every shelf at
  max(|du|,|dv|) = 2 with the cell between in the gap. The anvil against the opposite wall (u 14, v 5), the book chest and the crafting
  table on the back wall, three signs (ENCHANTING / ANVIL / BOOKS) on plank wall cells with free stands.
- **The shell is a record; the fit-out is NOT cells.** The record's `fit` list (anvil, table, shelf-01..15 in placing order: back row
  first, the way in last) is placed by the **fitter**, so a missing anvil is never damage, never holds the project order and never makes
  a builder stop `no_material` (the copper hall's dead end).
- **The fitter** (the complete record's upkeep, one lease `fitter`): `fitNext` picks the first open slot whose plan is whole --
  `fitPlan` expands it to the root (anvil -> iron blocks -> ingots; table -> book -> paper -> cane; shelf -> planks -> logs), using the
  bag's fit items and its own owed cargo, then the town above the keeps (**iron 64**: the toolkit's picks; **diamonds 3**: a deep pick).
  It draws exactly that (matsWithdraw, each verified take booked into a per-bot cargo file), crafts the chain at the workshop's own
  table, places the item by buildrun's visit on a one-cell record, stamps the slot `placed` when it reads back, and banks the leftovers.
  A stamped slot that later reads open is **lost**: named, never rebuilt by itself.
- **Each furnishing is its own request:** `_enchantshop_gate fit=anvil|table|shelves why=ok|wait|placed|held short=<root item:n>
  need=<root bill>` rows (town-wide cadence: on change, else every 30 min); `requests.mjs` turns each into one town request per
  (furnishing, root input) -- `enchant_table:obsidian x4`, `enchant_table:leather x1`, `enchant_shelves:leather x45`, ... -- and closes
  an input's request when it is no longer short, all of them when the furnishing stands.
- **Order:** `shop.mjs PROJECTS` after the storage hall, after the extension and its demolition; unsited it WAITS until the extension
  is sited with X1 (`enchantOpening`); siting is the plot exactly (`enchantSitingPlan`: X1's own land:plot zone is the one zone
  dropped -- the coordinator's "X1 an exception to the land-plan plot reservation"; every other zone, record and corridor refuses).

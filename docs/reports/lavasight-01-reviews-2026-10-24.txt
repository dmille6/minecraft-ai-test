DESIGN r1 (882dfb66) -- Codex (codex exec --sandbox read-only)
==================================================================================================================
VERDICT: CHANGE

1. **Price-only is reasonable, but the "never a wall" proof is false.** Movement costs include omitted charges and entity costs; legal base moves can disappear.
2. **The footprint is approximate, not equivalent to the guard.** It misses deep-drop checks, ignores solid occlusion, and does not correctly represent all head/intermediate/landing charges.
3. **Seed hashing checks out:** all three examples reproduce using signed int64 little-endian input and digest `readInt32LE(4):readInt32LE(0)`. Atomic publishing is appropriate. Expiration, world-to-directory mapping, and same-seed foreign files need explicit contracts.
4. **Reflex suppression is the right approach.** Keepout's intentional exclusions remain, but ordinary skill/homefix paths inherit the cost-deletion problem. Protectzone shares the same accumulated movement cost; it is not arithmetically independent. The proposed tests do not establish executable recovery through the corridor guard.
5. **The gates are insufficient.** G2 is tautological as specified; G3 is neither independently recomputable nor a check of move preservation. Refusals/bot-hour alone can reward reduced movement.
6. **R1-R6:** R1 agree, with lifecycle fixes; R2 reject the current proof; R3 max-composition is sensible, but 16/4 needs a corrected bound; R4 defer warn-only rows unless a decision uses them; R5 separate publisher is acceptable with explicit destination mapping; R6 retain bot-hour rates as secondary, adding guard-check and completed-goal denominators.

**Findings**

- **P1 - A priced walk can be deleted.** Design `docs/reports/lavasight-01-design-2026-10-24.md:159` excludes entity costs, which `bots/node_modules/mineflayer-pathfinder/lib/movements.js:284` adds before the deletion checks. A read-only probe of the installed forward generator produced **base cost 53, candidate no neighbour**: 26 entity intersections in each checked body cell plus three 16-point charges yields 101. This is a no-dig walk. Preserve base-admitted moves at the move level, or retain a deterministic recovery mechanism; a per-cell cap alone cannot prove preservation.

- **P1 - The move enumeration and dig exception are incomplete.** At `movements.js:417`, `:419`, `:439`, and `:449`, diagonal cost includes **4-5 selected charges**, or **5-6 for jump-up**; both side alternatives are evaluated, so raw hook calls are higher. Forward normally charges 3, cardinal jump-up 3, drop-down 3, vertical down 1, and up/tower 1. Parkour is disabled. Design `:152` misses the conditional diagonal charge and miscounts vertical down. Also, `dontCreateFlow` checks immediate liquid neighbours (`movements.js:257`), not the whole core/ring; it does not justify dismissing newly deleted digs at design `:163`. Include water, `liquidCost=2`, entities, placements, and dig labour in behavioural comparisons. The actual death-site price is **16**, not 25 (`bots/src/deathsites.mjs:19`).

- **P1 - "Exactly the footprint" and its proposed equivalence test are wrong.** Design `:141-146` conflicts with `bots/src/lavaguard.mjs:12`, `:29-38`, and `:54-58`: solid blocks terminate below scans, while the centre drop scan reaches 12 blocks and checks lateral lava. Probes confirmed that solid-covered lava is accepted despite being in the proposed core, while lava six below is refused outside even the ring. Drop-down also never charges its landing cell (`movements.js:489-500`); a generic head adjustment cannot repair that. Specify a conservative approximation honestly or price the actual swept move footprint, including occlusion and drops.

- **P1 - G2 cannot detect its intended defect.** Design `:212` defines `priced` as "index non-empty and not held"; consequently G2 at `:240` cannot observe `held=1, priced=1`, even if the callback incorrectly charges during a reflex. Record actual nonzero callback results with ownership inputs. G3 at `:241` also lacks replay inputs and checks only `>16`, missing an egress charge of 16 where the limit is 4. Require behavioural mutants that fail these gates.

- **P2 - Delivery needs explicit lifecycle and destination rules.** Design `:79`, `:103-113` assumes world W maps to `_pool-W`, and specifies rereading only on mtime changes. Expiration must run without a file change; login identity changes must invalidate cached cells; same-seed foreign-world files need world-label validation. Define handling for mixed-world directories, `POOL_STATE_DIR` overrides, and isolated pools without existing directories. See `bots/src/worldfacts.mjs:540-551`. Do not copy protectzone's retain-last-good error behaviour (`bots/src/protectzone.mjs:299`) into this fail-open reader.

- **P2 - Recovery tests stop before execution.** Design `:227-230` accepts "candidate finds the edge path" and "fallback selects home." But the corridor hook clears unsafe goals (`bots/src/index.mjs:1031-1035`), so neither proves escape or arrival. Test composed outward movement, corridor acceptance, and actual recovery completion, including exhausted relocation candidates and reflex ownership transitions.

- **P2 - The primary metric can improve through inactivity.** Design `:246` combines throttled corridor rows with blind-step refusals; corridor logging itself is limited to once per five seconds (`bots/src/index.mjs:1034`). Record unsampled checks/refusals, report refusal fraction per check, and pair that with completed lava-exposed goals per attempted goal. Keep refusals/bot-hour and productivity as supporting measures.

Review limitation: the referenced mineshaft r10 and hazardmap §7 reports were unavailable in the checked repository paths. Their exact contracts remain unverified. No files were modified.

DESIGN r1 (882dfb66) -- Claude (independent subagent, read-only)
==================================================================================================================
VERDICT: CHANGE

Answers to the six questions
1. Price-only instead of r10's exclusions. The approach is sound. For moves that do not dig, the bound holds with room to
   spare, but the text of 3.4 has several wrong facts (P2-1). With max() in force, I could not find any walk, jump, drop or
   scaffold move that lavasight deletes. The worst case is a diagonal jump-up: 1.414 + 5x16 + liquidCost 2 + 1 + 5 water
   entries = 89.4. Moves that dig can be deleted outside the guard's refusal set (P2-1). lavasight adds no dead end compared
   with the base. It also does not remove the one that already exists (P2-2).
2. Footprint and egress. The beside-core matches corridorSafe. The over-core does not, and drops are not priced (P1-1). The
   head-cell handling is fine: it only adds price. Egress works against the design's own goal (P2-2).
3. Delivery. Hashed-seed binding is correct. The uncommitted WIP bots/src/lavasight.mjs:47-55 does writeBigInt64LE, then
   readInt32LE(4):readInt32LE(0). That matches Guava hashLong().asLong() and the hi:lo of composter.mjs:529-533. Add a
   negative-seed case to the tests. Fail-open, the reseed handling (world_mismatch until the next refresh) and atomic rename
   are fine. The r1 text has gaps (P2-5).
4. Composition. I found no new dead end. keepout's 101 and the well's 1000 stay deliberate. protectzone, homefix and lavastep
   are independent. Two gaps: cost-capped searches (P1-1) and the reflex list (P3-1).
5. The read. G2 cannot fail (P1-2). G3 is a self-report (P2-4). The primary metric is distorted by the way its rows are
   written (P2-3).
6. R1-R6: answered at the end.

P1 (must fix)
- P1-1. The footprint is not what the guard refuses, so the "iff" test in 6 cannot pass. Design 0.2, 3.3 lines 139-147, 6
  line 222.
  - Over-core ignores solid floors. It prices F.y = y+1..y+3 within Chebyshev 1 regardless of what is in between. But
    cellLavaSafe stops its downward scan at the first solid block (lavaguard.mjs:12), and dropLavaSafe returns safe at dy=1
    on a solid block (lavaguard.mjs:38). So a walk on a stone floor directly above a lava lake is accepted by the guard and
    core-priced by lavasight. That matters most at the depths where deep miners will work.
  - The over-pricing becomes a wall in capped searches. searchRadius is a cost cap (astar.js:51,95): the gather approach and
    its re-plans, APPROACH_SLACK 400 (digapproach.mjs:77,251-256, index.mjs:733); the ore tunnel, SEARCH_SLACK 400
    (oretunnel.mjs:49,227); reachprobe, 96 (reachprobe.mjs:143). About 8 over-priced core steps is enough for a target the
    base reaches to come back noPath.
  - The guard refuses things the price never sees. Lava 4-12 below an open column, and lava beside the drop
    (lavaguard.mjs:29-40), are guard refusals the footprint omits. The pathfinder never charges a drop's landing cell
    (movements.js:476-521; already noted at deathsites.mjs:14-16).
  - Mixed-sign diagonals are sampled differently. For (0,0) to (1,-1), corridorSafe samples the side cell (0,-1)
    (lavaguard.mjs:54, integer nodes from index.mjs:1030). getMoveDiagonal charges only min(cost1, cost2)
    (movements.js:430-436). So a diagonal hugging the core can be cheap to the planner and still refused by the guard.
  - Fix. Price only where the column in between is open, read at index rebuild. Add the drop rule. In the A* behaviour test,
    assert that corridorSafe accepts the candidate's planned path.
  - The WIP has moved past r1. bots/src/lavasight.mjs already has solidity and DROP in its footprint, and a move-level
    wrapNeighbors/moveSurcharge. The doc must be re-issued to describe that mechanism. The WIP also has two defects:
    moveSurcharge samples from block centres (+0.5), while corridorSafe interpolates integer nodes. For the diagonal above
    the WIP samples (1,0); the guard samples (0,-1). So the claim "sampled exactly as corridorSafe" is false.
    composterSafeMovements calls proto.getNeighbors (composter.mjs:749), which bypasses a wrapper installed on the moves
    instance.
- P1-2. G2 cannot fail. 5 line 212 and 7 line 240 define priced as "index non-empty and not held", so held=1 && priced=1 is
  impossible by construction. Rows are also sampled at 1 per 30 s, which misses rare reflex-held searches. Fix. Count
  price>0 while held at the hook itself, from the reflex name, in liveness counters. The WIP's held_priced counter does this.
  Write it into the doc. Also name a gate mutant positive control, as the common contract in mineshaft 7 requires.

P2 (should fix)
- P2-1. The arithmetic in 3.4 (lines 151-165) is wrong in several places. The death disc costs 16, not 25
  (deathsites.mjs:19). The comment at index.mjs:572 is stale. So max() is a no-op where both apply, and that is the correct
  reason it is safe. liquidCost is 2 (index.mjs:518) and is not counted in 87.4. Chargings per move: drop-down 3
  (movements.js:489-494); getMoveDown 1; getMoveUp 1; diagonal can be 6 (movements.js:419,428; deathsites.mjs:10). Dig
  deletions are not limited to cells the guard already refuses. Two hand-dug deepslate blocks inside the ring cost
  1 + 2x46 = 93 in the base and 105 with lavasight. The move-level wrapper (cost added after the move is generated) avoids
  every cost > 100 guard, digs included. Adopt it and drop the per-charging argument.
- P2-2. Egress (3.3 line 147) flattens the price gradient right where the guard refuses. Within Chebyshev 2 of the bot, a
  core cell costs the same as a cell further from the lava. A bot standing in the ring therefore plans its first steps
  along the lava edge, and the guard refuses them. The bound already guarantees the bot can always leave, so egress buys
  nothing. The price is not what holds a bot inside the footprint: corridorSafe samples the bot's own cell on any +x/+z
  first step (lavaguard.mjs:54 with index.mjs:1030). That trap belongs to lavastep and the stand-off reflex. Drop egress.
  Or cap a cell only at the bot's own cell's price, never below it. Separately, "plan time" is really live
  bot.entity.position read mid-search. Capture it once per search.
- P2-3. The primary metric is distorted by how its rows are written (7 line 246, R6). _lava_corridor is throttled to one
  row per 5 s per bot (index.mjs:1034), so it saturates during refusal storms. A failed explore leg writes up to 4
  blind-step rows, one per heading (skills.mjs:6957-6959). Those rows also rise with any failed leg, and the price can cause
  more failed legs through larger searches and timeouts. Use bot-minutes with at least one lava refusal (episodes). Make
  danger-block episodes the co-primary.
- P2-4. No gate can see the harm the design worries about. G3 is the function's own max_price. Make it record the combined
  max(death, lava) value so the sum-instead-of-max mutant trips it. Add a counterfactual: on each canary noPath or timeout
  where moves were priced, run a bounded search with the price off (reachprobe-sized) and log base_found. Gate: noPath
  where the base would have found a path, compared on the same budget. This is r10's excl_blocked, kept.
- P2-5. r1's delivery text is missing three things. Staleness must be re-judged on every 60 s tick even when mtime is
  unchanged. The WIP does; r1 does not say so. Ten worlds share a seed, so seed binding alone cannot tell board-a's file
  from board-d's. Add the pool label check (WIP world_label). A file with published_at is never byte-identical, which
  defeats the skip-if-identical rule (2.1 line 87). The WIP uses generated_at instead.

P3 (nits)

R1-R6
- R1: agree. No same-day shared write in v1. Your own table shows 77.3% of refusals had the bot's own refusal within 1
  block in the prior hour, so the ring carries most of the re-encounter value. The ring is lost on restart; accept that.
- R2: missed moves and facts as in P1-1 and P2-1: diagonal at 6 chargings, the drop landing never charged, liquidCost, the
  death disc at 16, digs in the ring.
- R3: 16 is the right number because it is the death-site constant, built on the same 6-charging arithmetic. max() is
  required; sum would delete diagonals. 4 for the ring is fine.
- R4: drop the warn rows from this canary. They change no behaviour and add an O(nodes x 256) scan to every path_update,
  which fires on every partial. Ship them as separate telemetry.
- R5: keep the publisher as a separate mcbot job, but trigger it from a successful refresh rather than a fixed 05:40 cron.
- R6: see P2-3. Count episodes rather than rows, and pair them with danger-block episodes.

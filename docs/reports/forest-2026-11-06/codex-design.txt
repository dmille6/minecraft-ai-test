I recommend an **18-tree managed forest serviced by the groundskeeper**, with the old farm converted into a small park. This is a design only; no files changed.

The code confirms the suspected self-exclusion: `farmFootprint` reserves plot columns through ten blocks above; `shopFootprintRefresh` installs them; `footprintCost` returns 100; pathfinder rejects break costs ≥100; `collectManually` rechecks safety. Scheduling also favors open projects. Sink rotation can admit upkeep, but does not guarantee farm service. The supplied production counts were not independently remeasured.

1. **Siting and geometry**

Use the current wall **and extensions**, not home radius or `farmBounds`—which explicitly selects inside the wall.

Require the entire forest, canopy allowance, stands, and access to be outside the wall with a **four-block clear buffer**. Exclude villages, protected zones, project footprints, and land-plan road corridors. Prefer existing manageable forest in the `treefarm` sector; score tick coverage first, existing trees second, earthwork third. Preserve hills by selecting a bench or separate terraces.

For simulation-distance=6, initially require all forest chunks within **four chunks Chebyshev distance** of an identified, regularly occupied bot anchor. This is a conservative siting filter, not proof of random ticking. Record occupancy; no nearby player means no growth promise. View-distance=8 is irrelevant to growth.

Native-spruce layout, in local coordinates:

- Three strips; trunk rows at `z=0,9,18`.
- Six trees per row at `x=0,4,8,12,16,20`: **18 trees**.
- Envelope `x,z=-3..23`: **27×27 blocks**.
- Two-wide cross-paths at `z=4–5,13–14,22–23`, connected along an edge.
- Level each strip independently; allow ≤2 blocks cut/fill per column, ≤160 changed ground blocks per strip. Reject deeper pits or steep terrain for separate repair planning.

For every planting cell, reserve a **temporary three-rise stone staircase**: beside the trunk, columns at offsets 1,2,3 contain heights 3,2,1; offset 4 is the ground landing. That is six reusable blocks, with clear headroom and sightlines.

With soil at `G`, its upper stand has feet at `G+4`. A same-column trunk log at `G+9` is approximately **4.01 blocks from the adjacent stand’s eye**. Thus the design covers the supplied 5–8-log spruce range plus a nine-log contingency without relying on the optimistic ground-reach observation. Walk explicitly to this stand, confirm position, fell upper logs first, descend, then remove lower logs and stump. Never dig the occupied support.

**Do not plant 2×2 spruce initially.** “Where possible” requires a proven mega-tree access system; 13–30+ logs exceed this stand. Use single spruce now. Birch can later use three-block spacing with redesigned access; oak needs complete branch enumeration, including fancy oak, before admission.

Stairs exist only during harvest and are removed before replanting, avoiding growth obstruction. Preserve hills outside terraces; fill holes solidly, remove recorded junk, clear path headroom, and verify no remaining logs. Leaves connected to neighboring trees may persist: explicitly survey and clear attributable remnants rather than assuming decay.

2. **Record and perpetual state machine**

Introduce `managedforest@1`, keyed by pool, dimension, and town. Suggested record fields:

`home, wallGen, landGen, bounds, zones, anchorChunks, geometryVersion, infrastructureReady, strips[], debts[], retirementRef`.

Each strip stores:

`id, floorY, phase, cycle, plantedAt, firstGrownAt, lastSurveyAt, nextDueAt, leaseGeneration, cells[], treeManifests[], cleanup[], retry`.

Each planting cell names its species, soil, sapling position, ground stand, upper stand, staircase cells, landing, growth envelope, and light cells. Tree manifests enumerate actual logs/leaves with expected block names and stand assignments. Publish authorization before mutation; retain generations for audit.

Lifecycle:

**survey → establish → growing → ready → felling → cleanup → grade → replant → growing**.

There is no terminal `complete`; only infrastructure readiness. Unknown chunks mean **unknown**, never harvested or clean. Select the oldest planted eligible strip; finish its cycle before opening another. Ungrown cells remain pending and get a growth diagnosis after 60 observed occupied minutes, not automatic destruction.

Reserve **18 spruce saplings after initial planting**, plus separately claimed outside-tree replacement debts. Draw actual inventory, not stale bag totals. Before each harvest, escrow its replacements. Sapling drops are probabilistic: protect reserves, collect drops without shears, pause harvesting on shortage, and acquire additional stock through ordinary gathering.

Bone meal is optional: newest strip first, maximum **four uses per visit**, stop on growth. Preserve the existing park’s four-meal allocation. Zero meal must never stall rotation.

3. **Scheduling and outside replants**

Extend `groundskeeper.mjs::keeperWanted` and `keeperOrderPure`, and `skills.mjs::keeperOrder`, so forest due work acquires and retains groundskeeper service independently of finite projects.

In `buildTidyVisit`, dispatch a resumable forest visit: **120 seconds every ten minutes while actionable**, with cleanup/replant recovery before new felling. Persist due time across keeper handoffs. Report missed service after 15 minutes when an eligible keeper is available.

Add proposed `managedforest.mjs::{siteForest, forestDue, forestStand, advanceStrip}` and `skills.mjs::forestVisit`. Follow `cropHarvest`’s `cropStand → e.walkTo → reach check → e.dig → confirmation` pattern; use `buildrun.mjs::runVisit` for recorded earthwork and stairs. Do not assume `digReachFrom` alone proves reach: it tolerates missing feet and includes a margin.

Change `tidy.mjs::replantCells` and `siteStage` outband handling to allocate **durable forest-cell claims before felling**. Link source tree, destination cell, species, and cycle. No two debts share a planting; rotation’s own replacement cannot simultaneously discharge an outside-tree debt. If no compatible slot exists, defer elective felling or establish another approved strip.

Extend `skills.mjs::gkSweep` / `gksweep.mjs::sweepPick` with authorized forest boxes for drop collection.

4. **Reservations**

Separate siting exclusion from action authorization.

Emit explicit three-dimensional `zones` through `shop.mjs::recordZones`, approximately **G−3 through G+12 per strip**, extending only for recorded infrastructure. `skills.mjs::projectSiteNow` must therefore allow coal 20 blocks below, while protecting soil and supports.

Keep paths, soil, lights, and unrelated projects protected. Permit only the current lease holder’s exact manifested operation, block, generation, and phase. Apply this narrowly through `shopFootprintRefresh`, `footprintCost`, and dig guards; never disable the whole footprint or other safety checks. Walking must not excavate the forest opportunistically.

5. **Retiring the interior farm**

Publish a retirement record referencing the old generation; stop `buildTreeFarm` planting/harvesting and preserve reservations until ownership transfers.

Bots remove all enumerated trees—including previously waived floating logs—recover saplings and torches, remove temporary access, and retain sound fill. Repair holes and finish a level surface at **y=66**, subject to surveyed connections.

Options: park extension, market square, or building plot. **Choose the park:** the approximately 17×17 cleared area supports a two-wide crossing path, four planted beds, benches, and perimeter lighting with fewer dependencies than stalls or a building. Use `park.mjs::PARK_PALETTE` and decorator provisioning. Reuse floor material beneath the finish; leave no exposed cobble patches or dirt scaffolding.

6. **Tripwire contract**

Extend the `shopread` integration consumed by `infra/showcase/showcase_trip.py::{shop_module, shop_read}`; its imported implementation was not present as a standalone source here.

Retain `_build`, `_build_visit`, and lease rows. Add `project=managedforest` and retirement identity; operations include `survey`, `transition`, `harvest`, `clear`, `place`, `use`, `defer`, with kinds distinguishing logs, leaves, grading, stairs, teardown, saplings, and bone meal.

Every mutation must identify record generation, strip/cycle, cell, expected/result block, lease, and stand. Flag unrecorded or stale work, protected/corridor incursions, unsupported reach, double planting credit, premature cleanup success, and silent service starvation. Do not blanket-allow “tree debris.”

7. **Release proof**

In Paper, prove **three complete rotations of all 18 cells**, initially without bone meal, plus:

- Every supported height, canopy obstruction, snow condition, stair ascent/descent, teardown, and replacement.
- Actual random ticking at proposed chunk boundaries and anchor absence.
- Sapling balance, collection losses, reserve protection in `composter.mjs::compostAllowance`.
- Restart during every phase; lease expiry, competing bots, unloaded chunks, inventory exhaustion.
- Continuous competing projects without forest starvation.
- Underground coal allowed; surface damage and neighboring-project digs refused.
- Full interior retirement with zero floating logs, holes, or scaffolds.
- Tripwire positive fixtures and mutants for every authorization boundary.

Principal risks are unproven reach/sightlines, stochastic sapling yield, overlapping canopies, lost ticking coverage, and cross-record races. Any failed proof blocks that behavior’s release.

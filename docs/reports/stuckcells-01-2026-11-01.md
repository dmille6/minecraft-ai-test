## The remedies

1. **The snow read.** `readBlock` carries a snow layer's properties into the visit.
2. **The sealed pocket.** In a workshop or house, a fill or base cell sealed on six full non-leaf faces counts done. This is
   zigHidden's rule. A pocket that is opened again is built normally.
3. **The waiver, inside the visit.** A placeable cell that no stand reaches, deferred 3 times (summed, this visit included), is
   waived. Only roles that may stay hidden qualify: earth fill for a workshop or house, and foundation or core for the
   ziggurat. The segment is then read again. The cell above it in its column is waived at once if it has no stand either (the
   chain). The bound is 6 per workshop or house and 12 per ziggurat. Waived cells are listed in `waived` on the record, each
   gets a `build op=waived` row, and every done rule reads the list. There is never a waiver on a complete record's repair. The
   order's stale rule lifts a stamp whose cells are done, or due their waiver and clear.
4. **A permanently blocked cell is deferred.** In a workshop or house, a cell holding a block the visit never digs (read,
   known, no foreignKind) is deferred, not `blocked` for good. The project then stamps and the town's next project goes on.
   Unread cells and failed digs keep `blocked` and are retried at once.
5. **The ziggurat footing.** The ziggurat gets the wall's footing: core blocks are placed under a cell whose ground was dug away.
6. **The stair's support chain.** A chain of up to 3 supports (`supportChain`): every click is in reach, none goes into the
   stair's air or the bot's body, and up to 3 chains are tried when the server refuses one. Every placement and every refusal
   is a named row.

# roadsite-01show, 2026-11-20: roads that cross the paths and the land grading, reach the warehouse, and light their lamps

**In one line.** On the live sink at 11-20 14:48Z, all three traffic routes came back `no_site`. The cause was that road siting
refused every column another record held. Those columns were mostly the countryside's planned grading (country@1 fills and cuts)
and the paths' cells. Roads now share those columns on terms that keep both records true. The warehouse's own road may enter the
warehouse's zone; the owner decided this on 11-20 ("Let roads enter warehouse area"). The road kit now draws the town's lanterns.

- **Replay** on the 11-20 world and pool: the head sites **all four routes**; the base sites none.
- **Paper copy:** the bots sited the village road themselves and built all 64 of its cells, the lantern included. 0 deaths.

## The rule (bots/src/roads.mjs `roadShares` / `shareOk` / `slabOk`)

- **PATHS cells.** The road's surface is the path cell: the road's level L equals the cell's y. The road lays its tread there
  (stone bricks or cobblestone, both in `PATH_PALETTE`), never a kerb, an accent or a slab. The path keeps a walkable cell that
  reads done, so the road becomes the path at that column.
- **GRADING cells (tidy@1, country@1).** I took the "road's own grading" option, not "step round", because both records then read
  done and the road keeps to the walked line.
  - Fills (`earth`/`core`) are done by any solid. Every fill must sit at or under L, because a fill above L would bury the road.
  - Cuts and clears must sit at or above L, because a cut under the road would dig its ground away.
  - A cut at L itself is allowed only if it was listed for a block the road never lays. A cut listed for cobblestone would dig
    the road's own block.
  - The road grades that column itself, within its own cut and fill limits.
  - Any other role of these records (topsoil, access stairs, lights) still refuses.
- **Everything else still refuses:** buildings, walls, the yard, every zone and every village.
- **Slabs.** A slab never goes on a path cell, or on a grading fill at its own level. The router skips a level change that would
  put one there, and `roadInstance` refuses such a layout and routes round the column.
- **Turns.** Every column of a turn's 5x5 square is checked, and the layout re-checks every paved column (this was Codex r1's
  blocker).
- **Starting beside built work and land cells.** A verge column (beside the road, never on it) may hold built work or a stem, or
  be another record's column. It is left standing with no fill, no clear and no lamp. Water and unread columns still refuse.

Other changes the replay forced:
- A 1x1 pillar (open on all four sides, dirt included) now counts as the bots' stack rather than natural ground.
- `ROADS.fillMax` goes from 2 to 4, for the bots' 3–4 deep trenches. `proveBuild` and `dropFill` decide which stands are reachable.
- A road never touches itself. The cheapest state path had looped back across the village road's first stretch.
- A blocked road ends at the point nearest its goal.
- A route's first instance now paves its start row and keeps the landing's level for `runMin` rows. Before, the row between the
  landing and the road was left as natural ground.
- `scripts/roads-offline.mjs` now uses `zoneReason`. Its old guard read village-taiga-6_1 as free ground, and the replay laid
  work-92_68 inside the village.

## The warehouse (r3: the owner's word, 11-20)

- `roads.mjs roadOpenZone`: a **project** route whose destination is the warehouse opens exactly that record's zone
  (`project:warehouse`).
- It never opens the destination's **box**. The route's box stays refused, so the road stops at the warehouse's edge.
- It never opens another zone: not T3's, not a village's, not a protected zone. It never opens anything for another route.
- `skills.mjs sinkGuardFor` (`open`) drops only a record zone of exactly that why, and only for a road. The protected zones are
  never dropped.
- A start at a corner of the ring may also leave along its line's own way out. The warehouse's start leaves the north-east corner
  northward, beside the wall extension.

## The lantern (r3–r5)

Why the kit made no lantern:
1. The road kit only ever crafted lanterns, from iron ingots above `ROAD_IRON_KEEP` (32). The town held 18 ingots, so it never
   crafted any, while its chests held **179 lanterns** (town index, 13:04Z).
2. On the showcase, the live R1 and R3 posts carry the torch fallback.
3. In the rig, the bags carried no torch and the town read was stale, so the post stayed dark.

What changed:
- The kit now draws the town's spare lanterns first (`roadLanternDraw`).
- It goes **first to the chests the index says hold them** (`indexHolders`, passed to the withdraw as `first`). In rig runs 4 and
  5, the withdraw's 12 nearest-first visits never reached the store hall.
- It crafts only what the chests cannot give. Codex r3 found a blocker: the crafted count double-subtracted the bag's lanterns.
  That is fixed in r4.

Why the given lanterns left the bags: **the bags were full** (36 of 36 slots, with clay and cane stacks), so the server's `/give`
dropped them on the ground. That was a fault in how I set up the rig, not a bag rule.

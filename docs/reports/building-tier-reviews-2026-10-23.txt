CODEX (codex exec --sandbox read-only): VERDICT: CHANGE
1. **P1 — §2.3–2.4; `structcount.py.txt::describe/rooms_of`: building count changes with incidental scaffold connections.** Two lit houses connected by cobble produce **one building**; attaching enough buried scaffold suppresses room detection for the entire component. The village control bypasses `describe`, so it misses this failure. Count buildings independently of material components, with fixtures for connected houses and a house attached to a mine stair.

2. **P1 — §2.4; `structcount.py.txt::LIGHT/World.block`: “lit” does not mean lit.** The reader discards block properties, and `LIGHT` accepts unlit lamps, candles and campfires. Its neighbourhood search also accepts lights below the floor or outside the room. An in-memory fixture reproduced the unlit-lamp false positive. Preserve states and require an active light at an explicitly permitted interior position.

3. **P1 — §2.2, §2.6, Q9: “BUILT = a bot placed it” is unsupported.** The controls already find 652 natural palette cells surviving the masks. Conversely, stamp coordinates and padded worldgen boxes erase bot replacements and additions indiscriminately. The untouched showcase proves neither attribution nor recall in inhabited worlds. Label this an estimated, observable-palette count; use records or placement provenance for bot attribution, and test additions inside masks and around their padded boundaries.

4. **P2 — §0 controls, §2.6; `scan/*.out`: the plinth control is overstated.** `found_built` accepts any palette block at a planned coordinate, without checking its material family. `isolated-d` is `state: plan` with 66 hits; `isolated-b` is `complete` with zero planned cells. These are not “10 complete wells.” Match explicit component IDs and allowed plinth materials, report actual record states, and demonstrate failures for wrong blocks, disconnected cells and empty plans.

5. **P2 — §2.5–2.8, §6: daily output is not yet a reliable canary instrument.** Fixed cron times do not establish backup completion, freshness or coverage of a +360 read. Unreadable starts are reported but do not invalidate results; the supplied negative controls include them. Require snapshot completion markers, observation timestamps, coverage/validity gates and atomic publication; show UNKNOWN for stale or incomplete data. Schedule canary reads against qualifying snapshots.

6. **P1 — §3.3: the inherited admission rule is reversed.** TP §3.2 requires the whole BOM to fit within **80% of spendable stock**. This document admits when stock covers **80% of the BOM**, allowing an underfunded start. Restore recipe-aware allocation of finished parts, intermediates, raw inputs and fuel, with the intended reserve margin. Test the boundary behaviour; source assertions cannot prove this decision.

7. **P1 — §3.2–3.3; `skills.mjs::smeltInner`: reusing the furnace session can burn forbidden fuel.** The fuel allow-list filters bag planning; existing furnace coal is not cleared before `putInput`. The existing session also handles foreign input/output and fails open when leasing is unavailable. Require verified permitted furnace contents and an acquired lease before loading, plus quantity-bounded building fuel. Test coal already in the furnace, foreign iron, unavailable lease storage and kit fuel in the bag.

8. **P1 — §3.4; `well.mjs::stockWellStacks`: `NEVER_DISPOSE` does not protect the new targeted parts.** Stock-target disposal bypasses `isWellJunk` and checks `stocktarget.isProtected` instead. Adding capped part groups can therefore discard glass, panes or cut copper despite `NEVER_DISPOSE`. Restore TP §3.3’s protected, always-bankable part contract, or explicitly redesign every disposal path. Include copper doors and fallback parts, and test a full bag when the town is at cap.

9. **P2 — §3.2, §4.6 M3–M4: interrupted production breaks the stone guarantee.** An interruption after 1–3 stone outputs cannot craft bricks, and SIGKILL cannot run `finally`. Stone remains scaffold-eligible; “KEEP during the job” neither survives restart nor excludes pathfinder consumption. Carry forward TP’s durable carry-note recovery, with protection before the next movement/order, partial-batch handling and deposit-failure tests. Three free slots must be checked against every intermediate and recovered furnace stack.

10. **P2 — §3.3, §4.2; `funding.py.txt`: funding is not recipe- or execution-exact.** The script ignores sand admission, pools non-interchangeable cobble kinds, omits fallback costs and rounds fuel across sessions. For example, 89 bricks require crafting 92, and eight smelts consume six whole planks per cold session—not an amortized fractional fuel allocation. Stations and earthwork also need funding. Generate BOMs from exact blueprint cells and executable batches, then regenerate both copper and fallback eligibility after buffer production.

11. **P1 — §4.3, §6.2, §8: isolated-world eligibility silently contradicts D2.** Town-plan §4.1 explicitly excludes isolated worlds; their five separate journals cannot provide this design’s single town allocation and lease domain. Nevertheless, four isolated worlds contribute to “14 of 16” eligible towns. Exclude them and recompute denominators, or obtain an explicit scope decision and design shared state before admitting them.

12. **P2 — §4.3; `fitb.py.txt`: the fit evidence does not establish a usable town-plan site.** The screen uses Chebyshev bands instead of the inherited outer Euclidean limit and omits sectors, paths, farm/shaft/monument reservations and complete plinth history. Its accepted sites therefore do not establish draw eligibility or furnace-drain reachability. Run the composed predicate over actual plan reservations, retain the ≤44 Euclidean bound for cells and stands, and report qualified sites separately from geometric candidates.

13. **P1 — §4.1, §5: segment fencing and concurrent mutations are underspecified.** §5 names the frozen **record generation** as the fencing token; successive holders of one segment share it. Independent segment proofs also do not serialize enclosure checks or timeout fallbacks against outstanding clicks. Specify a fresh lease epoch per acquisition, atomic project-wide builder-cap admission, and mutation/settlement serialization. Test expired holders, simultaneous final walls and a click crossing a fallback transition.

14. **P2 — §4.4–4.6: the sealed-house remedy does not survive later damage.** “No bot needs to enter again” conflicts with reopening completed projects for repair. A bot can enter through damage, after which repair must evacuate it or preserve an exit. Blanket exclusions on every movements clone also conflict with the stated survival exemptions. Define deterministic reopening/evacuation and authority-scoped escape behaviour; test damage, entry, repair and restart through the real loop.

15. **P2 — §4.3, §4.6 C1: carrying a sapling does not guarantee replanting.** No reachable, legal replant site is reserved before felling, and no durable obligation survives a crash between clearing and planting. Preflight the clear sequence and replant location against all zones and plots, persist the obligation before felling, and select its recovery deterministically. Refuse or re-site when that complete sequence is impossible.

16. **P2 — §6: tiers and exposure need an executable registration plan.** `mats-01` introduces allocation, withdrawals, crafting, furnace policy and recovery—not an established small fix with strong Paper evidence. Default it to tier 3 unless a narrower tier-2 scope is justified. Also draw at least two towns for shop-01’s two-town exposure requirement; “tier 3 pools as drawn” permits an impossible one-town run. Explicitly retain all CLAUDE.md tier-2 conditions and the 240-minute draw cooldown wherever tier 2 remains.

17. **P2 — §8 C4, §10 Q4: neither recommended wording nor promotion-first execution satisfies B6 literally.** Waiving the follower lag still builds on the bench before the showcase. Present this as a requested change to “showcase built first,” or specify a showcase-first sequence using the same candidate code and the single canary lane. Do not describe promotion-first as already satisfying B6.

Round 1 status: **fixed at design level: 1, 3, 6, 8, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17. Partially fixed: 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 13. Finding 12’s composed-site evidence remains deferred.** The saved scan totals reconcile; all nine selftests pass, and the three mutants fail their named cases. Remaining findings:

2. **P2 — §2.6; `structcount.py.txt::main`, `mutants.py.txt` — failed controls do not stop execution.** All three mutated selftests print `FAIL` but exit **0**. The mutant runner likewise only prints `SURVIVED`, and does not validate subprocess errors. Make failed selftests, surviving mutants, missing case output and subprocess failures exit nonzero; demonstrate that the daily wrapper refuses publication.

3. **P2 — §0/§2.6; `structcount.py.txt::scan` — R1 #4 is only partly fixed.** Material-family matching and record-state reporting are corrected, but `component_is_building` still finds a structure by matching size and bounding-box containment, rather than component identity. 4. **P1 — §2.5; `copyw.sh.txt` — R1 #5’s freshness gate can pass stale data.** The proposed observation timestamp is the copied region file’s mtime, but the supplied plain `cp` resets that timestamp. Even a fresh **source** region mtime does not establish freshness of every relevant chunk. 5. **P1 — §3.3/§4.2/§6.2; `funding.py.txt`, `funding-20261023.txt` — R1 #10 remains materially wrong.** The rerun still ignores sand in `admits`, uses the census’s combined cobblestone/deepslate column, and prices **stone-brick slab trim** instead of the specified plank fallback. Generate bills from explicit cells and chosen fallback roles, allocate species-specific stock and all inputs, then regenerate the results. The house wall count also needs correction: a 5×5 perimeter over two layers, minus two door cells and three panes, requires **27**, not 26, planks.

6. **P1 — §3.2/§7 MAT-FURN — R1 #7 misses already-burning fuel.** An empty fuel slot can coexist with remaining burn time from previously consumed coal. Slot checks therefore do not prove a plank-only session, and item-count accounting cannot identify that opening heat. Mineflayer exposes burn state separately from `fuelItem()`. Require a fresh, verified cold furnace before loading, or durable provenance for its remaining heat; test empty slots with an active coal burn. [Mineflayer furnace implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer/master/lib/plugins/furnace.js)

7. **P2 — §3.2/§3.4/§4.4 — R1 #9 still permits scaffold consumption after interruption.** Carry-note KEEP rules name orders, but movement exclusion applies only during build visits. `PATHFINDER_SCAFFOLD` still includes stone, stone bricks and planks, so restart travel can consume noted materials before `mats_return`. Apply durable carry protection before movement begins, remove finished building stones from ordinary scaffold selection as TP §3.3 requires, and test restart travel plus deposit failure. When stone’s bank target is full, retain the unresolved note rather than simply “banking the rest” and closing it.

8. **P1 — §4.1/§5; SHOP-COOP2 — R1 #13’s settlement barrier remains incomplete.** Waiting for other **live** holders’ last clicks does not cover a crashed/expired holder’s already-sent packet. Nor does acquiring the closer token explicitly stop ordinary holders from issuing another click after the settlement check. Logging a late placement against its old generation cannot prevent an unsafe enclosure. Add a project-wide mutation barrier honored by every sender, durable pre-send intents, and reconciliation of expired holders’ unsettled mutations before closing or changing fallbacks. Test both interleavings.

9. **P2 — §4.3; `fitb.py.txt` — R1 #12’s required composed proof has not run.** Relabeling the counts as geometric upper bounds is correct, but sectors, reservations, complete plinth history, routes and five-house packing remain untested. The `≤43` pad shortcut also does not prove a full one-cell stand ring stays within Euclidean 44 at diagonal corners. Run the actual composed predicate and construction sequence on frozen plans, checking every stand explicitly; report qualified shared towns separately before building the actuator.

10. **P2 — §4.3/§4.6 C3 — ghost-cell recovery is still advisory waiting.** An unconfirmed dig becomes unknown until a fresh server update, but “the next visit” does not cause an unchanged, already-loaded chunk to resend that cell. The project can repeatedly revisit the same poisoned cache without recovering. Specify a deterministic, bounded resurvey route that obtains fresh chunk data, with an executable fallback when that route is unavailable; test a rejected dig followed by no spontaneous block update.

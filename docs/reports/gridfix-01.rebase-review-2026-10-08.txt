I withdraw Round 1’s P2 blocker on A/C. The late-equip hazard is pre-existing; gridfix improves inventory ownership while potentially extending the timing window. Keep the remaining handoff issue on airpocket-02.

1. **Base versus gridfix**

On base `0cdb1a8`, `bots/src/craftsync.mjs:413–419` waits up to `preemptWaitMs` (2,000 ms), then merely increments `preemptTimeouts` if ownership remains. The guarded action unconditionally invokes the original method afterward (`:505–512`). There is no caller-expiry or cancellation check. C’s base `e4f2917` has the identical craftsync blob.

Airpocket’s `bots/src/airpocket.mjs:284–293` races equip against 1,500 ms, catches the timeout, and prices the dig from the currently held item. **The timeout does not cancel equip.** Thus the base already permits an outstanding equip to complete after that snapshot, potentially during a dig if admission succeeds. The 2-second bound establishes the missing guarantee; it does not mean every ordinary craft unwind takes that long.

The base also permits the guarded action to proceed while ownership remains. Its write hook (`craftsync.mjs:489–502`) lacks gridfix’s stopped-state packet refusal, so an equip that performs inventory clicks can overlap that state.

Gridfix improves those properties:

- Cleanup drains outstanding clicks and clears/resyncs the grid before release (`craftsync.mjs:408–460`).
- Preemption throws `craft_busy` if ownership persists past its extended bound (`:596–606`).
- Stopped clicks are refused before invocation, with a write-hook backstop.

However, the guarded equip still invokes after a successful wait without checking airpocket’s expired deadline (`:710–714`). Cleanup can therefore make late completion more likely or later. **Better ownership and cleanup; still an incomplete rescue handoff.**

That amplification is real, but I do not find a new failure class requiring this rebase to block. The appropriate airpocket-02 fix is to complete a bounded handoff **before issuing equip/stand**, wait for `bot.craftSync.busy()` to clear within the health budget, and abandon the step if handoff fails. Merely waiting after launching equip leaves the queued operation alive. If queuing remains part of the design, cancellation/expiry must be checked at the eventual invocation boundary.

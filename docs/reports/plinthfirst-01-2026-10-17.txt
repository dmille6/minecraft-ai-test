THE DESIGN (as built; rounds r1-r6 below)
1. WHERE: build_well's site resolution, skills.mjs townWellSite (Option A), when plinth-first is in force:
   - compute: the natural search runs READ-ONLY as the row's shadow=. The answer is "exhausted" (tally cols:0,plinth_first:1),
     and an unread cell is still a deferral, never a no-site record.
   - refuse: after wellSiteRefusal, plinthFirstRefusal decides:
     - the plan cap is buildable;
     - a cap the town replanned away from is refused;
     - a natural STARTED pit (half_dug..covered_floored) is finished, never orphaned;
     - any other natural site is refused.
   - plinthWant: under plinth-first, "no usable well and no record" is itself the trigger. It is not gated on a well kit. It is
     withheld while a natural pit is being finished, and while any chunk around home is unsent (areaLoaded).
2. EXISTING WELLS: build_well's `exists` and plinthWant's wellUsable come first, so a sealed natural well is used and nothing is
   torn down. A retired one -> plinth. The replaced site's pit goes into the new record's prev via carryPrev `extra` while it
   could trap a body (path exclusions, visitor cover).
3. RAISED: PLINTH_FIRST_MIN_RAISE = 2.
   - FINDING: plinth-02's planner takes the cheapest candidate. On ground that still seals a well that is k=0 with NOTHING placed:
     the very cell canonicalWellSite takes. That is asserted in a test.
   - From k>=2 all 8 sealing walls (g, g-1) are placed blocks, and every recorded retirement was one of them.
   - Ladder (plinthFirstMinK): a 'none' at k>=2, followed by the natural search's own exhaustion, drops the floor to 0. Under the
     policy a candidate that places nothing is never admitted (fills > 0; Codex r5).
   - 'none' younger than 24 h: the natural search has the town back, then plinth-first tries again.
4. PLINTH-02 FIXES (they apply with plinth-first off too):
   - isolated guard (above);
   - complete_lost only on EVIDENCE about the cap:
     - plinthCapLoss classifies the refusal: structural or transient. A built well's blocked stand is transient.
     - capRefusalNext keeps one episode per class in junkwell-plinth-<home>.caprefused.json. Structural counts once seen twice
       >= 10 min apart. Transient counts after >= 6 h and >= 3 sightings. Only an affirmative recovery resets an episode: the cap
       passes, or its well is read sealed (never unsure).
     - While a refusal is held: no_effect well_site_held, a 30 min per-bot backoff, no no-site record, no replan.
     - Measured cause: board-c's finished cap was refused 10-16 16:58Z and 21:19Z; its well was built there 10-17 06:53Z. Under
       plinth-02 those no-site records would have replanned a second plinth.
   - plinthOrder: no order from more than 12 below home (TOWN_V). The 5 "could not reach" visits started in mines, and three
     'unreachable' stalls void a plan.
   - none_aged needs a no-site record NEWER than the none.
   - Rows:
     - no_site rows carry shadow=, lost=, cur=<x,y,z>:<reason>;
     - _well_pit_dug carries pf=<0|1>;
     - _plinth planned carries why=trigger_<why>[_<evidence>];
     - the record carries plannedFor, plannedEvidence, minK.
   - Licence `_plinthfirst_mode phase=mode v=1 policy= shared= minraise=2 lost=evidence dy=12 w=`, once per spawn.
     This is CONFIGURATION; the effective state is pf= on each dig.
   - Revert = PLINTH_FIRST false (or env PLINTH_FIRST=0). That reverts the policy, not the fixes.

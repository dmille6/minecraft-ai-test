Reviewed read-only on `zig-offline` at `b7cc60b5`. The approved design file is absent, so I skipped it. The requested test passes **15/15**. Snapshot files are unavailable here; world-specific results could not be independently regenerated.

The **14,688 body cells, 3,780 skin cells, and 24 stair blocks** check out. The following issues prevent approval.

1. **P1 — Stub end faces escape the liquid survey.**  
   `bots/src/shaftplan.mjs:59–60` removes the end face from the shell, and `:80–81` surveys only dig and shell cells. With collar 71, water at local `(0,63,9)` yields `bottom:16, stop:null`, although opening the adjacent stub exposes that water. **Control:** moving water to the side shell at `(-1,63,8)` stops the shaft; dry stone reaches 16. Separate the safety survey boundary from the cells requiring permanent lining.

2. **P2 — Partially unread columns are accepted.**  
   `bots/src/monument.mjs:104` counts unknown cells but continues; `:160–170` never refuses `tally.unknown`. One unread cell inside the proposed structure produces `ok:true, unknown:1`. **Controls:** the fully read dry site passes, visible lava at that same cell refuses, and a wholly unread column refuses. Require complete reads of the relevant construction and clearance volume.

3. **P2 — Base-height selection causes false refusals.**  
   `bots/src/monument.mjs:186–192` chooses the cheapest height before applying hard constraints. A flat site at ground 69 with one edge column reaching 72 chooses base 70 and refuses its single cut. Base 71 satisfies the strict rules with **zero cuts and 2,303 foundation blocks, a 15.65% share**. **Control:** the unmodified flat site passes. Minimise cost among feasible heights. The strict/r64 qualification counts need rerunning afterward.

4. **P2 — Dirt pillars are classified as terrain.**  
   `bots/src/monument.mjs:110–115` accepts a natural-looking block whenever the block immediately below is solid. An eleven-block dirt pillar returns `top:80` and no debris; the identical cobble pillar returns ground 69 and eleven debris cells. This affects base height, cuts, clearance and collar eligibility. The report’s blanket pillar-clearing claim at `docs/reports/zig-stage1-z0m0-2026-10-15.md:62` exceeds the implementation.

5. **P2 — Cleared material is also credited as completed construction.**  
   `bots/scripts/zigsite.mjs:63–67` subtracts every solid plan cell, including logs and litter, from placement demand. A single log produces `clear:1` while reducing placements from 14,712 to 14,711. **Control:** the same dry site without the log needs 14,712. Credit only material actually retained after clearance. The “natural ground” description and aggregate placement estimate at report `:90–95` need correction and regeneration.

6. **P2 — Accepted water columns are not necessarily filled.**  
   `bots/src/monument.mjs:163` counts water regardless of height; `:181` fills the apron only through `baseY-2`, and `:184` excludes water from clearance. A single apron water cell at `(-1,69,23)` is accepted with `waterCols:1` but **zero foundation and apron fill**, leaving that water outside the plan. **Control:** the dry counterpart also passes with zero fill. Thus `waterColumnsFilled` and report `:59` promise an operation the scorer does not establish. Account for every accepted water cell’s replacement or drainage.

7. **P2 — Farm protection is missing from Z0 and fails open on unavailable farm data.**  
   `bots/scripts/zigsite.mjs:39` computes farm plots, but `:51` passes only composter/well records into the ziggurat’s avoidance list. Farm plots can extend to 26 from home while the apron can begin there. **Control:** a synthetic site with corner `(28,-24)` passes without a farm exclusion and refuses when plot `(26,0)` is supplied. Additionally, `:39` and `:72` turn farm errors or unknown results into an empty exclusion list. Distinguish confirmed absence from unavailable evidence and protect farm cells in both gates.

8. **P2 — “Each guard with a positive control” is not supported by the committed tests.**  
   Report `:27` overstates `bots/test/monument-plan.test.mjs`. Missing paired coverage includes lava, partial reads, foundation-share refusal, avoid overlap, collar surface boundaries, shaft unknowns/no-landing, waterlogging and falling-block accounting. The existing shaft water test covers one side-shell location only. My additional controls confirmed foundation refusal against a dry acceptance and falling-block detection against dry stone, but these protections need committed regression coverage—including the failures above.

9. **P3 — The returned plan is not globally ordered by layer.**  
   `bots/src/monument.mjs:68–75` appends every stair after the completed body. Layer order drops from 15 to 0 at index 14,688. The ordering test at `bots/test/monument-plan.test.mjs:33` filters stairs out, masking this contract violation. Merge stairs into their corresponding layers.

The relaxed rules are **honestly identified as operator choices**, including the additional **96-block exception for placebo-d**; that exception is also recorded in JSON under `rulesUsed`. The collar’s ±6 check implements the stated numeric rule, though its ground input inherits the pillar problem. Landing spacing, stub floor/ceiling inclusion, and falling-shell placement accounting passed the inspected controls. The decoder also passed synthetic solid/air/missing-chunk controls across negative coordinates.

The report should retain those explicit qualifications, withdraw the unsupported “all guards” claim, and regenerate qualification counts and construction totals after the fixes.

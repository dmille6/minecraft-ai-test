2. DESIGN
---------
Kit (skills.mjs toolkit; toolkit.mjs pure):
- The draw is townSearchNext/townSearchView/townSearchOrder (townsearch-01/02): every town container, nearest the bot (no
  journal group counts iron), each once per run (one key per double chest), re-ranked after every visit, at most
  KIT_SEARCH_VISITS 12, never inside KIT_CRAFT_RESERVE_MS 60 s of the clock; a visit that could not look inside leaves this
  bot's search 30 min (searchFailSkip, shared with withdraw_pick).
- kitTakes judges the iron before the sticks. A container short of iron is a miss PER DRAW SIZE (kit_iron for 4, kit_iron_<n>
  below; seen holding h -> a miss for every n with n + 4 > h), 60 min, ended by any deposit. A holder that lacked only the
  sticks the bag cannot make is held back from this bot's search 60 min (bot.kitNoSticks).
- Outcomes (kitSearchEnd): candidates left + progress -> `more` (no backoff, at most KIT_MORE_MAX 3 in a row); left without
  progress -> `stalled`; exhausted but the town not wholly in view -> `unseen`; else no_sticks (remedy named) / no_iron.
- The order's gate townKitIronMiss = townSearchEmpty over THIS bot's draw size, with its no-stick hold-back: the same rule.
- Rows: `_toolkit` kit rows carry town= cand= visits= vfail= maxrep= left= after credit=; NEW `_kit_search` (one per searched
  run, written when the search ends, duration = its walks: the change row); NEW `_toolkit_search_mode` licence per spawn.
Smelt (skills.mjs jobWithdraw/smeltTown; smeltjob.mjs pure):
- A copper pass (its own 2 h miss, 12 visits, ends 125 s before the clock), then with copper in hand a COAL pass, coal its
  own need with its own 2 h miss `_coal_miss` (never skipped for lacking copper; 16 visits; ends 115 s before the clock).
  Both facts noted for every opened container. No coal anywhere in view -> no copper taken (no_fuel at once).
- townCopperMiss = townSearchEmpty(raw_copper, 2 h).
- search_more (a pass cut with candidates left, with progress) and smelt_late (the smelt would start with < 95 s): no strike,
  at most 3 in a row; ONE dry-job bound across reasons -- SMELT_DRY_MAX 12 jobs ending search_more | search_stalled |
  no_fuel | smelt_late since the last success pause the job 6 h. The 6-no_fuel pause stands. The job still never burns the
  bag's own fuel.
- smelt() takes opts.deadline (smelt_town passes the skill's deadline less a 20 s bank reserve); the iron rung's smelt
  passes none and is unchanged.
- townSearchOrder takes missTtl as { need: ms } (missTtlOf); townContainerKeys is deleted (no callers).
NOT CHANGED: the model's withdraw verb (withdrawSweep), withdraw_pick's own search, deposits/reserves (another agent's area).

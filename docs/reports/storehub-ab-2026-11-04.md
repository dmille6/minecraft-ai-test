## 4. The clerk (piece B)

One bot at a time (the record's `clerk` lease, 12 min; a 60-min term then 30 min off; owner O4: one clerk). A visit (150 s, the
storehouse upkeep's budget): owed cargo first; else `storehub.mjs clerkMoves` picks ONE source -- a town-box chest the index knows (the
storehouse and its extra chests), else a hall chest's misplaced stacks -- and at most **6 stacks** (bag keeps 4 free), never cobblestone,
never a kind the bag holds (no merge, Codex review blocker 3), one destination class. The cargo intent is written **before** the take;
the take is withdraw's verified visit; then each stack to its section's home -> its own overflow -> OVERFLOW (unknown -> UNSORTED), or the
owner's listed junk (`HUB_THROWABLE` = well.mjs `OWNER_JUNK_1004` + `SCAFFOLD_DECORATIONS`, a test asserts equality) down the town
well. **Never thrown**: tools, swords, ores, raw metal, ingots, logs, planks, fuel, building stone, glass, copper, food, plants,
DECORATIONS (wool, stained glass, bricks: building material in entrusted stock), unknown items. A full hall is a backlog: what finds no
room goes back to its source (or the roomiest town chest), never to the well. Plants: a PLANTS chest with per-type caps (64 a sapling
type, 128 bamboo, 32 a flower: owner O2); over the cap the stack stays where it is (composting surplus is piece E).

Rows: `_storehub_clerk phase=visit|cargo|cargo_gone junked= src= took= sorted= item_home= more= overflow= unsorted= back= cargo_left=
skipped= ms= stop=`; the well's rows of a clerk throw start `via=clerk lost=`; `_hub_sign` (the label writer's); `_storehub_mode`.

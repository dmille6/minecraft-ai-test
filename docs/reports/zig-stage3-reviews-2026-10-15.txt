1. **P1 — Placement can occur after lease loss or abort.** `bots/src/buildbot.mjs:56–60`, `bots/src/builder.mjs:364–369`  
   The executor checks the fence, then the adapter awaits `lookAt` and four ticks before calling `placeBlock`. Body and escape checks also precede asynchronous equip work. I reproduced `placeBlock` executing after both lease loss and abort during the tick wait. **Fix:** finish preparation first, then revalidate bodies, stand/escape, abort, and fence immediately before placement. Test the real adapter with state changes during those awaits.

2. **P1 — Gather/tunnel protection is not enforced at dig time.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:1848`, `bots/src/oretunnel.mjs:192`  
   Gather checks reservations during enumeration, then collectblock can walk before digging without another structure check. The tunnel exclusion applies during planning; its executor consumes planned breaks without reevaluating that callback. A newly recorded monument can therefore be dug even after the two-second cache expires. **Fix:** add an uncached reservation check at the actual target-dig boundary, including tunnel breaks, while preserving transit/survival exemptions. Test record creation during approach and tunnel execution.

3. **P1 — Mine’s inherited walk-off cannot escape a large reservation.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:10625–10638`, `bots/src/minewell.mjs:74–82`  
   Relocation searches only 16 blocks horizontally and ±1 vertically. At the centre of an unbuilt 48×48 reservation, every candidate remains reserved; I reproduced refusal despite an unobstructed walk to the apron. Upper terraces also require larger elevation changes. The result merely advises exploration. **Fix:** derive reachable walk-off destinations from the monument boundary and terrain, execute the walk deterministically, then replan. Add centre and summit chain tests.

4. **P2 — Reserved blocks consume gather’s entire candidate allowance.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:1845–1848`  
   Filtering happens after `findBlocks(count: 32)`. Thirty-two nearby reserved blocks hide farther unreserved material within `maxDistance`, producing `nothing_found`. The alternative searches repeat this pattern. The Paper scene’s 12 reserved stone cells cannot expose it. **Fix:** exclude reservations inside enumeration, before counting results; test more than 32 reserved matches plus reachable unreserved material.

5. **P2 — Incomplete builds and truncated proofs report success.** `bots/src/builder.mjs:351`, `bots/src/builder.mjs:308`  
   `runPlacements` returns `complete` when only blocked or unloaded cells remain. Separately, `sequence(maxSteps: 1)` returns a success-shaped result for a two-cell plan. I reproduced both. **Fix:** require `done === total` for completion, return explicit blocked/unknown outcomes, and return `why: 'max_steps'` when proof work remains.

6. **P3 — Two “killed mutant” tests never mutate anything.** `bots/test/structureguard.test.mjs:207–221`  
   The gather and tunnel cases inspect unchanged source; neither applies a mutant nor demonstrates the intended assertion failing. **Fix:** apply uniquely anchored mutations and exercise behavior, especially gather enumeration and adapter timing. Label remaining source checks accurately.

======================================================================== CODEX r2 (final answer, verbatim)
1. **P1 — The final placement gate still permits entombment after asynchronous preparation.** `bots/src/builder.mjs:399–405`  
   The gate checks body overlap, but does not repeat the bystander escape check or `standOk`. I reproduced a bystander entering a dead-end tunnel during `hold()`: fresh planning rejects the placement with `body_walled`, yet the gate returns `null`, placement succeeds, and the bystander cannot exit. The builder’s own stand/escape proof also predates equip, look, and tick waits.  
   **Fix:** revalidate the current stand and before/after escape routes for current bodies inside the final gate, using fresh reads, with the fence last. Add tests for movement and world changes during preparation.

2. **P1 — Tunnel reservations still are not enforced at the dig boundary.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:1713–1720`  
   A 500 ms poll cannot guarantee “stops before the next dig.” The installed pathfinder consumes `toBreak`, awaits equip, and calls `bot.dig` without reevaluating exclusions. A reservation recorded between polls can therefore be dug. Furthermore, the watcher checks only the original `plan.breaks`, while execution replans and can choose different breaks.  
   **Fix:** add an uncached, tunnel-scoped check for the actual block immediately before each dig packet, after equip/look preparation. Preserve transit/survival exemptions. Test reservation creation between polling ticks and breaks introduced by replanning.

3. **P1 — Gather’s fresh reservation check still precedes the final look await.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:1373–1374`, `bots/src/skills.mjs:1398–1407`  
   Normal `bot.dig(block, undefined)` awaits Mineflayer’s `lookAt` before sending its packet. The explicit `beforeDig` branch likewise awaits a look after the structure check. I reproduced a record appearing during that explicit look: `collectManually` subsequently dug the now-reserved cell at `232,63,230`.  
   **Fix:** complete the look first for every gather target, then perform the fresh reservation check and other final checks, and call `bot.dig` with `forceLook: 'ignore'`. Test a record created during the look.

4. **P3 — The tunnel reservation error becomes an unhandled rejection.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:1728–1732`  
   The first `.then` already settles the outer promise through `resolve`/`reject`. The following `.then` throws the reservation error on a detached promise chain. I reproduced the caller receiving `GoalChanged` while the intended structure error surfaced separately as `unhandledRejection`.  
   **Fix:** check `reservedNow` before settling the returned promise, preferably by returning a single promise chain with cleanup in `finally`.

Validation: builder **21/21** passed; structureguard passed through the repository runner. The additional reproductions above expose gaps those tests do not cover.

# Glass windows: sand to glass to panes in the windows (2026-11-08)

Owner, 11-08: build a sand-to-glass line (gather sand, smelt it to glass and glass panes) so that cottages and other buildings
get real windows, then queue it. sandrung-01 retired the gatherer chain's sand rung, and it was KEPT on 11-08 because generic
sand gathering was a wasted attempt. So this is a capped, project-driven need, not a gather rung.

Code: `glass-01-on-1e593b9b` 7dcab440 (fleet) and `glass-01show` 36a36ce5 (showcase). The pure decisions are in
`bots/src/glass.mjs`, and the walks in `bots/src/skills.mjs` (`glassSand`, `glassGlaze`, `sandDigs`, `glazeCells`).
Rollback: `GLASS=off`.

## 2. Design (one piece, tier 2: `protected.py` says not protected)

The town materials job (`mats.mjs materialOrder`) gets two kinds. They come after its own gates (switch, town, cooldown, room,
index coverage, bank room) and before the parts:

1. **glaze.** A COMPLETE building's window cell that stands a fallback block of its own palette (planks, a trapdoor) is dug,
   and a glass pane from town stock is placed in it. The pane goes on the course below if it can, else on the wall beside it,
   else on the block above. An order glazes at most 8. If the pane does not land, the fallback goes back when the bag has one;
   otherwise the record reads damaged and its own repair visit fixes it.
2. **sand**, only while the town's unglazed windows outnumber its panes:
   - Target: BUILD_RESERVE sand 16, plus 6 for each 16-pane batch still needed. **Capped at 40.** No unglazed window means no
     sand order at all.
   - Each order digs at most 12, from **surface** sand 24-96 blocks from home (`sandCellOk`).
   - A cell is dug only if every condition holds: it is the top of its column; its stand is dry; the cell below is a solid floor
     (a one-deep hole); there is no liquid beside the cell or its stand; there is a step out (`no_exit`, the Codex r1 blocker);
     there is no water 2 or more deep within 2; and it is not in a protect zone or a workshop cell.
   - The bot stands on the sand, digs it from on top, drops into the hole and picks up the sand there. Each cell is judged again
     on arrival.
   - The cells come in one walk, each nearest the last, so the bot lowers a patch of beach rather than leaving pits.
   - What is dug is banked. A failed cell is skipped for 6 h. An order that finds nothing makes the bot wait 2 h before asking
     again.
3. The **panes** kind is unchanged (6 sand on 4 planks makes 6 glass, which makes 16 panes; stoneworks-01 keeps glass on the
   planks rung).
4. **Showcase only: the trapdoor fallback.**
   - The styled blueprints' windows become `['glass_pane', '%wood_trapdoor', '$wall']` (`glass.mjs windowPalette`; cottage@1s/f
     and townhall@1 regenerated).
   - `shop.mjs resolve` counts a wood part (a species' stairs, slab or trapdoor, which the visit crafts) as had. So a new window
     bills a trapdoor of the walls' wood when the town has no pane, and glaze swaps it later.
   - Existing records keep the palette they were sited with.
   - For cottage2-01show: use `windowPalette()` for every window role.

The reading (records plus the world, cached 60 s) is installed per spawn in `features.mjs`, with the `_glass_mode` licence row.
materialOrder's caller is protected (`cognitive.mjs`), so its arguments are fixed.

## 4. The read

The read is the generic one at +90 and +180, plus these lines:

- **DEFECT:** a canary death within 4 blocks of a `_glass op=sand` dig.
- **DEFECT:** `_glass op=glaze` placefail greater than placed on 3 or more rows.
- **REPORTED:** the sand rows (stop, got, banked, dist, refused), the glaze rows (placed), and the canary towns' glazed windows
  (a region read, as in section 1).

The goal: 74 fleet windows and 32 showcase windows in glass, with no drowning near a dig.

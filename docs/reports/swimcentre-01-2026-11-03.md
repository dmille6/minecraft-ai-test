## 4. The fix (bots/src/swimcentre.mjs; SWIMCENTRE=0 restores the old controls exactly)

When the drowning rescue or the unowned float hold rises, the body is steered to the CENTRE of the column it rises
through, with jump still held:
- route `out`: always the target column's centre (the old stroke aimed at the bot's fractional position + 1 block);
- route `up`: the head column's centre, only when a column the 0.6 hitbox overlaps is capped at the cell over the head
  row (or at head height) -- an uncapped straddle rises as before;
- the air pocket's rise: centred under the dug cell (every 100 ms in its own loop).

Centred within 0.2 the body is inside the 1.0 column on both axes. The 500 ms reflex tick decides
(`riseCentreDecision`, pure) and renews an aim (700 ms TTL); a per-physics-tick steerer writes forward and a level look
with a coast-aware brake (water inertia 0.8: 4 ticks of speed), because one 500 ms stroke at ~0.1 block/tick is a whole
block -- the ping-pong. It never touches jump. Ownership: one predicate (`centreMay`) gates both the decision and every
tick: never while the air pocket, the flooded-pocket rung, poolescape, the ramp sidestep, a danger move, an escape or
maroon climb, the in-wall exit or a stranded routine holds the body, and outside the rescue never while a skill runs.
Lava guard 2 (`holdForwardSafe`) sweeps a stroke into another column; centring inside the feet's own column enters no
new cell. A refusal, a lost target or another owner releases the aim at once.

Not a new reflex and not a new refusal: the rescue's own stroke lands in the hole it already chose.

## 7. Second survival fix: design note (ready for the next S slot)

**drop-and-dig-01: dig the air pocket standing.** Mechanism B: after apseal, 7 of 13 post-apseal drownings had the
pocket refused on the time budget; centring removes the 3 with an `out` route, leaving 4 in 31 h (~3/day). The bot
floats pinned under its own roof, so the dig is priced and performed at 25x (head in water and off the ground,
prismarine-block `digTime`); standing it is 5x (the AF scene measured 2.9 s against 14.1 s floating).
- Change (airpocket.mjs `airPocketStep`/`airPocketAdmit`, reflex `prepareAirPocket`): when the plan is OK and a solid
  floor is within 2 cells below the feet, release jump, wait up to 1.5 s for `onGround`, re-price standing, dig, then
  rise (centred, this change). Admission prices the standing dig plus the sink time only in that case; a floor deeper
  than 2 or none keeps today's floating price. The roof stays within reach (eye 1.62 over a floor 2 down is 2.4 below
  the roof cell; reach 4.5).
- Refusal chain: a sink that never lands (no floor contact in 1.5 s) falls back to today's floating dig, if that still
  fits the budget; never a new dead end.
- Tests: pure `standingPlan({floorDy, onGround, digStanding, digFloating, health})`; the refusal chain (no floor ->
  floating; floor -> standing admits at 18-20 HP where floating refused). Paper: AF (floor at -1) and a new AFD (floor
  at -2) against base; the post-apseal sites hive-b-Delta 907,52,447 and placebo-d-Comet 202,34,188 as failmine cuts.
- Expected: ~1.5-3 deaths/day. Unknown: the backup columns show water at dy -1 under all 4 sites, so the floor depth
  is 2+ there and must be read from the region copies before building.
- Also ready to measure, not to build: **falls** (53 of 174, 30%): the `_fall_path` rows say the pathfinder's own plan
  had no drop (max 0-1.3 with mdd=6) when the bot fell 11-33 blocks, so the floor under a planned walk node was not
  there. The first step is a read of those nodes against the region copies (a client/server world mismatch, a dug or
  fallen floor, or an unloaded edge), not a fix.

# shop-01 step 1: the siting planner on the fleet's frozen town plans (2026-10-25)

The building tier's design (docs/reports/building-tier-2026-10-23.md, section 4.3) makes this the first step of shop-01:
"the runtime planner's run on frozen plans ... before any actuator code, reported with both reviews". This is that run. No
actuator code exists yet; nothing here runs in a bot.

## What was run

  (the design's 167 placements), `bots/scripts/buildsite-offline.mjs` (the offline runner), `bots/test/buildsite.test.mjs`.
  Branch `shop-on-d55ce14`, on the fleet build d55ce14d.

  `scripts/host/townplan_blockdump.py` dumped home ±50 at y home−16..home+32 (`data/dumpall.sh.txt`). None of the 16 dumps
  has a missing chunk.
- **Plans:** a copy of each pool's state directory from 10.0.0.31 (/var/lib/mcai/_pool-<world>). Each plan head is
  planstore's own walk; no generation was rejected. Also from the copies: protected-zones.json, the plinth well record with
  its `prev[]`, and the tree-farm record. Z0/M0 come from zig-z0m0-2026-10-15.json.
- **Results:** `data/siting-run-20261025.jsonl` (one row per town, with every refusal counted) and `data/<world>.record.json`
  (the 12 fitted records).

## Named deviations from the design text (for review)

- **D1: the sector when the pavilion is unsited.** Design 4.3 sites the workshop "in the pavilion's sector". 7 of 16 frozen
  plans left the pavilion unsited. Its 35-wide extent met the bench fixture zone, or no sector was free for it. The planner
  then tries every sector no other outer plot holds (well, farm, gate, shaft, monument, hall), in ring@1's own order
  (`workshopSectors`). The workshop's 11x9 footprint still meets every zone and reservation test cell by cell. Without D1, 7
  towns cannot build at all; with it, 5 of the 7 have a site.
- **D2: the floor is any full solid, found by a level search, not a "ground" read.** Design 4.2 says "g0 is the most common
  ground level". Neither townplan's `groundY` (the top solid with room above) nor structcount's `natural_top` survives
  today's towns. Both read floating scaffold as ground (the columns above). So, for each floor level L in home ±12, each pad
  column must have a full solid at L (placed or natural: blocks do not fall), or a fill of at most 3 down to one. The volume
  L+1..L+4 is then cleared:
  - natural solids there are a CUT (at most 3 per column);
  - any other solid is SCAFFOLD litter;
  - logs and leaves are trees;
  - bot furniture refuses the level.

  The design's limits stand (fill ≤ 40, cut ≤ 30, litter ≤ 40). The level with the least fill + cut + litter wins, then the
  one nearest home's y. The pad sits at s = L + 1. When no level is feasible, the reason reported is the one at the pad's
  most common top-floor level.
- **D3: the walk starts from the whole plaza.** A bot in town stands anywhere in it. The walk is seeded from every walkable
  cell within Chebyshev 8 of home and home.y ±4, not from home's own cell, which is boxed in on board-a, hive-a and
  isolated-c (a block directly over it). Steps are ±1 with head room, so a reached stand walks back to a plaza cell. This
  proxy never digs or scaffolds, which a real pathfinder does: it is conservative.
- **D4: the blueprint's free choices.** The design gives counts, not cells. The front ring gap and the back ring gap are both
  at the middle column (u 4), making 26. The doorway is (u 4, v 1) at s+1 and s+2. The panes are at s+2 in the middle of the
  back wall's two halves and of both ends. The back row is torch, furnace, table, furnace, torch on the floor, and the
  standing row is in front of it. The roof edge is the 20-cell ring over the walls, and the inner roof is 15 cells.
- **D5: the fill is proven.** The fill (cobblestone, bottom up) is a segment `earth` before `pad`, proven like any other.

## What the proof is, and what it is not

For each segment in the blueprint's order (earth, pad, ring, the four walls, roof edge, roof inner, fit-out), a cell is
placed only when all of these hold:
- a neighbour is solid now (the support);
- a stand is walked to from the plaza, with this segment's cells still to place as obstacles and never floors (placed cells
  are walls and floors). The walk is recomputed only when nothing has a step and something was placed since; a builder
  walks onto the pad it has laid;
- the eye (feet + 1.62) is within 4.5 of the face centre, on its open side, with a clear sight line (sampled every 0.1);
- the stand is in the domain;
- a torch never takes the face above.

**Shown to fail:** a moat 11 deep around the plaza leaves every candidate screened and none proven (`no_step`). A ditch 1
deep is walked through (the positive control). On the real worlds, 5 isolated-d candidates failed the proof before the
sixth passed.

**Not proven here (the actuator's and Paper's):**
- the clears themselves (dig reach and order; the whole-tree reach rule is the screen's, y ≤ s+6 within 3 of the footprint);
- bodies;
- ghost blocks and the resync probe;
- the lease and barrier protocol;
- door state (the workshop has no door);
- the room test with a lit light;
- whether a sapling grows at its reserved cell (7 air above is the screen's clearance);
- the pathfinder's own route (D3's proxy is stricter than the pathfinder).

## Tests

`bots/test/buildsite.test.mjs`: 35 behaviour tests over a fake world, each refusal with its positive control.
- **Blueprint:** counts, the doorway, the gaps.
- **Flat site:** domain, reach, the doorway faces home, segment order.
- **Proof failure:** the moat.
- **Screen refusals:** chest, water, holes, earthwork over 40, litter vs cut, a path, a zone, a well, the farm, Z0, M0, sector,
  domain.
- **Trees:** whole-tree felling, the replant cell's band and state, no soil, a 2x2 trunk, dark oak, a tree too tall.
- **Steps:** torch faces, the sight line, a planned cell is never a floor, the plaza seeds.
- **D1:** its three cases.

Five anchored mutants are each killed by their test: the reach check, the floor rule, the earthwork total, the replant gate
and D1. eslint no-undef is clean on the three files.

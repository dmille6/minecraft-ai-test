# shop-01 step 1: the siting planner on the fleet's frozen town plans (2026-10-25)

The building tier's design (docs/reports/building-tier-2026-10-23.md, section 4.3) makes this the first step of shop-01:
"the runtime planner's run on frozen plans ... before any actuator code, reported with both reviews". This is that run. No
actuator code exists yet; nothing here runs in a bot.

## What was run

- **Code:** `bots/src/buildsite.mjs` (pure: a `read(x, y, z)` and the plan head), `bots/src/blueprints/workshop@1.json`
  (the design's 167 placements), `bots/scripts/buildsite-offline.mjs` (the offline runner), `bots/test/buildsite.test.mjs`.
  Branch `shop-on-d55ce14`, on the fleet build d55ce14d.
- **World:** read-only `cp` copies of each bench world's region files around home, taken on 10.0.0.30 at 10-25 15:53Z.
  `scripts/host/townplan_blockdump.py` dumped home ±50 at y home−16..home+32 (`data/dumpall.sh.txt`). None of the 16 dumps
  has a missing chunk.
- **Plans and records:** a copy of each pool's state directory from 10.0.0.31 (/var/lib/mcai/_pool-<world>).
  - Each plan head is planstore's own walk; no generation was rejected.
  - From the same copies: protected-zones.json; the plinth well record (plan steps, cap and stair, and every `prev[]`
    column); the newest junk-well site; the newest tree-farm generation; the town index's chest entries (the walk's seeds,
    D3).
  - Z0/M0 come from zig-z0m0-2026-10-15.json.
  - Every file is read fail-closed: a file that cannot be read or parsed refuses the town (`records_unreadable`), and only a
    missing file reads as absent.
- **Results:** `data/siting-run-20261025.jsonl` (one row per town, with every refusal counted) and `data/<world>.record.json`
  (the 11 fitted records).

## Named deviations from the design text (for review)

- **D1: the sector when the pavilion is unsited.** Design 4.3 sites the workshop "in the pavilion's sector". 7 of 16 frozen
  plans left the pavilion unsited. Its 35-wide extent met the bench fixture zone, or no sector was free for it. The planner
  then tries every sector no other outer plot holds (well, farm, gate, shaft, monument, hall), in ring@1's own order
  (`workshopSectors`). The workshop's 11x9 footprint still meets every zone and reservation test cell by cell. Without D1, 7
  towns cannot build at all; with it, 4 of the 7 have a site.
  - Open: a free sector reserves nothing for the houses (B1 step 2), whose plots §4.3 puts either side of the gate path.
- **D2: the floor is any full cube, found by a level search, not a "ground" read.** Design 4.2 says "g0 is the most common
  ground level". Neither townplan's `groundY` (the top solid with room above) nor structcount's `natural_top` survives
  today's towns. Both read floating scaffold as ground (the columns above). So, for each floor level L in home ±12, each pad
  column must have a full cube at L, or a fill of at most 3 down to one:
  - a full cube may be placed or natural;
  - a gravity block counts only over another floor (r2: sand and gravel fall);
  - fences, walls, slabs, stairs and similar are not floors.

  The cells the fill takes and the volume L+1..L+4 are then classified:
  - bot furniture anywhere from the fill's floor up refuses the level;
  - natural cubes in the volume are a CUT (at most 3 per column);
  - any other solid is SCAFFOLD litter;
  - logs and leaves are trees.

  The design's limits stand (fill ≤ 40, cut ≤ 30, litter ≤ 40). The level with the least fill + cut + litter wins, then the
  one nearest home's y. The pad sits at s = L + 1. When no level is feasible, the reason reported is the one at the pad's
  most common top-floor level.
- **D3: the walk starts from TOWN** (r2; r1 seeded every plaza cell, which certified a site behind a moat inside the plaza).
  The seeds are:
  - home's own start cell;
  - every walkable cell beside a chest in the town index (a chest a bot has opened);
  - the stands of the plan's placed stations.

  Steps are ±1 with head room, so a reached stand walks back to a seed. Home's own cell is boxed in on board-a, hive-a and
  isolated-c (a block directly over it), and their chests' seeds reach about 11,000 nodes each. The proxy never digs or
  scaffolds, which a real pathfinder does, so it is conservative.
- **D4: the blueprint's free choices.** The design gives counts, not cells. The front ring gap and the back ring gap are both
  at the middle column (u 4), making 26. The doorway is (u 4, v 1) at s+1 and s+2. The panes are at s+2 in the middle of the
  back wall's two halves and of both ends. The back row is torch, furnace, table, furnace, torch on the floor, and the
  standing row is in front of it. The roof edge is the 20-cell ring over the walls, and the inner roof is 15 cells.
- **D5: the fill is proven.** The fill (cobblestone, bottom up) is a segment `earth` before `pad`, proven like any other.
- **D6 (r2): no replant owed for a 1-log bush or floating logs.** A jungle bush is one jungle log under oak leaves; no
  sapling of either kind replaces it. Floating logs are logs that stand on nothing but logs or leaves.

## What the proof is, and what it is not

For each segment in order (earth, pad, ring, the four walls, roof edge, roof inner, fit-out), a cell is placed only when all
of these hold:
- **The support is the builder's** (r2, builder.mjs at eb407fa0: `refUsable`, `supportsFor`): the block below or a side,
  never the face above, and never a block the click would open (doors, trapdoors, chests, barrels, shulkers, tables,
  furnaces, smokers, hoppers, lecterns).
- **The stand is walked to from town**, with this segment's cells still to place as obstacles and never floors (placed cells
  are walls and floors), on full-cube floors only. The walk is recomputed only when nothing has a step and something was
  placed since; a builder walks onto the pad it has laid.
- **The face is in reach and in view:** the eye (feet + 1.62) is within 4.5 of the face centre, on its open side, and every
  cell the segment crosses before the face is known and empty. The check is an exact voxel traversal (r2), stricter than the
  builder's 0.05 sampling.
- **The stand is in the domain.**

Then every open interior cell must be walked to.

Trees (r2): every log of a felled tree must be:
- in the plot's clear box (the footprint + 3);
- in no reservation or zone;
- within dig reach 5.0 of an eye on the pad.

**Shown to fail:**
- a moat 11 deep around the plaza leaves every candidate screened and none proven (`no_step`), while a 1-deep ditch is walked
  through (the control);
- a moat inside the plaza with no town chest outside it fails, and a chest bots used outside it passes;
- a fence ring fails, and a cobblestone ring passes;
- a doorway sealed last fails `interior_unreachable`;
- on the real worlds, isolated-b, and 5 isolated-d candidates before the sixth.

**Not proven here (the actuator's and Paper's):**
- the clear sequence itself (dig order);
- bodies;
- ghost blocks and the resync probe;
- the lease and barrier protocol;
- the room test with a lit light;
- whether a sapling grows at its reserved cell (7 air above is the screen's clearance);
- the pathfinder's own route (D3's proxy is stricter than the pathfinder).

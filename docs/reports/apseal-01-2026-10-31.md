# apseal-01: the air-pocket rescue seals wet roof sides, then digs (2026-10-31)

Survival entry, tier 3 (`bots/src/reflex.mjs`, `bots/src/airpocket.mjs`). Queued behind promptcache-01 (S after P).

## Mechanism (measured)

ES, 7 days to 10-31 22:00Z, 80 bots: 207 deaths, of which 56 were drownings, the top cause. In 48 of the 56, the
air-pocket plan (`airPocketPlan`) refused within the 400 s before the death with "water beside the roof cell would fill
the pocket" (or "... above the roof cell"). In 47 of those 48 the first refusal came at 18-20 HP, about 85 s before the
death. Across the week there were 86 such refusal episodes, and 46 of them ended in a drowning.

Positive control: in the same window, 95 air-pocket steps ran (60 success, 35 opened, 0 failed) and 30 refusals had
other reasons. The step works when it runs. The refusal was the dead end: it named no remedy the bot could perform, and
the rescue had nothing else to try.

Geometry was read from the 10-31 backup region copies (failmine `fetch`) at all 48 sites. 47 would plan with seals:
19 need one seal, 9 need two, 12 need three, 4 need four and 2 need five. The remaining site has kelp beside the roof
and still refuses. The trap at isolated-d 357,57,180 (4 deaths) is a cobblestone roof with water on three sides; its
cut is failmine `drowned-1899-isolated-d-357_57_180`.

## Change

When a side or the top of the roof cell is plain water, a bubble column or seagrass, the plan lists that cell in
`plan.seal`. The step (`sealRoof`) fills each listed cell with a non-falling block placed against the roof cell's own
face. It uses a bounded look, a synchronous `sealGate` check and `forceLook: 'ignore'`, then reads the cell back to
confirm it is solid. Only after that does it dig.

- A seal that fails stops the step before any dig.
- Admission adds 1.5 s per seal.
- The reflex refuses when the bag holds fewer non-falling placeable blocks than there are seals.
- Lava, kelp, waterlogged solids, and a roof the bot would interact with when it has a wet side all still refuse.

The variant composed with airpocketwin-01 (`9ab8f72c`) runs the seals after the window handoff. It never equips a seal
block through an open container window.

## Known gap

9 of the 47 sites have a dirt or grass roof and a bot with no shovel. Digging that by hand while floating takes about
19 s, and admission refuses it at 18-20 HP. Sinking to a floor, or placing one, so the bot digs standing would admit
them. That is the next increment if the read shows those refusals.

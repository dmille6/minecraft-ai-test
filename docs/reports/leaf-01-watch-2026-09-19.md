# leaf-01 at +90: the refusal it removes was load-bearing (read-only note, 19 Sep 14:30 UTC)

Not my canary. Read-only, no fleet action taken. Flagging because the watched line is the project's endpoint metric
and the mechanism is already legible in the read's own output.

`VERDICT NOT_YET (+90) :: deaths 0 (0.000) vs control 0.053/bh | exposure leafread.gather_runs_canary = 209 (min 100)
| WATCH: items gathered/bh`

## The change did what it intended, and the endpoint went the wrong way

leaf-01 lets a `*_leaves` neighbour count as exposing a `*_log`, because `oak_leaves.boundingBox` is `'block'` so a
trunk inside its own canopy read as buried. The buried refusal did fall, exactly as designed:

| line | canary pre -> post | control pre -> post | DiD |
|---|---|---|---|
| buried refusals / bot-h | 3.65 -> 0.87 | 3.88 -> 4.29 | **-3.18** |
| acquired logs / bot-h | 0.64 -> 0.07 | 4.25 -> 4.10 | **-0.42** |

The read's own annotation states the rule this trips: *"a fall here with NO rise in logs is the failure mode, not the
win."* That is the case here. Refusals fell by three per bot-hour and logs did not rise.

## Where the freed attempts went

Canary post conversion classes:

```
failed/no_safe_target 78 | success 45 | failed/no_path 38 | failed/unreachable 25
aborted/interrupted 11 | failed/nothing_found 8 | unknown/collect_budget 4
```

**141 failures against 45 successes.** The refusal was not hiding reachable wood. It was declining targets that the
approach cannot reach anyway, cheaply. Removing it converts a cheap refusal into an expensive failed attempt, and the
guards show the cost landing where you would expect:

- `items gathered/bh` **-63%**, against a guard band of >= -50%. This is the breach.
- `explore/bh` **+81%** -- gather fails, the bot falls back to explore.
- `blocks moved/bh` +25%, `working share` -3%, `immobile` +3.5 pp DiD with 0 newly immobile bots.

This is the same shape as the lava-guard chain: a refusal is an INPUT to the next policy, and what the next policy
does on failure is the thing that must be measured.

## Caveats, stated plainly

- **The log counts are tiny.** One acquired log on the canary post-window, nineteen pre. At that volume logs/bot-h is
  close to unmeasurable and the DiD should not carry the decision on its own. The conversion classes and the -63%
  items line rest on much larger denominators and are the stronger evidence.
- The canary pools were already 6.6x worse at logs than control BEFORE the change (0.64 vs 4.25/bot-h), which is why
  this has to be read as a difference-in-differences and never cross-sectionally.
- Deaths are clean: 0 canary against 0.053/bot-h control.

## The point for the operator

On the registered rules this is one guard breach, so it is a WATCH and the loop will ride to the next read. Worth
knowing before that read: **`items gathered/bh` is the endpoint this project is judged on**, -63% is closer to the
severe line than to the band, and the mechanism that produced it is visible rather than inferred. If it holds at the
next read, the honest reading is REVERT on the mechanism rather than KEEP on a single unbreached guard.

If the hypothesis is worth saving, the shape that would test it is a refusal that declines the target for the reason
the approach actually fails on -- `no_safe_target` is 55% of the canary's failures -- rather than removing the buried
check and letting the approach discover the same thing expensively.

# leaf-02 at +360: the breaching tripwire cannot be measuring what its note says (22 Sep 02:10 UTC)

Read-only, no fleet action. **This corrects a claim I made three hours earlier.** At +90 I reasoned that the cover
fallback was converting refusals into `no_path` failures that write persistent avoid rules, on the grounds that the
tripwire breach (+5.4 pp, now +8.0 pp) was numerically close to the cover attempts' share of gathers. That arithmetic
coincidence is not attribution, and the code rules the mechanism out.

## By construction, a cover attempt cannot produce a `no_path`

`barrenFailClass` (skills.mjs, in the canary build):

```js
if (timedOut >= barren) return 'collect_budget'
return coverRounds > 0 ? 'unreachable' : 'no_path'
```

**Any** run that attempted a covered log has `coverRounds > 0` and is therefore classified `unreachable`, never
`no_path`. The tripwire counts gather runs whose fail class is `no_path` (`leafbread.py:94`). So the guard the note
names as "supposed to hold this flat" holds it flat by reclassifying exactly the runs the change creates. **The
change's own attempts are absent from the numerator the tripwire watches.** Whatever moved that share, it was not a
cover attempt being misfiled as durable evidence.

## And the breach is concentrated in two bots that barely use the fallback

Canary post-cutoff, per bot:

| bot | no_path | unreachable | cover attempts |
|---|---|---|---|
| board-d-Delta | **83** | 16 | 3 |
| board-b-Bravo | **64** | 14 | 9 |
| board-d-Alpha | 47 | 23 | **48** |
| the other seven | 82 | 221 | 28 |
| total | 276 | 274 | 88 |

Two bots produce **53% of the `no_path` while making 14% of the cover attempts**. The bot that makes 55% of all cover
attempts (board-d-Alpha, 48) sits mid-table on `no_path`. There is no dose-response. This is the pattern that rolled
back a good deploy once before: a pool that happens to contain chronic emitters of a pre-existing failure.

## What this means for the verdict

- The **deciding line is the one to watch, and it is knife-edge**: `cover_productive_share` 6.0%, five logs banked out
  of 84 attempts, against a REVERT threshold of 5.0%. One fewer banked log (4/84 = 4.8%) reverts it. A line that turns
  on a single event is not yet a result, whichever side it lands on.
- The productivity comparison in the read is the honest frame: 6.0% against 12.4% for an ordinary log gather, with a
  counterfactual of a refusal that banks nothing. The fallback is half as good as a normal gather and better than zero.
- `logs_did` is -1.55 against a **placebo-calibrated** floor of -4.49, so the endpoint is inside the null band. That
  calibration is why leaf-02 has not reverted where leaf-01 did on a typed -0.5.
- Deaths favour the canary: 1 at 0.017/bot-h against control 0.037.
- Exposure 84 of 100, tracking to meet the floor before the extension reads at +540 and +720.

**The tripwire should not carry a revert here, and on the registration it does not: it is a WATCH.** The thing worth
fixing is the instrument's own note, which claims a mechanism its guard has already precluded. A tripwire whose stated
rationale cannot apply will eventually be read as evidence by someone in a hurry.

## My error, named

I put "five logs banked against roughly seventy-nine persistent avoid rules written" in front of the owner as a
premise before checking whether the fail classes could co-occur. They cannot. Two minutes with `barrenFailClass` would
have shown it, and the project's own rule covers this exactly: say the denominator before you say the number, and a
number that merely matches a hypothesis is not the hypothesis confirmed.

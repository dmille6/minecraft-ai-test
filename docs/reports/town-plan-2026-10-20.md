# Town plan: one layout for every town, measured first (2026-10-20, design only, r3)

OWNER 10-20 ~12:30Z: "yes, lets do this". Towns should be organized and easy for the bots to use, and look designed
("build cool structures, survive, and work together"). Approved scope: measure each town as it is, before pictures, a town
TEMPLATE plus a plan per town, a siting file the projects read, sorted deposits, a tidy-up chore, and storehouse-01 moved
from conditional to planned with item frames as section labels. No sorting hoppers. Nothing torn down. It does not
displace the current queue.

COORDINATOR 10-20 (relaying the owner): a new SHOWCASE world starts from this design (branch `showcase-1020`, ~10 bots on
the Studio brain, kept builds only, hourly timelapse from frame 0; its bots start once the plan's first code pieces are
KEPT on the bench). So this plan also covers the GREENFIELD case (§5): the siting file is written at founding, before
any chest, station or well exists, and the first canaries are the pieces a fresh town needs to grow in the right shape.
The showcase site note (`docs/reports/showcase-site-2026-10-20.md` on `showcase-1020`) had not been pushed when r2 was
written; §4.7 says how the two documents meet.

This is a plan, not a build. No bot code, no queue entry, no world touched. Everything was read read-only:

## 0. Answers first

**1. The towns are not short of storage. They are short of ORDER, and what that costs is FINDING things, not walking.**
- 348 town containers in 16 towns hold 2,956 of 9,396 slots (31%).
- 2,378 of those 2,956 slots (80%) are partial stacks. Merging each item into full stacks would free 1,634 slots (55%).
- 270 of 348 containers (78%) hold items from 3 or more groups (stone, wood, metal, fuel, ...).
- Counting a double chest once and only containers inside the town circle, the towns have 10-30 containers. Sorted as
  the stacks lie today (v1 never merges stacks), a town needs 7-14 homes plus an overflow (§6.2), so 0-18 drain over time.
- Iron ingots sit in 9-25 containers per town. The fullest one holds 16-60 of the town's 82-241.
- The searches pay for the scatter: in 24 h, **428 of 660 `toolkit` runs** failed "no town container in reach holds 1 iron
  ingot with 4 to spare (3 tried)", and **441 of 1,582 `smelt_town` runs** found "no coal in the town containers looked in".
  Every one of those towns held iron and coal in the census.
- Walks are short: 5-17% of bot-time is spent in town (median 8%), about four-fifths of it within ~4 blocks of home. Town
  chores move the bot a median 2-8 blocks (the well 17). That is a displacement estimate, not path length.

**2. How deposit picks a chest today, plainly (§1.2).**
- The automatic town deposit (a bag at 34+ slots, in town) uses the **nearest one or two town containers** within 16
  blocks, and puts each surplus stack into an **empty slot**. It never looks at what a chest already holds. So every visit
  starts a new partial stack in whatever chest is closest. It also never banks iron or coal: the iron ladder keeps them.
- The model's `deposit` verb uses the **nearest chest or barrel within 48 blocks of where the bot stands**, and tops up
  partial stacks in that one chest. It is the path that brings iron and coal home.
  - 201 of 406 deposits in 24 h failed "No path to the goal".
  - The target was a town chest in 149 of the 202 failures that name one.
  - The bot was outside town in all 203, a median 30 blocks out.

**3. The core is full. A separate 9x9 storehouse fits inside the town-storage circle in 0 of 16 towns.**
- The containers sit on or beside the 13x13 home platform: 7-31 of each town's 11-35.
- Around them stand 19-75 crafting tables and 6-23 furnaces at town height: 769 and 176 in all, with 543 and 758 more
  deep.
- So the retrofit storehouse is **the chests already on the platform, given group homes and labels** (§6). A roofed
  storehouse building is the greenfield pattern (§5); in the bench towns it is an owner decision (O3).

**4. The template, "ring@1", is concentric (§3).**
- The home platform is at the centre, with the storehouse and workshop beside it.
- The well sits at the edge of the no-dig zone. The farm and the shaft are in the ring beyond.
- The monument, pavilion and hall plots are outside 22.
- An expansion gate faces the frontier.
- Lit 2-wide paths run out from the platform.

**5. The siting file (§4)** is one per town: `townplan-<home>.g<N>.json`, full snapshots, compare-and-swap generations
written with `link()`. It is usable before anything exists. It REPLACES the `townsites-<home>` file the TP design 3.4
proposed, so there is one siting file, not two.

**6. What sorted deposits can and cannot do.**
- `town_deposit` never banks iron or coal, so the metal and fuel homes fill only from the `deposit` verb (125 successes a
  day fleet-wide), from `smelt_town`'s copper bank, and from the tidy-up chore.
- **The fix for kit iron and job coal is the SEARCH,** not the sorting:
  - toolkit-03 (queued: every-container search) is a hard prerequisite;
  - `indexsearch-01` makes that search open the containers the index says hold iron or coal first. It is the earliest
    measurable win: 428 kit_no_iron failures a day.
- **Tidy-up** is what moves the existing iron and coal into one place.
- Sorted deposits keep new stock from scattering.

**7. The first canaries are the pieces a fresh town needs to grow in the right shape** (§9):
1. `townindex-01`: count every item in every container opened; observe-only.
2. `townplan-01`: the plan file and planner.
3. `found-01`: the first containers and stations go into their plots, including a town's very first chest.
4. `sortdeposit-01`.

`indexsearch-01` runs alongside. After them come `farhome-01`, `drainscope-01`, `workshop-01`, `tidy-01`, `paths-01`, and
`storehouse-01` (after pavilion-01 proves the roof).

**The showcase gate is a Paper founding scene plus a bench no-harm read, not a KEEP** (O9). That asks the owner to waive
"kept builds only" for three pieces. A KEEP on the bench would take days per piece, because founding events are rare
there.

**8. Item frames cannot be built today** (§1.3, O2).
- There is no leather in any town container, and only 6 leather in 3 bags fleet-wide. A frame needs 1.
- Signs (planks and a stick) are the recommended v1 label, with frames as an upgrade.

### 1.2 How deposit and the searches work today (bots/src at origin/main 48fb1a67)

**`town_deposit`** (skills.mjs `townDeposit`, `townContainers` ~7001-7030, `bankInto`; the deterministic order at 34+
slots in town, hygiene.mjs `TRIGGER_SLOTS`):
1. Candidates are the town containers within `STORAGE_REACH` = 16 of the bot (3D) and inside the town (16 h / 12 v of
   home).
2. They are taken **nearest first**, at most `TD_MAX_CONTAINERS` = 2. Skipped: full, unavailable, unusable or backed off
   (town memory), a solid lid, and the second half of a double chest already listed.
3. The plan (towndeposit.mjs `townDepositPlan`) banks whole bag slots, smallest first, above the keeps:
   - the stockpile and the scaffold;
   - tools;
   - the goal;
   - **the iron ladder: raw iron, ingots, nuggets, ores, coal, charcoal** (towndeposit.mjs `IRON_LADDER`). These are never
     banked by this order.
4. `bankInto` moves each stack into an **EMPTY container slot**. Merging was rejected on purpose: a stack whose components
   differ swaps on the server while the client predicts a merge (towndeposit.mjs ~201-208). `verifyTown` re-opens the
   container and credits a step only if the destination slot holds exactly that stack.
- **It never asks what a container holds.** Each deposit is a new partial stack in the nearest chest.
- With **no** town container in reach it returns no_effect.

**`deposit`** (skills.mjs `deposit` ~2633; the model's verb, and the bank step inside `smelt_town`):
1. With `preferAt` (a recovery's new chest, a job's chest), that container comes first.
2. Otherwise `findBlock` takes the **nearest** chest, barrel or trapped chest **within 48 of where the bot stands**: not
   deep (|dy| <= 12 from home), not skipped, and in town if the plan is cobble only.
3. With none in 48, it walks home through `home` (with its route repairs) and looks again.
4. With none even at home it fails `nothing_found` (~2734). **A town with no container never gets a first one from this
   path.**
5. mineflayer's `transfer` fills non-full stacks of the item in that one container first, then empty slots (checked in
   the harness's mineflayer, lib/plugins/inventory.js `clickDest`).
6. A full or blocked chest goes to the full-town recovery. That anchors on the failed chest and places at most 4 new
   chests a day, never beside another chest (chestfull.mjs `chestSiteRefusal`: "they would join into a double chest").

**The searches:**

| who | how it picks containers | limit | what it skips |
|---|---|---|---|
| `withdraw_pick` | `townSearchOrder` (withdrawpick.mjs): containers the count says hold a usable pick, then unknown ones, nearest | every container | misses for 60 min |
| model `withdraw` | `withdrawSweep(bot)` nearest within 48 of the bot (~10253) | 3 visits | travel backoffs |
| `toolkit` | `withdrawSweep(bot, {town: true})`: **nearest to home** (~10144), in town | 3 tried; one container must hold the ingots + 4 spare | containers missed for kit iron |
| `smelt_town` (`jobWithdraw`) | `withdrawSweep` in town | 3 | **when copper is asked for, containers remembered (2 h) as holding no copper**, i.e. the coal-only chests; coal is taken only from a container with copper or once copper is in hand (~11508-11528) |

- The toolkit also takes sticks only from the iron's own container, or makes them from the bag's wood (toolkit.mjs
  `kitTakes`).
- **toolkit-03** (queued) moves the kit's iron and the job's coal onto the every-container search (`townSearchOrder`).
  - That search puts a container first (tier 0) only when the stock journal holds a count for the need, and iron and
    coal have no journal group. So even with toolkit-03, the metal home is one unknown among all, taken nearest first.
  - `indexsearch-01` (§7.7) gives the search the index's iron and coal counts.
- **A metal-only or coal-only home makes the two OLD searches worse, not better:**
  - the coal home would be copper-missed;
  - the metal home would have no sticks, and `kitTakes` refuses iron from a container without sticks when the bag has no
    wood.
  - So toolkit-03, with sticks drawn independently of the iron's container, is a prerequisite of sortdeposit-01 (§8 rows
    K1, K2).

## 2. Prior art (searched 10-20 by web search and repository search; sources with URLs in the reviews file, round 0)

**GDMC** (Salge et al. 2018; judges' notes 2021; 2022 winner van der Staaij et al.).
- Judged on adaptability (fit the terrain without levelling it), functionality (an avatar can move and reach resources),
  narrative and aesthetics.
- Judges preferred clear zones, a centrepiece at the core, and paths with little terraforming.
- The winner grows roads from the flattest spot and zones plots by neighbour type. A 2022 entry grows plots breadth-first
  from existing buildings at a similar height.
- **Applies:** functional zones, a core, plots grown outward, minimal terraforming.
- **Does not apply:** every entry places blocks instantly through a world-editing API, with no inventory, travel or
  material. Here a bot carries and places each block, world edits are forbidden, and nothing is torn down. So the
  template must be cheap and resumable: paths are a shovel's use, and storage starts as the chests that exist.

**Vanilla villages** (jigsaw structures).
- A centre, then streets, then houses, with typed connectors and fallbacks. The extent is defined by job blocks and beds.
- **Applies:** the plan as a graph of plots joined by paths, filled when needed.

**Copper golem** (Java 1.21.9).
- Moves items from a copper chest into a chest that is empty or already holds the item: the game's own "deposit where it
  lives" rule.
- **Not usable:** the server is Paper 1.21.8 (PAPER-BUILD.txt), and it would move items outside the count journal.

**RimWorld / Dwarf Fortress stockpiles.**
- Filters and priorities, idle haulers move stored items to a better pile, small piles beside the workbench.
- **Applies:** the tidy-up chore, and the workshop beside the storehouse.

**Player storage conventions.**
- Categories, an overflow chest, labels, and the most-used chests by the entrance.
- **Applies:** the groups, the overflow, and metal/fuel first.

**Bot projects** (mineflayer-collectblock, Voyager, mindcraft, Project Sid, MineLand, Baritone).
- Deposits go to a listed chest or a chest at a position. The search found no open-source mineflayer project that sorts
  storage by category.
- Project Sid reports agents lack the spatial reasoning to build structures.
- **Applies:** the layout comes from code (a deterministic planner and a file), not from the model.

**Mechanics** (minecraft.wiki):
- A chest opens unless a redstone conductor is directly above it; barrels always open, and two adjacent chests join.
- Signs and item frames are not conductors.
- Torches cannot stand on `dirt_path`.
- A sorter needs a comparator, which needs nether quartz.

## 4. The siting file and the town index

### 4.1 Name, place, protocol (compare-and-swap)

- **Name:** `townplan-<hx>_<hy>_<hz>.g<N>.json`, in the town's state directory (`/var/lib/mcai/_pool-<pool>/`, beside
  `junkwell-plinth-*.json` and `treefarm-*.g*.json`).
- **Every generation is a FULL snapshot.** N+1 is built from N and carries `prev: N` and `prevSha` (sha256 of N's
  bytes). It is created with `link()`, which fails if N+1 exists (one writer per N; never pruned).
- **The current plan** is found by walking back from the highest generation file on disk:
  - a generation is VALID when it parses, its `prev` names a valid generation, its `prevSha` matches that file's bytes,
    and it keeps every cell its predecessor marks `placed` or `building`;
  - the current plan is the highest valid generation;
  - an invalid file is skipped, never deleted.
- **A writer's cycle:**
  1. find the current plan V;
  2. make its change on V;
  3. link generation `max(N on disk) + 1`, with `prev: V` and `prevSha` = sha256(V);
  4. on EEXIST, recompute V and rebase (at most 3 tries, then give up this visit: §8 P4).

  An invalid generation therefore never blocks later writes (the next number is always max + 1), and a stale writer's
  file fails `prevSha` and is skipped.
- **"Lower generation wins an overlap"** (TP 3.4) means: a claim present in V wins over a conflicting claim first written
  later, and the later writer must rebase or drop its claim.
- **Placed and built cells are immutable.** A generation that drops or moves a cell marked `placed` (a container or
  station that exists) or belonging to a plot in state `building`/`complete` is invalid (above).
- A unit test and a Paper scene cover: an invalid generation followed by a successful write; a stale writer; two
  writers racing one number.
- **Fencing:** a project mutates the world only while holding its project lease (TP 3.4: 4 min, renewed per placement),
  whose generation is checked before each placement. The plan generation it claimed under is recorded in the lease.
- **A file from another world (`world` differs) is absent.** A reader that does not know `v` treats the plan as absent and
  behaves as today: that is the rollback (`TOWNPLAN=off`).
- **Isolated worlds:** no plan in v1 (TP D2). Their town state is per bot (§1.3).
- **One siting file:** this file is the TP design's "townsites" lease file. Adopters (plinth, the town floor, recovery
  placement, tree farm) read it before any inner-domain construction. A structure that keeps its own record (plinth,
  tree farm, monument, shaft) is referenced by `ref`, never copied. Its own file stays authoritative for its cells, and
  the planner treats those cells (+1) as occupied, re-reading the record on every generation.

### 4.2 The town index (townindex-01; the counting the plan reads)

The stock journal counts only the stock-target groups (cobble, logs, planks, sticks, stone, dirt and the rest, a few
tools). Iron, copper and coal are not targets (stocktarget.mjs), so today **nothing knows which container holds iron or
coal**. The plan needs that, so a separate index comes first:
- **What:** every time a bot opens a TOWN container (any skill: deposit, withdraw, count_town, town_deposit, smelt
  jobs), it records what the SERVER sent at the open: per item name the count, plus the number of used and empty slots.
  This happens at the open only, never from the client's prediction after its own clicks.
  - Any mutation by the bot marks the entry `dirty` until a re-open. `verifyTown` re-opens; the `deposit` verb does not,
    so its entries stay dirty until the next open.
  - A double chest is one container, keyed by its lower (x, z) half, and the entry lists both halves' keys, so it maps
    onto the per-half keys town memory and the cobble journal use.
- **Kept separate from stock counts on purpose.** The index never feeds cobblecap, stock targets, prune or disposal.
  Adding names to the `stockCounts` record would make absent groups "a known 0" and could create disposal targets.
- **States:**
  - `known` (counted, with its age);
  - `stale` (older than 6 h);
  - `dirty` (a lost window after a mutation; cobbleObserve's `dirtyIfLost` rule);
  - `unknown` (never opened since the index began).
- **count_town** adds index-unknown town containers to its candidates, bounded as today (3 per visit).
- **File:** `town-index-<home>.jsonl`, append-only, compacted like the cobble journal's checkpoint.
- **No reader in this canary,** so it is observe-only.
- **Rows:** no new ES fields (an unmapped field rejects whole documents). The later reads join rows to index entries by
  bot, time and container:
  - `town_deposit` rows already list each container tried (`tried[].at`);
  - the `deposit` verb's success detail gains a short `at x,y,z` (inside the 300-character cap).

  Both arms carry these once townindex-01 is PROMOTED, which is why sortdeposit-01 builds on the promotion, not on the
  canary.

### 4.5 Who writes it

- **The planner is bot code, deterministic,** and works in any world.
- **Every bot computes the same plan** from the same reads. The first bot at town with every plot cell loaded links gen 1.
  Unknown cells mean "no plan yet" (TP siting rule).
- **Home is configuration,** not chosen by a bot. The planner starts from the configured home.
- **Greenfield (`mode: greenfield`).** At the first visit of a town with no plan and no town container:
  1. **The planner fits ring@1 in the four orientations.**
     - Every container and station cell must be READY: a solid, full block below, air in the cell and the cell above,
       no liquid within 1, standable from a cell that walks back home.
     - No fill and no digging are needed for a READY cell, so founding never depends on a foundation job.
     - An orientation qualifies when its first 4 storehouse section cells and 2 workshop station cells are READY.
     - It takes the qualifying orientation with the most READY cells (ties: E, S, W, N).
     - The storehouse walls, which need a pad, come later with storehouse-01 and its own fill.
  2. **With no qualifying orientation,** it falls back to an ANNEX.
     - It searches the cells at Chebyshev 7-15 (inside the 16 circle) in a fixed order (ring, then x, then z) for READY
       cells, off paths and plot reservations.
     - It takes the first 4 as `store` cells (`store.kind: annex`) and the next 2 as `work` cells.
  3. **With no READY cell at all** (all water or rock face), it writes `store: unplaced`.
     - `found_store` is not eligible, and the plan is re-fitted at each visit.
     - It is reported as `found:no_site`, and the read names the town.
  - Well, farm and outer plots get sectors on the remaining sides.
- **Retrofit (`mode: retrofit`).**
  - core = the existing platform;
  - `groups.homes` from the index (§6.1);
  - workshop = the free strip nearest the core;
  - well and farm = their records;
  - outer sectors from Z0/M0.
- **This document's per-town plans (§6) are offline previews** of the same rules, run on the census and the region
  copies. The runtime planner runs them on the index and the live world.

## 5. Greenfield: how a fresh town grows (the showcase)

At founding (timelapse frame 0) the town has a configured home and nothing else.

Today, **nothing would ever place its first chest**:
- `deposit` with no container fails `nothing_found`;
- `town_deposit` with no container returns no_effect;
- the recovery needs a first chest to anchor on.

So the founding needs its own orders (`found-01`). Every order is deterministic, bounded, and uses what bags carry: 638
chests, 178 tables, 87 furnaces fleet-wide.

| step | what appears | order (code) | trigger | material |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F0 | **the plan** (no blocks) | townplan-01 | first bot in town with the plot cells loaded | — |
| F1 | **the first container**, in the `overflow` cell (the first section cell by the door) | `found_store` (found-01) | in town, a plan, **no town container**, and the bot carries a chest or barrel | 1 carried chest/barrel |
| F2 | **a group's home container**, placed when that group's first stack is about to be deposited and the group has no home | `found_store` (found-01) | a deposit or town_deposit in town would bank a stack of a group with no home | 1 carried chest/barrel. **Section cells have their own cap** (the section count) and do not use the recovery's 4-a-day / 12-standing budget, which stays for recovery chests outside the plan |
| F3 | **crafting table and furnace in the workshop** | `found_work` (found-01) | in town, the workshop has no table (furnace), and the bot carries a spare one (more than KEEP_ONE keeps) | carried only (a fresh town has nothing banked) |
| F4 | **sorted deposits** from then on | sortdeposit-01 | every deposit in town | — |

### 7.2 `sortdeposit` (sortdeposit-01): the container is chosen by group, the slot stays empty-only

**The rule,** for each stack the existing plan banks (the keeps, iron ladder, cobble cap, stock targets and value rules are
unchanged), destinations in order:
1. **the item's group home(s)**, when the index says it has an empty slot or is unknown;
2. **another non-drain town container the index says already holds that item**;
3. **the overflow**;
4. **today's rule** (the nearest town container).

Drain containers are never steps 1-3.

**Click mechanics unchanged in v1:** whole stacks into EMPTY slots, as `bankInto` does now, verified by `verifyTown`. No
top-up. A top-up (merging into a partial stack) needs component equality and an exact remainder of 0. It is left to a
later piece with its own Paper scenes: components that differ (potions, damaged tools), concurrent change, interruption,
no empty slot.

**Visit budget:**
- at most **3** containers per `town_deposit` visit (was 2), inside the same 45 s;
- the 2 homes that take the most stacks under the rule, then the OVERFLOW for every stack left over, so the visit still
  frees the bag;
- with no overflow (hive-b), leftovers go by today's rule into the containers already opened. That container may be
  another group's home: a full bag is worse than one misplaced stack, and tidy moves the stack later. The read counts
  these as "misplaced";
- the opens are on the platform, a few blocks apart; the read reports visit duration.

**The `deposit` verb in town** uses the same destination order before its own `findBlock`. Outside town it is unchanged
(the far routing is farhome-01, §7.6).

**What it moves:**
- `town_deposit`: every group except the iron ladder (iron, coal);
- `deposit`: all groups, including metal and fuel (125 successes a day fleet-wide);
- `smelt_town`'s copper bank (via `deposit`).

**What it does not do:** fix kit iron or job coal. That is toolkit-03, a prerequisite, plus tidy for the existing stock.

**Without a plan, or with `TOWNPLAN=off`,** every deposit is today's.

### 7.3 `tidy` (tidy-01): the idle tidy-up chore

A deterministic town order like `town_deposit`, never the model's choice.

**v1 moves only groups with no cap and no stock target:** metal, fuel, valuable, farm, build, misc. Stone, wood, earth and
tools are excluded:
- cobblecap and the stock targets judge a deposit as a new bank, so a relocated stack could be refused at the cap and end
  at the well;
- dirt, sand and gravel targets are 0, and tools have targets.

**Eligible when all hold:**
- in town (d <= 8);
- no other town order eligible (town_deposit, compost, well, withdraw_pick, toolkit, smelt_town come first);
- at least 4 free bag slots after the keeps (bagbudget's allowance untouched);
- not paused, and the per-bot cooldown (20 min) has passed;
- the bot holds the town's tidy lease (one tidier per town, 4 min TTL, renewed per move).

**One visit:** at most 4 stacks, 3 containers opened, 60 s, ONE stack at a time through these phases.

**Pick:**
- The source is a drain container (or a home holding another group's stack) within 8 of the destination, with the most
  such stacks.
- The destination is that item's home, with at least one empty slot in the index, re-checked at the open.
- **The source is never a destination, and a drain container never is.**
- Only a name **the bag does not already hold** is picked, so whatever of that name is in the bag later is the carry.

**Phases**, each written to `tidy-carry-<bot>.json` (fsync) before the step it names:
1. **`intent`**: source, destination, name, the source slot and its server count n, and the visit id.
2. **`withdrawn`**: the stack moved by two clicks from the source slot into an EMPTY bag slot, the bag slot recorded.
   - It is server-confirmed (the window's slot data) before the phase is written.
   - It is not withdraw's verb: no withdraw hold, no merge into another bag stack.
3. **`deposited`**: that bag slot moved by two clicks into an EMPTY destination slot. It is `bankInto`'s move, not a
   deposit PLAN, so no keep rule (iron ladder, stockpile) applies.
4. **`closed`**: after `verifyTown`'s re-open shows the destination slot holding the stack.

**While a note is open, the carried name and count are a KEEP** for every other order:
- town_deposit, deposit, prune, dispose_well, the bag allowance, craft ingredients;
- so nothing else banks, burns, crafts or throws it.

**Return (interruption, crash, restart): `tidy_return`** runs first at the next town visit, before any other town order,
at any bag fill. It reconciles by LOOKING, not by trusting the phase:
- It opens the source and the destination, and reads the bag.
- If the stack is still in the source and the bag holds 0 of the name, nothing moved: the note is closed.
- If the bag holds the name, it moves that bag slot to the destination's empty slot, else back to the source, else the
  overflow (two clicks, then a verified re-open). A slot that grew from pickups is moved whole: extra items of that name
  land in their own home, which is where they belong.
- If the destination holds a new stack of the name matching n and the bag holds none, it was deposited: closed.
- **Otherwise the note is kept `unresolved`, never deleted.**
  - tidy pauses in that town;
  - the read lists every unresolved note with the three observations;
  - a human or a later tidy visit resolves it.
- Every crash boundary (before and after each phase write) is a Paper scene.

**Never moved:**
- an item under a live claim (withdraw holds, project allocations, cobblecap claims, toolkit draws);
- a tool;
- anything from outside town.

**Terminal state:** a visit that moves nothing is a strike; 3 strikes in 6 h pause tidy in that town for 6 h
(`paused:<reason>`, TP 3.2).

**Conservation (gate):** per visit, the carried name's bag count returns to 0, and the container deltas sum to 0 (server
re-open). Every note is `closed` within one visit or one `tidy_return`, or is listed `unresolved`. The gate trips on any
unresolved note.

**Progress (primary):** the share of each moved group's town stock that sits in its homes (index). Also, containers
emptied.

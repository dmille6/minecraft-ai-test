# pillars-01show, 2026-11-20: bots take down the lone pillars bots left outside the walls

**Owner, 11-20:** "remove them, all of them. i want a clean manicured landscape". This is about the lone bot pillars outside the
town walls on the showcase world. The vision render counted 456 of them in its frame.

**In one line.** A host survey lists every pillar outside the walls whose blocks CoreProtect proves a bot placed. Every tidy visitor
takes a lap through that list and digs each pillar top down to the ground round it. In a 40-minute rig run on a copy of the showcase, three bots took
**299 of the 1,440 listed pillars** down to the ground, 35 of them on the frozen sea or the water, with **0 deaths**.
It never stood on ice over water, and it dug nothing outside the list. The main source of the pillars is the pathfinder's own
scaffold on walks. That code is protected scope, so it is not changed here; it is reported below. One cause the take-back owned is fixed here.

## 3. The source fix (this piece) and what it cannot touch

- **`takeback.mjs` `stackedUnder` (not protected):** a receipt with another of the walk's own open receipts directly over it is not
  ripe. It waits for its top. Once the top is dug, the block under it is next (`takeOrder`). If the top closes any other way, the
  block is judged as before.
  - Measured effect, an upper bound: 104 of the 1,044 walk blocks in today's pillars.
  - Test (`takeback.test.mjs`): a tower whose top is out of sight. Before the fix, its lower block was closed `adopted built_on`. Now it waits, and both blocks come down once the top is seen. There is an anchored mutant.
- **PROTECTED, so not changed here; this needs the control session or a tier-3 canary:** the dominant source is the pathfinder's
  own towers on walks: `allow1by1towers` and the scaffold list on the walk and gather profiles in `bots/src/index.mjs` and
  `bots/src/scaffold.mjs`, used by the protected skills `explore`, `goto` and `gather`. scaftown-01's +20 placement price covers
  only 80 round home, and gather is exempt from it. 759 of the 1,695 proven pillars stand within 80, and placements per day are flat
  from 11-07 to 11-19, so the price does not stop them. Options for that change:
  - price towers outside the town too (`scaftown.mjs` is not protected, but its radius changes every bot's walk);
  - a walk that ends on a tower comes down it;
  - the take-back's 5-min expiry widened for `shape=pillar` receipts.
- Until then the lap below is the standing clean-up. A newer `pillarclean.json` the host drops in the pool directory replaces the
  bundled list without a release. That is how pillars placed after 11-20 04:15Z get listed.

## 4. The removal (bot side)

`bots/src/pillarclean.mjs` and `skills.mjs pillarcleanLap` run at the head of every tidy visit, keeper or helper, after the water lap. A lap runs at most every 5 min, or at once after a lap that dug.
- **It reuses the visit's clear machinery** (`buildrun.mjs runVisit`): a dig stand on the walk's nodes, the eye in reach and the sight clear, no scaffold, no tower, no dig to get there, 3 slots, and the crew split by pillar id.
- **The pillars nearest the bot come first,** whole pillars at a time (24 blocks a lap), each pillar **top down** (`clearKeepOrder`). The column ends at its neighbourhood's median, level with the ground round it.
- **It never stands on a listed block.**
- **The sea guard (control session 11-20: dig from the side, never stand on ice over water):** `seaFloors` puts every solid floor with a liquid under it, within 5 of a lap's pillars, into `clearNoFloor`. That covers the frozen sea's ice and a block laid on the water. So no stand and no walk node is there, and `pillarClearOk` refuses any dig while the bot's own floor is such a cell. A sea pillar is dug from the shore's ground, or it waits.
- **Each block is judged again at the dig** (`pillarVerdict`):
  - still the listed block;
  - air over it (or a listed block, taken first);
  - nothing hung on its sides;
  - no liquid against it;
  - not a record's cell or clear, not a plan plot, not a protected zone;
  - not the block under this bot or under any other body.
- **The guard** reads every guarded project's record, the bridges and the plan strictly. One unreadable record means no lap.
- **Outcomes.** A block already gone closes. A held block gets a row. A block reached in two laps and left standing is set aside for 6 h. Nothing is ever waived.
- **Rows:**
  - `_pillarclean op=survey` (`pillars_done=n/1440`, standing, take, held by why);
  - `op=dig` (cell, block, CoreProtect's placer, pillar);
  - `op=aside`;
  - `op=pass` (cleared, `pillars_cleared`, `pillars_done=n/N`).
- **Progress** is pillars level out of the list's 1,440.
- **Switch:** `PILLARCLEAN=off`.

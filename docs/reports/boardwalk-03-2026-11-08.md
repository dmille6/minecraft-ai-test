## 2. What the bots do

`bots/src/boardwalk.mjs` (pure) and `skills.mjs waterWalkVisit`. The keeper's tidy visit runs the water visit every 15
minutes, before its stage work. Doing it first, rather than in the tail of a full visit, makes it deterministic.

- **Walk line: planks in place.**
  - Each column gets one deck cell, at its lowest listed cell.
  - There is one cell per segment. Each segment waits on the cell it is reached from, one step nearer a bank.
  - runVisit's foreign dig takes the listed block out, and the click lays a plank into the same cell before the next cell
    opens.
  - The visit opens only as many deck cells as the bag holds planks.
  - A deck cell stays one while it holds the listed block, a plank, or water (a hole that was dug but not yet planked).
- **Every other listed cell is cut, far end first.**
  - The order is rooted at the bank cell nearest home, so the digger always has the line behind it.
  - An island no stand reaches is passed over.
- **Never:**
  - an unlisted cell;
  - a listed cell that now holds anything else (the dirt family counts as one);
  - a cell any project's record names;
  - anything from a stale file (older than 30 days), another pool's file, or another home's file.
- **Planks** come from the bag first. Otherwise the visit draws the town's logs under the project reserve (512 logs kept)
  and crafts them.
- **Runner changes:**
  - buildrun `p.wet`: a water cell is clear for a placement (the showcase uses its own `p.intoWater`).
  - `DUG`: a dug cell that reads water is gone.
  - buildbot's dig confirms on water.
- **Rows:** `_waterwalk op=visit ...`, and `_build project=waterwalk op=clear|place`.
- **Switches:** `BOARDWALK3=off` (nothing happens), `WALKWAYS=off` (walk lines are cut too).

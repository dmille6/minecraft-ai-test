# tlpolish-1106: showcase timelapses "as nice as possible"

Owner 11-06: "i'd like the timelapses to look as nice as possible". The samples are in
`videos/timelapse-samples-1106/`: the live frame, today's settings, polished BlueMap and Chunky for two cameras, plus
three comparison clips. Owner picks 11-07:

1. **Both.** The 15-min timelapse uses polished BlueMap, and there is a daily Chunky golden-hour beauty video.
2. **Only the hero cameras are 4K.** Two new 4K cameras (the town and the active project). The existing cameras keep
   their size, with the hi-detail distance raised to 500.
3. **Motion smoothing ("C", ffmpeg minterpolate)** for the daily and history videos. It runs at night, and a day that
   cannot finish falls back to the dissolve ("B") and logs it.

## What changed

## Measured (denominators in brackets)

- **BlueMap frame cost**, with the slot's 400% CPU quota:
  - 1920x1080 at today's settings: 44-48 s on a fresh page (3 frames).
  - 3840x2160: 100-164 s on a fresh page (5 frames); 54 s on a reused 4K page (1 frame).
  - hires 500 on the existing cameras, shoot vs EWMA (1 shoot of 6 cameras): wide pair cameras cost about +25 s each
    (topdown 51.6 -> 76.4 s, angled 40.0 -> 63.1 s). Close-ups are unchanged (iso 28.8 -> 31.5, copperhall 23.5 -> 21.2,
    town 32.3 -> 30.3, stair 22.4 -> 19.2).
- **First live slot** (03:00Z): 417 s of the 690-s budget (render 28 + capture 389). 9 frames, including project4k
  (76 s, the crop farm); hero4k was deferred by heavy_per_slot.
- **Chunky** at 1920x1080 with 12 threads on .30: about 3.5-4.5 min per 80 spp after a ~2-min load. PSNR against the
  1030-spp frame: 80 spp 34.2 dB, 160 37.4, 320 41.2, 400 42.6, 640 46.5. **320 spp (about 16 min/view)** is the pick.
- **minterpolate vs dissolve** (9-s 1440^2 clip, .30, 4 threads): 361 s vs 15 s (graded dissolve) vs 8 s (today).

## Not done / notes

- **About 8 Chunky frames per view per day:** the only world copy on .30 is the nightly pull (one state a day). So it
  is the nightly set, and the beauty history grows by one frame a day. More would need a daytime read path into the
  mirror (a permission change), which the owner said only if the copy is too stale. It is not stale for a nightly
  render.
- **Disk:** 4K frames are about 10-16 MB, at ~96 a day (two cameras alternating every 30 min), roughly 1.5 GB/day. The
  VM has 202 GB free; the 14-day full retention holds about 21 GB. The NAS sync carries it too.
- **Shader client (out of scope):** needs a licensed Java account with Microsoft login on an always-on GPU machine,
  Iris plus a shader pack, and Replay Mod or a spectator camera client.

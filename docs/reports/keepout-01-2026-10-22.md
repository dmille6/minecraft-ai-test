# keepout-01: no dig in the column above a live fire death site (build note, 2026-10-22)

Built from docs/reports/comms-design-2026-10-21.md (Codex + Claude APPROVE r7): sections 0.2, 2.3, 4.2 (`fire_site`),
4.5, 4.6 (rows 4-8), 8 row 2 and 9 (C3, C12).

## Owner decisions (both settled)
- **C3, 2026-10-22 ~00:00Z: "yes"** to the design's C1-C9. keepout-01 is fire/lava only, triggered by any live fire
  site, in the underground-safety lane, ahead of expedition-01.
- **C12 (owner item 10), 2026-10-22 ~00:05Z: "yes"** (relayed by the coordinator): a live fire column covering a town
  chest, furnace or home makes the keep-out stand down for town jobs there, and the read reports the town. This is now
  an OWNER DECISION, no longer pending. It is implemented as `KEEPOUT_TOWN_YIELD = true` in bots/src/keepout.mjs, and
  `KEEPOUT_TOWN_YIELD=0` in a bot's env flips it without a build. `_keepout_yield` names the town (pool, site, home /
  chests / furnaces) once an hour per bot, and keepoutread.py lists it.

## What it does
- **The column:** within 6 blocks horizontally of a live fire death site (weight >= 1, class `death:fire`, not
  disputed), from the site's y up to y + 12.
- **gather and mine:** refuse dig candidates in the column whatever the body's position. Only moving out is exempt:
  reflex digs are `survival`, as are `_keepout_exit`'s own escape digs.
  - gather skips column candidates at enumeration (findBlocks returns the nearest outside, within the run's radius),
    applies a target conjunction, and walks on a route that never enters a column. If nothing outside is reachable it
    ends without a dig, with failClass `keepout`, the remedy named, and a 10-min (gather, block) cooldown within 32
    of the column.
  - mine adds the column to its composed keep-out, so the whole stair is planned against it before the first dig. It
    walks off to a start outside (far starts within 32), and every stair dig is checked again. If it finds nothing it
    makes no dig, uses failClass `keepout` and sets a 10-min mine cooldown.
  - the dig guard sits on bot.dig, outside the `_dig_town` instrument: a TARGET dig in a column is refused and never
    sent.
- **A body inside a column** runs `_keepout_exit` before anything else (cognitive.mjs, last in the order chain):
  - an outward-only walk;
  - else the escape dig at or above foot level, away from the centre, with the whole step validated first;
  - else the shaft climb above the column's top.
  - A failure, or three attempts that do not get out, backs off for 5 min, and the escape ladder has the body. The
    exit is never gated by admission memory.
- **Disputes:** a lava read that finds a disc clear (every cell loaded) disputes the site. It stays on file and priced,
  but no longer keeps bots out until the next fire death there.
- **world facts writes** are serialized across a pool's processes by a pid + start-ticks lock with atomic publication
  and break-lock removal of dead holders. Measured: the unlocked read-merge-write lost 40 of 80 concurrent deaths.
- **Rows:**
  - licence `_keepout_mode`;
  - liveness `_keepout` (counters, once a minute);
  - `_keepout_exit`, `_keepout_yield`, `_keepout_dispute`;
  - the canary gate rows `_mine_plan` (planned stair cells) and `_mine_dig` (executed cells), each with `in_column`.
- **Rollback:** KEEPOUT=0.
- **homefix-02 variant:** the watchdog's level-1 relocation never targets a cell whose arrival region is in a column.

## Tier

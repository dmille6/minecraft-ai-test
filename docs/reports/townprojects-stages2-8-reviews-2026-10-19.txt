Checking before builder placements also misses a chest subsequently placed beneath an existing block. `lidrule.mjs` explicitly documents how an inaccessible counted chest can prevent complete town counts. **Fix:** exclude reserved cells and their lid-clearance volumes from chest-placement paths starting in stage 2; define deterministic recovery for pre-existing conflicts. The ordinary wall-top sequence alone is incomplete.

- **P2 — §§0.1, 1.2–1.3, 3.3, 5–6: stock and flow denominators do not support the forecasts.** A fixed-container census cannot establish whole-town net iron growth; its 39.7-hour interval also straddles toolkit-02’s promotion. Furnace-placement locations do not measure the body time that load-and-leave automation could save. **Fix:** frame deferral as provisional prioritization and specify reopening thresholds for contention, latency, errors, and smelting body time. Reconcile **126 placements versus 127 summed band entries**, and distinguish the 99 positioned observations from missing positions.

- **P2 — §§3.2, 8/O9: the lightning argument needs difficulty-specific evidence.** Zero lightning deaths measures neither structural ignition nor rod-induced risk. The [LightningBolt implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mahtomedi/minecraft/main/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/LightningBolt.java) distinguishes Normal/Hard surrounding-fire attempts from subsequent flashes and separately damages nearby entities. **Fix:** verify the actual Peaceful Paper 1.21.8 behavior, specify rod clearance from combustible blocks and occupied terrace cells, and remove the unsupported claim that attracting strikes provides a net safety benefit.

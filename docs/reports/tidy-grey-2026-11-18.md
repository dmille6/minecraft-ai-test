# The clean-up paved the showcase grey (item 8, 2026-11-18)

Branch `tidytop-01show`. It is built on readfix-01show `6f7fcf377`, which is the live release 86084711 plus the memory fix.

## What was measured

Inputs: the region at 06:30Z and record `project-0_70_0.tidy.g199.json`. Both were copied to ci2 `~/item8/`. The measurement covers the survey boxes `[-45,-19,34,38]` and `[-39,-88,34,-20]`, which hold **9,746 columns**. That is the denominator for every count below.

- **Positive control.** The tops in those columns: grass_block 4,991, cobblestone 2,798, stone 353, spruce_slab 331, dirt_path 330. This matches the control session's count.
- **Grey tops.** 3,163 columns are topped with cobblestone, cobbled_deepslate, mossy_cobblestone or stone.
- **Stricter rule.** I counted a grey top only when it is the column top, has at least 2 of its 4 neighbours topped with grass or dirt, and those neighbours are within ±1 in height.
  - 826 such tops are the record's own fill cells: **809 role `earth`** and 17 `core`. None are waived and none are named as a cut.
  - 200 are not record cells at all, so the tidy job did not lay them.
- **Looser rule (the one the code uses).** At least 1 soil neighbour, either level or one step down. This finds 1,025 record fill cells.
- **Replay of `retopCells` over the real record.** I used a reader built from cols.json, with no keep-off.
  - It re-tops **1,000** of 7,076 cells. The rest are left for these reasons: buried 2,925, not_tidy (carried or landmark) 1,015, not_grey 455, no_soil_beside 377, covered 122, waived 76.
  - One refresh, capped at 300, adds 300 cuts. The record's cuts go from 2,637 to 2,937.

## The cause

- `bots/src/tidy.mjs:1764-1768` (the base). `coreFills` gave role `core` to every fill below a column's top. It left the **top** fill as role `earth`, on the reasoning that "dirt first: it greens".
- `bots/src/tidy.mjs:47`. The palette is `TIDY_PALETTE.earth = ['dirt', 'cobblestone', 'coarse_dirt', 'cobbled_deepslate', '*solid']`. The placer (`bots/src/buildrun.mjs:371` `blockFor`) takes the first block in that list the bag holds. A bag with no spare dirt therefore laid **cobblestone at the surface**.
- `bots/src/buildrun.mjs:399`. Because the palette includes `*solid`, that cobblestone then reads as done (`cellDone`), so no later visit ever looks at the cell again.
- **Supporting evidence.** The bags held cobblestone, and the fill bill (`tidy.mjs fillWant`) draws earth fills as cobblestone from the yard. That explains the 2,293 cobblestone placements against 946 dirt over two days.

## What changed

1. **Top fills are dirt, never stone.**
   - `bots/src/tidytop.mjs` adds `fillRole` and `surfaceFills`. They give a column's top `earth` fill the role `topsoil`.
   - `TIDY_PALETTE.topsoil` is `['dirt', 'grass_block']`, with no `*solid`.
   - These are applied at the end of `coreFills` (`tidy.mjs:1771`), which covers both siting and the hourly refresh's fresh survey.
   - The buried cells keep `core`, which means cobblestone first. That still works as the cobblestone sink.
2. **What happens when the bag has no dirt.** The visit's existing topsoil handling covers it, and the test exercises the whole chain:
   - The visit already bills topsoil as **dirt drawn from the town** (`skills.mjs:30361` `wantSoil`, then `extra.dirt`).
   - A topsoil cell with no dirt in the bag is **passed** (`tidy.mjs:1162` `soilSkip` → `alsoDone`, `skills.mjs:30421`). It is not tried and not deferred, so it is never counted toward a waiver.
   - It **stays open** for the completion check (`tidyProgress`).
   - It is laid as soon as there is dirt. Dirt comes from the next draw, or from the spoil of the visit's own cuts: `alsoDone` is read per cell, so dirt dug earlier in the same visit counts.
   - No path places stone: `blockFor(topsoil, bag of cobblestone)` returns null.
3. **Grey tops already laid go back on the work list.**
   - `tidytop.mjs` adds `retopCells` and `retopWhy`. They select `earth` or `core` fills that are the column top with no open fill above, hold cobblestone, stone or deepslate, have air above, and have grass or dirt beside them.
   - Such a cell becomes `topsoil`, stamped `retop`. Its grey block then no longer reads done, so the visit digs it itself through buildrun's foreign-block pass for open record cells, and lays dirt.
   - It never selects a waived, guarded, already-cut, carried, plot, prep or landmark cell, or one in a column the survey keeps off (`keepOff`).
   - It runs inside the keeper's refresh publish (`skills.mjs`, `tidyRefresh`), takes cells nearest home first, at most `RETOP.max` = 300 per refresh, and logs the count in the refresh row's `skipped.retop`.
   - **Review fix (Codex r1 BLOCKER: dirt running out left holes and closed the record):**
     - `retopHeld` passes every re-topped grey cell beyond the dirt the bag holds at the start of the visit. Those cells are read done by `alsoDone`, so they are never dug.
     - `soilPassDone` stops the completion check from reading a cell done when it was passed for lack of dirt but still holds air or a grey block. The record therefore stays open over a hole or a grey top.
     - Behaviour test with buildfake's `runVisit`, two grey tops: 0 dirt leaves both grey; 1 dirt gives one dirt and one grey with the record still open; 2 dirt gives two dirt and the record complete.
     - Side effect: a lawn `topsoil` cell (from roughground) passed with no dirt that still holds a grey block also keeps the record open now. The gkwork empty-visit stage close is still the backstop.
4. **Grass on top cannot be placed.** A bot cannot place grass_block legitimately: it needs silk touch, or `/give`, which is forbidden. It lays dirt instead, and dirt in the light next to grass turns green on its own.
5. **Switch.** `TIDYTOP=off` restores the old roles and turns off the re-top pass.

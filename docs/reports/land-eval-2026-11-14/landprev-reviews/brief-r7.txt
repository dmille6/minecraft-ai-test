Context. bots/src/topdown.mjs decides whether a terrain cell may be a gather target from F, the bot's "feet level": `topDownCell(solidAt, p, name, F, groundAt)` scans from the cell up to F; a roof run in that span that tops out at or below F -> `roofed` (refused); a cover rising above F -> `under` (taken); no cover up to F and the cell lies below F with ground higher on two or more sides -> `pitted` (refused); made ground (farmland, dirt_path) -> `made` (always refused). The verdict is NOT monotonic in F: the previous round found a cell (dirt at (0,60,0), air at 61, ground at 62-63 over it, ground on all four sides at y 61) that is `under` (taken) at F=62 and `pitted` (refused) at F=61. So neither rounding the bot's height up (a jumping bot read one level high) nor down (the scene above) is safe when the true level is uncertain.

The fix: `feetLevels(y, onGround)` returns every level the feet may be at: [floor(y + 0.2)] while standing on a full block or one a little short of full (path, farmland, soul sand: fraction >= 0.8); [floor(y), floor(y)+1] while standing with a fraction strictly between 0 and 0.8 (a slab); [floor(y)-1, floor(y)] while `bot.entity.onGround === false` (in the air or swimming); [] when the height is unknown. `topDownCellAt(solidAt, p, name, levels, groundAt)` returns a refusal only when EVERY level refuses; if any level takes the cell it returns that level's answer (an `under` one first).

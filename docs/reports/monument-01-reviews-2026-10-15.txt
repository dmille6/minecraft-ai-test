1. **P1 — Segment leases do not serialize the escape proofs.** `bots/src/skills.mjs:7510` permits simultaneous clicks in different segments. I reproduced two individually feasible placements that preserve a bystander’s alternate exit, but jointly wall it in. Add a two-exit, two-builder race test.

2. **P1 — An unresolved cover obligation does not prevent frontier advancement.** `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:170` unconditionally counts waived cells closed; both advance paths at `bots/src/skills.mjs:7453` and `:7535` use that census without checking covers. Test stacked waivers through successive layers, including the required stopped-frontier outcome.

3. **P1 — Partial and falling blocks are accepted as full, stable footing.** `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:148`, `:151`, and `:192` use the builder’s `boundingBox === 'block'` predicate. Using the installed block library, an oak slab, oak fence and sand all classify as `degraded`; slabs and fences can also discharge cover obligations. This violates the design’s full-cube, non-falling requirement and invalidates the assumed walk geometry. **Fix:** preserve collision shapes in monument reads and use a full-cube, non-falling predicate for completion, cover/anchor decisions and footing proofs. Exercise real slab, fence and falling-block states.

I reproduced an arrival/build followed by 30 stationary minutes returning no stuck span. **Fix:** evaluate rolling stationary intervals, resetting their start on movement or building; use the design’s ten-minute threshold unless explicitly relaxed. Add arrival-then-stuck and build-then-stuck tests.

6. **P2 — The clear whitelist admits non-instant digs.** `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:141` includes cobwebs. ROUND r6 at ad2b0615
-- CODEX --
Three remaining findings, reproduced against `ad2b0615`. They hold even assuming the barrier’s world-ordering guarantee is correct.

1. **P1 — An “absent” decision survives evidence that the author is present.** `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:436,444–453`

   B’s event loop stalls before processing A’s join. A writes its record, passes its lease check, then pauses before sending its placement. After lease expiry, B resumes: `begin()` sees A absent and puts A’s record into `ok`. B’s barrier drains the queued join, making A present, but `synced()` and `acquire()` never reconsider resolution. B deletes A’s record and clicks; A can subsequently resume and click using its earlier proof.

   **Fix:** Snapshot pending records, complete a barrier, then evaluate absence against the refreshed player list. Require that snapshot to remain valid through acquisition. Add a regression where `present(A)` changes from false to true during `sync()`; acquisition must remain busy.

2. **P1 — A buffered departure from an older session can settle the current session’s pending click.** `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:434–436`

   B stalls while A disconnects and reconnects. The new A session writes a pending record and pauses before its packet. B first observes that record while its player list still contains old A, so it returns busy. B then processes the buffered old-session leave and new-session join. On its next attempt, `leftAt(A) > first` authorizes deletion of the **new session’s** record, although that session remains present and can still click. The recorded `session` is never consulted.

   **Fix:** Establish departure eligibility only after a barrier following the record snapshot has drained older membership events. For an author present then, require a subsequent departure, followed by another barrier before settlement—or use an authoritative connection-generation identity. Add the queued leave/rejoin regression; merely checking presence again does not fix this branch.

3. **P2 — Departure ordering still depends on an adjustable wall clock.** `bots/src/buildbot.mjs:43`; `bots/src/monumentjob.mjs:432–436`

   If an old departure is recorded at time 2000, the clock rolls back, and a current-session record is first observed at 1000, the second `begin()` treats the old departure as subsequent. I reproduced acquisition succeeding without any new departure while the author remained present.

   **Fix:** Use client-local event ordinals for observation/departure ordering, scoped to the observing connection. Keep wall-clock timestamps for diagnostics and lease expiry, not settlement evidence. This also avoids equal-millisecond departures being missed. The session-ordering fix above remains necessary.

On the other requested points:

- **Record-first / lease-second:** the ordering addresses the stated takeover race, provided pending-record resolution is sound.
- **Barrier ordering:** I did not establish a counterexample in the inspected Paper paths, but the supplied single-client probe does not prove the universal cross-client broadcast guarantee. Paper explicitly sends the clicked cells directly back to the clicker, so those updates preceding its ack are insufficient evidence for an observer’s barrier. Add a two-client probe with delayed observer processing. [Paper placement handling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaperMC/Paper/ver/1.21.8/paper-server/patches/sources/net/minecraft/server/network/ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.java.patch)
- **`use_item`:** the monument’s plain stone-family blocks have no ordinary air-use placement behavior. However, Paper fires interaction events for nonempty hands, so “no effect” is conditional on plugin behavior. [Paper use-item handling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaperMC/Paper/ver/1.21.8/paper-server/patches/sources/net/minecraft/server/network/ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.java.patch)
- **Remaining stall:** a present author whose barrier never answers blocks progress indefinitely. That is the intended conservative behavior; recovery requires an answered barrier or proven connection termination.

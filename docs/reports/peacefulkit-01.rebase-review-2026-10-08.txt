**P2 — A/C: airpocket equips before the interrupted furnace relinquishes inventory ownership.**

Locations: `bots/src/reflex.mjs:1414–1415`, `bots/src/airpocket.mjs:286`, `bots/src/runner.mjs:150`; furnace cleanup at A `bots/src/skills.mjs:7201` / C `bots/src/skills.mjs:7494`.

Concrete scenario: a sealed drowning rescue starts while smelt is taking back a wooden sword or draining another stack. The fastest pickaxe is outside the hotbar. `runner.interrupt()` only aborts the signal; airpocket immediately calls `equip()`, while smelt’s in-flight transfer and asynchronous `finally` can continue clicking.

The installed Mineflayer implementation makes this unsafe:

- `equip()` uses the item’s player-inventory slot numbers and calls `moveSlotItem()`.
- `clickWindow()` targets the **currently open furnace window**, whose player-slot offset differs.
- Those equip clicks can also encounter the sword or another stack already on the shared cursor. There is no inventory-operation exclusion between airpocket and furnace recovery.

Consequently, equip can move the wrong stack or interfere with recovery; the intended pickaxe may never reach the hand, causing an otherwise admitted rescue to fail its post-equip budget check. Cursor conservation and sword accounting are not established for this interleaving.

The survival-release hook at `bots/src/reflex.mjs:1491` does **not** protect smelt: it releases `inventoryUnsettled`, installed by the withdraw hold path. Smelt does not install that hold. The movement arbiter likewise does not guarantee inventory cleanup completion: its acknowledgement deadline is 500 ms, versus up to 12 seconds for furnace recovery.

This is an unsafe composition found by inspection, **not a reproduced Paper loss or double-credit**. Fix the inventory handoff before airpocket’s equip/stand operations, including cancellation of stale transfer continuations; simply waiting the full drain budget would compromise rescue timing.

I withdraw the Round 1 A/C blocking finding. The window/equip race exists on live `dbb4d78`; peacefulkit adds exposure within that existing race, but I do not find a rebase-specific regression sufficient to block A/C.

1. **The overlap already exists without peacefulkit.**

   On `dbb4d78`:
   - `bots/src/runner.mjs:150–154`: `interrupt()` aborts the controller and returns synchronously; it does not await cleanup.
   - `bots/src/reflex.mjs:1414–1415`: interruption is immediately followed by `airPocketStep()`.
   - `bots/src/airpocket.mjs:265–267`: the step calls `bot.equip()` without checking that the window has closed.
   - `bots/src/skills.mjs:7331–7332`: smelt’s `finally` awaits `drainFurnace()`.
   - `skills.mjs:7116–7130`: that drain takes output, input and fuel asynchronously, then closes the window. Its recovery budget is already **12 seconds** (`7096`).

   Thus the live build already permits rescue equip during post-abort furnace clicks. Existing takes also lift items onto the cursor through Mineflayer’s `putAway()`; cursor overlap is not introduced by swords.

   The survival release is narrower than a general window barrier: `dbb4d78:reflex.mjs:1491–1497` calls it only when `bot.inventoryUnsettled?.release` exists. An ordinary open furnace does not satisfy that merely by being open.

2. **Peacefulkit changes the exposure, but the underlying defect belongs to the airpocket handoff.**

   In A (`6f1921a`), with corresponding C (`4be1a29`) lines:
   - Staged-sword confirmation adds up to **300 ms of explicit waits before draining**, even after abort: `skills.mjs:7586–7589` / C `7879–7882`.
   - Cursor settlement adds possible recovery clicks before and after takes: A `7186–7195`, `7217`, `7241` / C `7479–7488`, `7510`, `7534`.
   - Sword take-back adds another transfer path: A `7522` / C `7815`.
   - The drain still closes after recovery: A `7249` / C `7542`; rescue equip remains at `airpocket.mjs:286` in both.

   These are real additional interleavings; saying “identical hazard with no change whatsoever” would overstate it. However, asynchronous post-abort transfers, loaded cursors and an open window were already present. The extra sword safeguards also prevent unsafe takes into a full bag and stop further takes after a stuck cursor.

   **P3 follow-up, nonblocking for this rebase:** fix the rescue/window handoff on airpocket’s line for furnace, chest and crafting windows. Before inventory-moving equip, it needs a bounded handoff that prevents outstanding cleanup clicks from continuing against a different window. Simply closing the window before equip is insufficient if old asynchronous transfers can still resume.

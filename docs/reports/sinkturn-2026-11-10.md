### Cause 1: the sink tier is below every finite project with a slot

`shop.mjs activeProject` offers a sink only when no open finite project has a slot for the bot (`freeOpen`). The showcase always
has one (the town hall; the tidy for the crew), so the tier was reached about twice a day.

### Cause 2: the tower can never close, and it heads the picker's order

`sinks.mjs sinkOrder` puts an OPEN instance first. Tower T3 (sited 11-04 09:45Z, record g6) stands with only its lights open:
six torches on the top terraces (`T3-light`, segment `T3-D`), deferred `no_stand` by Maple, Lumen, Kite, Juno (11-08/09) and Iris
(11-10). Iris placed four on 11-10 13:48Z; two remain (46,75,-14 and 46,75,-10, six deferrals each over five holders, the first
11-08 16:00Z). The sinkwaive-01 waiver skips light and fire cells, so the instance can never complete. It stays open, heads every
pick, and holds the tower kind's next instance (the status page's "Showcase tower: stuck 32 h"). Stuck stamps: Kite 11-08 17:00Z
(`stalled`), Juno 11-09; each holds the 6-h back-off only.

## The fix (smallest that moves both)

1. **The sink turn** (`sinks.mjs SINK_TURN`, `sinkTurnDue`, `finiteStreakAfter`; `shop.mjs activeProject` option `sinkTurn`;
   `skills.mjs shopActive` and `buildShopVisit`). Each bot counts its finite build visits in a row (`bot.finiteStreak`; a sink
   visit resets it). After `SINK_TURN.every` = 4, its next pick is the sink the picker offers (the existing `sink`: wanted, a
   slot free, not stuck, not idle for this bot). It comes behind the repairs and the service tier and ahead of the open finite
   projects. At most one build visit in five goes to a sink; with no sink to work, the order is unchanged. The grounds crew
   (`keeperNow`: the keeper or a helper lease) never takes the turn. The decorator's pick is already its decor. The 11-05 share
   before the sinks starved was 19% (214 of 1,127).
2. **A blocked sink yields** (`shop.mjs noteIdle`). A sink visit that changed nothing, or aborted, marks that sink idle for this bot
   for `SINK_TURN.idleMs` = 3 h (it was `IDLE_MS` = 1 h). That is longer than a turn's interval, about 75 min at the showcase's
   ~4 visits a bot an hour, so the bot's next turns go to the next sink in the picker's order. Without this, the blocked tower would
   take every turn (the mutant in the tests shows it).
3. **A torch no stand reaches is waived** (`sinks.mjs sinkWaiveDue` option `lights`). A light or fire cell is waived as a stone cell
   is (3 deferrals over every holder, the first at least 6 h old, at most 12 a record), but only when its deferral named a stand
   (`/no_stand/`). A decor flower never is. On the live counts, the next tower visit waives both torches and the visit after it
   completes T3; the picker then rotates past the tower.

`SINKTURN=off` rolls all three back (no turn; a sink's idle mark 1 h; no light waived).

### Refusals and remedies

No new refusal. A sink turn ordered in a mine meets the existing `underground` skip (sinkfix-02: asked again at the surface). A
sink that cannot be worked (`slots_full`, `off`, `zones_unknown`: `IDLE_NOT_A_VISIT`) marks nothing, and the count starts again
after the visit. A sink that is stuck, idle or full is not `sink`, so the order falls through to the finite projects as before.

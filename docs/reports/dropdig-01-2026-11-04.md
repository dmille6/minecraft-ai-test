## 2. The change

Only when the FLOATING price is refused on the health budget (the 70 working pocket steps are untouched; the pre-empt of an
in-flight escape still prices the floating worst case; the held-off row never drops):
- `dropAdmission` (pure): a full-cube, harmless floor within 2 under **every** column the 0.6-wide body overlaps, plain
  still water in the descent and at the landing eye, every target (roof + seals) within 4.5 of the standing eye; price =
  sink + extra rise (1.5 s/block) + seals + standing dig x1.5 + 3 s against the same budget. The roof cell stays absolute.
- `airPocketStep` reads `plan.drop`: release jump, sink until landed (3 still polls in the column), then the base step.
- **Ground guard** (on with DROPANDDIG): any standing-priced dig (dropped, or found standing) must settle first, is checked
  on the ground synchronously at the dig, polled every 50 ms (two polls off the floor stop it), and the server's answer is
  awaited 400 ms before jump is pressed; a roof the server put back (then or during the rise) is FAILED with the 5-s
  cooldown, never "opened". Paper 11-04 found this on the base's own found-standing path (r0 build): the bot touched the
  floor, the dig was priced standing, it lifted, mineflayer wrote optimistic air, the server kept the stone, "opened", drowned.
- A sink that never lands, a lift-off stop, or a refused standing dig: nothing dug, jump stays held, the short cooldown,
  and the cell is not dropped from again for 60 s. The next admission then refuses exactly as the base did.

## The change

Torches, and ladders below the ladder escape's minimum, become bankable. Nothing else.

- `bots/src/kitshrink.mjs` (new): KIT_BANK = torch, ladder; `kitBankDecision(name, held)` -> 'bank' | 'escape_ladders' |
  null; `LADDER_ESCAPE_MIN = ladder.mjs ladderEscapeMin(LADDER_CLIMB_NEED)` = 26 (asked of ladderPlan, a finite reach,
  bounded search, null keeps every ladder); switch `KIT_SHRINK=on|off` (default on); KIT_NO_TRIP; the bank row.
- `ladder.mjs`: `LADDER_CLIMB_NEED = 25`, `ladderEscapeMin`. `reflex.mjs`: ladderReady and climb_ladder read
  LADDER_CLIMB_NEED (was `PILLAR_MAX_BLOCKS + 1`, the same 25); PILLAR_MAX_BLOCKS exported; a test asserts the equality.
- `bankable.mjs bankableInventory`: a kit name skips `not_wanted` (after tools, KEEP_ONE, reserves and withdraw holds); a
  bag at 26+ ladders excludes them as `escape_ladders` (not junk); returns `kit` (its share of `count`).
  `depositDue({ kitBankable })`: without storage in reach the kit's share does not count -- the kit alone never walks a bot
  to a chest (Codex design r2 P2). `depositNoopReason`: `deposit ladder` at 26+ -> "... — nothing to deposit, keep working".
- Callers pass `kitBankable` (admission.mjs, prompt.mjs depositSituation, skills.mjs depositWorthIt).
- `skills.mjs` roomRemedy / slotRemedy FAR branches pass KIT_NO_TRIP as `keep`: a far bot is never sent home for kit
  (Claude design r1 P2-3). townDeposit writes `_kitshrink phase=bank` (start counts + server-verified banked; bounded).
- `index.mjs`: `_kitshrink phase=mode` once per spawn (the licence).

Expected: ~136-140 of 2,675 used slots (5%), ~1.7 per kit-holding bot, banked at the next town deposit. The slots MOVE into
town containers (empty slots only; +2 torch / +7 ladder pickups a day fleet-wide); the read guards the town side.

What it does not do: stations, bucket, chest stay (3-4 slots/bot); freeing them needs the tech ladder to credit a placed or
town-held station, or a full-town recovery that takes a chest item back out of the container it stands at -- separate
designs, offered to the owner. No junk-list change. No withdraw change (another agent owns its container search;
withdraw's room-making simply sees the kit as bankable through depositPlan and still banks it LAST by its own rank).

CONSEQUENCE, STATED: under this rule climb_ladder can be reached only by a bot that picks up 26+ ladders between town visits
-- in effect dead by construction (moot today: 0 of 80 bots, 0 of 10,972 escape tries). A future ladder-escape fix must
lift the rule for ladders (one line in kitshrink.mjs).

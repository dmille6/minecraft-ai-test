## What B5 is

- **Bot side** (`bots/src/roles.mjs`, wired in `cognitive.mjs`, tier 3, default OFF): with `ROLES=on`, a valid lease for this bot at
  town asks its job (BUILD = `build_shop`) first among the discretionary town jobs (ahead of the smelt job, the drain and the
  materials job); the job's own admission decides; a refusal is released with one row. `ROLES` unset = today's chain.
- **Host side** (`scripts/overseer/assign.mjs`): host eligibility = `shop.mjs buildOrder` itself (parity test), the validator,
  greedy, seeded random, the oracle, the 5-min revalidation, the token bucket.
- **Spend guard + caller** (`scripts/overseer/spendguard.py`, `caller.py`, Mac only): vendor-counted input, worst case reserved
  and fsynced before the send and bound to the request hash, one send (`max_retries=0`), settle from usage, open reservations
  at worst case, day allowance + 2-day carry, 80% stop for shadow/paper, earmarks, vendor reconciliation. Ledger
  `~/overseer/spend.jsonl`, price table `~/overseer/prices.json`.
- **Paper** (`sandbox/coop/arms-e2e.cjs`, `arms-batch.cjs`, `arms-read.py`): 5 real bots, the town's stock for every project, raw
  copper and coal so the smelt job competes for each visit (fleet 6 h, 80 bots: build_shop 7.6 bot-h vs smelt 2.6 + drain 1.9 +
  materials 1.5); arrivals from the measured process (9.6% at town, stays median 1 min / p90 3 min), the same schedule for
  every arm of a pair; 60-min windows; arms rotation, random, greedy, oracle, frontier (claude-sonnet-5-5, gpt-6.1-sol,
  claude-haiku-5-5), starved.

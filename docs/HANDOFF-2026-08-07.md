Written before the move to the new network, where the current fleet is
unreachable. This is the state of the work, not a narrative of how it got here.

are **role names** (`scout scout2 miner gatherer gather2`), never display names —

## The pathfinding diagnosis

Movement is the binding constraint: `goto` 3.0% success over 1042 attempts,
`home` 1.7%, and 80 of 81 deaths were falls at y20–39. Three defects, all fixed
today, all the same shape — a value reported rather than measured.

1. `goto` decided WHY a walk failed by regexing its own error prose. The pattern
   `/no path|took to long|timeout|exceeded/i` matched our own wrapper message
   "pathfinding exceeded 25000ms", so 393 expired travel budgets were reported
   and persisted as "no route exists". The pathfinder returned NoPath **zero**
   times in that window. It now reads `e.name` — the pathfinder's typed error
   contract — and skills that know why they failed say so explicitly, with
   `classifyFailure` demoted to a fallback.

2. The reflex stuck detector calls `bot.pathfinder.stop()`. Verified against
   mineflayer-pathfinder 2.4.5: `path_stop` is emitted ONLY by `stop()`, which
   only our code calls. So `goto()` rejected with `PathStopped` rather than our
   AbortError, `e.aborted` was undefined, and 596 interruptions **we caused**
   were charged to the skill. `path_interrupted`/`path_budget`/`goal_changed`
   no longer write lessons at all.

3. Fixing the classifier only stopped new bad rules. All five bots had `home:{}`
   in their avoid table — four past the block threshold, Gather02 at 24 failures
   — built entirely on those corrupted labels. 30 movement rules purged
   (`scripts/purge-corrupt-movement-lessons.py`, idempotent, dry-run default).

## The finding that has not been acted on

New `path_update`/`path_reset` instrumentation, first 20 minutes:

    34x  _path_noPath   "noPath after 1 nodes, 0ms"
    24x  _path_reset    "stuck"

A* visited **one node** and returned noPath in **zero milliseconds** — the search
refusing to start, because the bot's current position has no legal first move.
At y=68 and y=71, i.e. on the SURFACE, not at mining depth. Scout01 alone was 37
of 77 events. With `canDig=false` and `allowParkour=false`, a bot on top of
foliage or in a one-block hole has nowhere legal to step and no way to dig out.

`goto()` in mineflayer-pathfinder *resolves successfully* when
`results.path.length === 0`, which is why this was invisible.

## What NOT to do next, and why

The obvious fix for the falls is lowering `maxDropDown` from 6. Resist it. Fewer
legal moves is exactly what "noPath after 1 node" already is, so tightening
movement to stop falls may trade 80 deaths for a fleet that cannot take a step.

Also: deaths report "fell **47** blocks", not a planned 6-block drop. That is a
planned drop where the floor was not where the planner thought — which with
`canDig=false` and active mining nearby points at block-update path invalidation,
not a drop limit. `path_reset` now records `block_updated` as one of its nine
reasons, so a few hours of running answers this instead of guessing.

Two numbers to read first:
  - does `home` succeed now that it is not gated? (tests the purge)
  - does `block_updated` climb in the `path_reset` distribution? (tests the above)

## Open, in priority order

1. Underground movement profile / fall reflex — there is currently NO fall
   reflex at all. The layer handles danger blocks, low health, entombment,
   suffocation and stuck. Nothing for falling.
2. Pathfinder probe bench — no LLM, no gate; bearings × distances, recording the
   PLAN result (`getPathFromTo`, a generator — consume to a terminal status, not
   just the first yield) separately from execution.
3. Long-term: take long-distance travel away from the LLM. A deterministic nav
   service over a waypoint graph of known-good edges; the pathfinder only for
   short local legs; route memory storing edge reliability rather than "skill
   impossible"; the gate blocking edges, not semantic actions like "go home".
   Rationale: letting mineflayer locomotion dominate every trial means measuring
   brittle pathfinding rather than planning, memory or coordination.
4. Decay cannot help actions being retried; give-up backoff compounds to 180-min
   caps that leave bots idle. Both were diagnosed as downstream of movement.

## Corrections to earlier claims in the record

Stated confidently, later disproved. Listed because the transcript preserves the
errors next to the fixes with equal weight, and this file should not.

- **"Nothing beyond 100m has ever been found."** Wrong. Home is `28,0`; a config
  read without sourcing a bot env file returned the `0,0` default and shifted
  every deposit by up to 28m. There are 13 past 100m. `survey_2_at_100` had been
  retuned to 90m on that basis and is now reverted.
- **"`eat` is broken, 0/118."** Wrong. All 119 are `no_effect` ("already full"),
  which the harness deliberately scores as neither win nor loss. A fail column
  computed as total-minus-success conflated them.
- **"The fleet is running `64eb591`."** It was not. `CODE_VERSION` is written by
  the deploy script, so hand-copying leaves it stale — eleven hours of documents
  were misattributed. Hence the digest.
